## Description

| --- | --- |
| [![Defense.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/6/66/Defense.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Defense.png) | You can protect yourself from any attack of the enemy in close-quarter combat by using this Defense skill. Remember that, while Defense is a way to protect your body, it also lets you retaliate. Successfully defending yourself disrupts the enemy's flow of attacks, opening themselves up to your attack. If you determine that you are on the defensive during a battle due to the one-sided attack of the enemy, you can instantly turn the tide of the battle by using this skill. Don't be discouraged even if your enemy does not lose his flow of attack, but persistently lead him on to spend his energy. After tiring out the enemy, administer a quiet and calm strike. Defense is the most effective preparation for your attacks. |

## Details

- When active, it boosts the [Defense and Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense_(Stat) "Defense (Stat)") of the user, and allows the user to block one enemy attack.

  - [Normal Attacks](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Normal_Attack "Normal Attack"), [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill "Windmill"), [Charge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Charge "Charge"), [Lance Charge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance_Charge "Lance Charge"), [Icebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Icebolt "Icebolt"), [Lightning Bolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lightning_Bolt "Lightning Bolt"), [Human Ranged Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human_Ranged_Attack "Human Ranged Attack"), [Elf Ranged Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf_Ranged_Attack "Elf Ranged Attack"), [Support Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Support_Shot "Support Shot"), [Arrow Revolver](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Arrow_Revolver "Arrow Revolver"), [Mirage Missile](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mirage_Missile "Mirage Missile"), [Crash Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crash_Shot "Crash Shot"), [Throwing Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Throwing_Attack "Throwing Attack"), [Water Cannon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Water_Cannon "Water Cannon") when used at long range, [Charging Strike](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Charging_Strike "Charging Strike"), [Focused Fist](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Focused_Fist "Focused Fist"), [Spinning Uppercut](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spinning_Uppercut "Spinning Uppercut"), [Somersault Kick](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Somersault_Kick "Somersault Kick"), [Act 4: Rising Action](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Act_4:_Rising_Action "Act 4: Rising Action"), [Grapple Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Grapple_Shot "Grapple Shot"), [Bullet Slide](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bullet_Slide "Bullet Slide"), [Bullet Storm](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bullet_Storm "Bullet Storm"), and [Shooting Rush](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shooting_Rush "Shooting Rush") trigger Defense.

    - Elf Ranged Attack and Normal Attacks from [Dual Wielding](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Wielding "Dual Wielding") or [Dual Guns](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Guns_List "Dual Guns List") only causes one hit to strike rather than both.
    - When Defending against a melee attack, the attacker is stunned for a short while.
    - If a person is Dual Wielding and hits a defending opponent with [Splash Damage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Splash_Damage "Splash Damage"), the defending opponent will receive both hits but the damage from the second hit is unaltered. However, the defending opponent is not stunned.
  - Defense can only block one hit, so be wary of Multi [Aggro](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Aggro "Aggro").
  - Having a [shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shields_List "Shields List") equipped greatly increases the effects of the Defense skill.

    - [Small Shields](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Small_Shields "Small Shields") reduce the total damage taken by 50%
    - [Medium Shields](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Medium_Shields "Medium Shields") reduce the total damage taken by 70%
    - [Large Shields](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Large_Shields "Large Shields") reduce the total damage taken by 100%
- This skill does not increase [Magic Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Defense "Magic Defense") nor [Magic Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Protection "Magic Protection") and therefore [Magic](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Magic_Skills "Category:Magic Skills") and [Alchemy](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Alchemy_Skills "Category:Alchemy Skills") attacks' damage is not reduced.
- While defending, the user will be forced to walk.
  - However, if the user is equipped with a [Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shields_List "Shields List"), they will be allowed to run.

    - This does not extend to [Partners](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Partners "Partners"), and NPCs.
- Being hit with Defense loaded will negate stun, allowing retaliation.
  - Users must wait for the Defense animation to end before they can retaliate.
    - The actual defending animation varies between [Weapons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Weapons "Category:Weapons"), [Races](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Player_Races "Category:Player Races"), and whether or not a shield is equipped in the active slot.
  - Using Melee attacks against a target with Defense loaded will result in the attacker's attack sequence being interrupted.
    - It is possible to bypass this using a Magic Bolt followed quickly with another attack.
- [Poison Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Poison_Attack "Poison Attack") and [Mirage Missile](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mirage_Missile "Mirage Missile")'s poison effect does not occur if the target successfully Defends from it.
- Using Defense with a shield passively increases [Melee](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Melee_Skills "Category:Melee Skills") and [Ranged](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Archery_Skills "Category:Archery Skills") Defense.

However, damage taken is still reduced.

  - Thunder's and Ice Spear's stun and freezing effect, respectively, are negated by a shielded target using Defense.
  - A target equipped with a shield or guard cylinder with Defense loaded will not be knocked down by Magnum Shot or Gold Strike.
- Starting at Rank 5, surrounding players gain a defense bonus while Defense is active.
  - Players do not need to be in the same [party](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Party "Party").
  - This does not affect the user.
  - However, the buff range is extremely small; they must be almost on top of the Defending player to obtain the bonus.
- Defense has a cooldown of 7 seconds, which occurs after successfully defending an attack.
- Cannot be used with a [Lance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance "Lance") equipped.
- Can be used during [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill "Windmill")'s animation.

## Obtaining the Skill

#### [Tutorial](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Tutorial_Quests "Beginner Tutorial Quests")

Rewarded for completing the Tutorial Quest [Item Equipping and Usage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Tutorial_Quests#Item_Equipping_and_Usage "Beginner Tutorial Quests").

#### Other Methods

Or, for humans,

- Talk to [Ranald](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ranald "Ranald") located at [Tir Chonaill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Chonaill "Tir Chonaill")'s School with the [Skills Keyword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skills_Keyword "Skills Keyword"). You will receive the skill along with the _[Defense Guidebook](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense_Guidebook "Defense Guidebook")_.

Or, for humans,

- Take Ranald's class and complete the _[Basic Combat 2 Mission](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Classes_and_Training "Classes and Training")_.

## Training Method

### Tips

- Defense is easily trained by getting a low-leveled monster (such as a [Gray Town Rat](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gray_Town_Rat "Gray Town Rat") or a [Brown Fox](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Brown_Fox "Brown Fox")) that hits you for extremely minor damage and using Defense after every attack.

  - However, such monsters do not attack aggressively and must be provoked. It is highly recommended to use [Rock Throwing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rock_Throwing "Rock Throwing"), [Taunt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Taunt "Taunt"), or using and cancelling [Enthralling Performance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enthralling_Performance "Enthralling Performance") as they aggravate the enemy into attacking while not harming them.
- The requirement "Successful in using a special skill to attack a defending enemy" means to use [Smash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Smash "Smash"), [Magnum Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magnum_Shot "Magnum Shot"), [Firebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firebolt "Firebolt"), [Water Cannon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Water_Cannon "Water Cannon") or [Assault Slash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Assault_Slash "Assault Slash") on the enemy while it has Defense completely loaded.
- To fulfill "Observe the enemy using the Defense skill", you must attack the enemy ( **not** with skills that ignore the Defense Skill) while they have Defense completely loaded.
