This skill is based on discipline of the body rather than knowledge. With high Combat Mastery skill, you deliver more powerful attacks than someone who is not using this skill. ## Details

- Passively increases Minimum and Maximum [Damage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Damage 'Damage') and [Balance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Balance 'Balance') of melee attacks, as well as [Health](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Life 'Stats'), [Strength](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Strength 'Strength'), and [Dexterity](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dexterity 'Dexterity').

    - Increases HP by 10 and Balance by 1% per Rank. Other stats are dependent on [Race](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Player_Races 'Category:Player Races').

- Max/Min Damage from Combat Mastery affects all melee weapons (ranged weapons such as [Bows](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bows_List 'Bows List'), [Crossbows](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crossbows_List 'Crossbows List'), [Atlatls](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Atlatls_List 'Atlatls List'), [Shuriken](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shuriken_List 'Shuriken List'), and [Dual Guns](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Guns_List 'Dual Guns List') are not affected).

    - Balance from Combat Mastery affects all weapons, including ranged ones.

- If a [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human 'Human') or [Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant 'Giant') player is [Dual Wielding](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Wielding 'Dual Wielding'), the bonuses from Combat Mastery will apply **once** despite having two weapons.
- Rank C Combat Mastery is required to obtain the quest for [Blaze](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blaze 'Blaze') and [Evasion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Evasion 'Evasion'); Rank 5 is required to obtain the quest for [Rage Impact](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rage_Impact 'Rage Impact') and [Bash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bash 'Bash').
