## Details

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/a/ae/CarpentryWoodworking.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:CarpentryWoodworking.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:CarpentryWoodworking.png "Enlarge")

The woodworking part of carpentry

- Carpentry is a form of production that utilizes [Firewood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firewood "Firewood") to produce wooden weapons.

  - Please see the [Carpentry List](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Carpentry_List "Carpentry List") for a list of possible items that can be produced by Carpentry and for success rates.
- Carpentry requires a [Lumber Axe](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lumber_Axe "Lumber Axe") for [chopping wood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Chopping_Block "Chopping Block") or a [Woodworking Plane](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Woodworking_Plane "Woodworking Plane") for [shaping and fashioning wood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Carpentry_Workbench "Carpentry Workbench"). Both tools can be purchased from [Tracy](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tracy "Tracy").
- [Dugald Aisle](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dugald_Aisle "Dugald Aisle") is the only location where both forms of Carpentry can be performed. [Chopping Blocks](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Chopping_Block_(Homestead) "Chopping Block (Homestead)") and Carpentry Workbenches can be placed in a [Homestead](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Homestead "Homestead"). Carpentry Workbenches can also be found in [Emain Macha](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Emain_Macha "Emain Macha").
- Like [Potion Making](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Potion_Making "Potion Making"), [Blacksmithing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blacksmithing "Blacksmithing") and [Tailoring](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tailoring "Tailoring"), [Dexterity](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dexterity "Dexterity") has no influence on Carpentry.
- The rate of successfully chopping wood is `40% + Carpentry Rank Bonus + Production Mastery Bonus.`
  - Upon success, there is a:
    - 82% chance of receiving normal [Firewood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firewood "Firewood").
    - 15% chance of receiving [Average Firewood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Average_Firewood "Average Firewood").
    - 3% chance of receiving [Fine Firewood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fine_Firewood "Fine Firewood").
- Higher grades of firewood produced by Carpentry amplify the effects of the following:
  - [Campfire](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Campfire "Campfire") regeneration rate.
  - [Enchant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enchant "Enchant") burning success rate.
  - _**Reduced**_ [refining](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Refining "Refining") success rate at [Vales](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vales "Vales")'s [Old Furnaces](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Old_Furnace_(Public) "Old Furnace (Public)").
- When crafting a bow, the game will tell you the stat bonuses and a message regarding the quality.
  - i.e. "Your creation is far superior to anything you can get from a store.", "Your efforts created an above-average `item`!", "Your hard work produced a very nice `item`!"

### [Carpentry Quality](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Item_Quality\#Blacksmith.2C_Carpentry.2C_and_Tailoring "Item Quality")

- Weapons and Equipment crafted with the Blacksmith, Carpentry, or Tailoring Skills will receive an Item Quality that correlates to additional stats the items can receive.
  - For Blacksmith and Tailoring, the game determines the base Quality depending on how well you did in the Skill minigame.
  - For Carpentry, the game determines the base Quality randomly based on the Carpentry Skill Rank.
  - The Quality then has various possible bonuses added to it.
    - Crafting on [Monday](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Monday "Monday") will provide an additional +3 Production Quality.
    - Using certain Titles (i.e. the [the Master of Carpentry](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/index.php?title=The_Master_of_Carpentry&action=edit&redlink=1 "The Master of Carpentry (page does not exist)") 1st Title and the [who is Known Far and Wide as 2nd Title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Who_is_Known_Far_and_Wide_as_2nd_Title "Who is Known Far and Wide as 2nd Title"); the [Edern 2nd Title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Edern_2nd_Title "Edern 2nd Title"), [Crazy Crafter 2nd Title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crazy_Crafter_2nd_Title "Crazy Crafter 2nd Title"), or [Special Luminous Cosmic Milky Way 2nd Title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Special_Luminous_Cosmic_Milky_Way_2nd_Title "Special Luminous Cosmic Milky Way 2nd Title")) can increase Quality.
    - Using items Enchanted with [Product Quality Modifiers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Product_Quality_Modifiers "Product Quality Modifiers") (i.e. [Steady](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Steady "Steady") or [Daunting](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Daunting "Daunting")) can increase Quality.
    - Using [Reforges](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Reforges "Reforges") (i.e. those for [Tailoring Kits](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tailoring_Kit "Tailoring Kit"), Accessories, or [Woodworking Planes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Woodworking_Plane "Woodworking Plane")) can increase Quality.
    - Using a respective [Production Quality Potion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Production_Quality_Potions "Category:Production Quality Potions") can increase Quality.
- [Player](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Player "Player")-crafted items will receive a Crafter's Signature _**if Quality is 80 or higher**_.

  - Blacksmith and Tailoring require the Skill Rank to be Rank 9 or higher.
  - The Crafter's Signature will show the name of the Character as well as their Skill Rank.
  - Set Effect rolls will not count towards a Crafter's Signature; some items will receive a bonus Set Effect point if Quality is high.
- Some [Erg Sacrifice Items](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Erg_Sacrifice_Items "Erg Sacrifice Items") require Signed items crafted with Blacksmith or Tailoring.

### Other Bonuses

- Skill Rank bonuses for Carpentry:

  - Rank 5: +1 Balance (Bows and Crossbows)
  - Rank 4: +2 Balance (Bows and Crossbows)
  - Rank 3: +2 Balance, +1 Durability (Bows and Crossbows)
  - Rank 2: +2 Balance, +1 Durability (Bows and Crossbows)
  - Rank 1: +2 Balance, +2 Durability (Bows and Crossbows)
- Active Carpentry Talent provides an additional **1 Minimum Damage** and **2 Maximum Damage** to crafted Bows and Crossbows.
- Active Carpentry Grandmaster unique effect provides an additional **1 Minimum Damage** and **1 Maximum Damage** to crafted Bows and Crossbows.
- Active Blacksmith Talent provides an additional **1 Minimum Damage** and **1 Maximum Damage** to crafted Instruments.

## Training Method

- Gathering wood from a tree with a [Gathering Axe](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gathering_Axe "Gathering Axe") will count towards the "Chop wood to gather Firewood" training method.
- Starting from Rank 8, training requirements include making bows that require [Leather Straps](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Leather_Strap "Leather Strap"), [Thin Thread Ball](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Thin_Thread_Ball "Thin Thread Ball") or [Thick Thread Ball](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Thick_Thread_Ball "Thick Thread Ball"), created by [Weaving](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Weaving "Weaving"). From Rank 6 and beyond, creation of these bows is needed to advance into further ranks.
- The "Make a high-quality Bow" training method starting from Rank 4 is completed when crafting a bow and receiving a message that describes your creation as "godly". The resulting bow will receive a Crafter's Signature.
