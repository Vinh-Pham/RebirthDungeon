## Description

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| [![Counterattack.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/c/c1/Counterattack.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Counterattack.png) | Counterattack allows you to turn your opponent's strength against them. This skill will greatly increase your abilities in close-range combat, as you'll be able to reflect almost any attack. The stronger your enemy's attack, the stronger your counterattack becomes. When you're equipped with knuckles, which are usually used by Fighters, you can attack with more agility. |

## Details

- Allows one to evade and/or block a physical attack and immediately strike back with a physical attack in return.
- Counterattack damage is a combination of a percentage of the damage done by the enemy and the user. Damage is calculated as: _`Skill Enemy Damage % + Skill Player Damage %`_
  - Damage returned is based on the enemy's normal damage. Skills that multiply damage will not raise the damage from Counterattack.
    - For example, if Counterattack reflects someone who used a Rank 1 Human/Elf Smash (500% damage multiplier) while having only 100 damage, then only 100 damage will be returned rather than 500 damage.
- After being countered, almost all opponents will be knocked down.
  - Enemies with [Advanced Heavy Stander](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Advanced_Heavy_Stander "Advanced Heavy Stander") are not knocked down; while Counterattack will return their damage, they may not be stunned and will continue to attack.
- Repels melee [Normal Attacks](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Normal_Attack "Normal Attack"), [Smash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Smash "Smash"), [Charge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Charge "Charge"), [Lance Charge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance_Charge "Lance Charge"), [Charging Strike](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Charging_Strike "Charging Strike"), [Focused Fist](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Focused_Fist "Focused Fist"), [Spinning Uppercut](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spinning_Uppercut "Spinning Uppercut"), [Somersault Kick](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Somersault_Kick "Somersault Kick"), [Dischord](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dischord "Dischord"), [Act 1: Inciting Incident](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Act_1:_Inciting_Incident "Act 1: Inciting Incident"), [Act 2: Threshold Cutter](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Act_2:_Threshold_Cutter "Act 2: Threshold Cutter"), and [Flash Launcher](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Flash_Launcher "Flash Launcher").

  - Counterattack will also apply to [Splash Damage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Splash_Damage "Splash Damage").
- Counterattack requires a certain amount of [Stamina](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stamina "Stamina") to activate and then slowly depletes Stamina over time.

  - Counterattack will stay active even after Stamina reaches 0 until it is canceled.
- [Critical](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical "Critical")chance for this skill depends solely on the target's Critical rate after factoring modifiers such as protection, plus the player's skill's Critical bonus. Player's base Critical rate (character window) is ignored.

  - When a Counterattack makes a Critical Hit, the critical damage is applied to the enemy's damage only and uses the enemy's Critical Hit skill, rather than the player's.
- Counterattack does not inflict [Wounds](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wound "Wound").
- Does not work against [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill "Windmill"), [Stomp](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stomp "Stomp"), [Throwing Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Throwing_Attack "Throwing Attack"), [Gold Strike](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gold_Strike "Gold Strike"), [Assault Slash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Assault_Slash "Assault Slash"), [Drop Kick](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Drop_Kick "Drop Kick"), [Pummel](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pummel "Pummel"), [Wire Pull](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wire_Pull "Wire Pull"), [Puppet's Snare](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Puppet%27s_Snare "Puppet's Snare"), [Act 4: Rising Action](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Act_4:_Rising_Action "Act 4: Rising Action"), [Act 6: Crisis](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Act_6:_Crisis "Act 6: Crisis"), [Act 7: Climactic Crash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Act_7:_Climactic_Crash "Act 7: Climactic Crash"), the eruption from [Act 9: Invigorating Encore](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Act_9:_Invigorating_Encore "Act 9: Invigorating Encore"), [Dual Gun Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Gun_Mastery "Dual Gun Mastery"), [Grapple Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Grapple_Shot "Grapple Shot"), [Bullet Slide](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bullet_Slide "Bullet Slide"), [Shooting Rush](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shooting_Rush "Shooting Rush"), [Bullet Storm](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bullet_Storm "Bullet Storm"), [Doppelganger](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Doppelganger "Doppelganger"), and [Classic Spirit Weapon Awakening](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Classic_Spirit_Weapon_Awakening "Classic Spirit Weapon Awakening"), and all [Archery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Archery_Skills "Category:Archery Skills"), [Magic](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Magic_Skills "Category:Magic Skills"), [Alchemy](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Alchemy_Skills "Category:Alchemy Skills"), [Monster](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Monster_Skills "Category:Monster Skills"), and [Pet](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Pet_Skills "Category:Pet Skills") Skills.
- The user cannot move while and after loading Counterattack.
  - However, if the user is equipped with a [Sword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Swords_List "Swords List") (excluding [Rapiers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Rapiers "Category:Rapiers")), [Blunt Weapon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blunt_Weapons_List "Blunt Weapons List"), or [Axe](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Axes_List "Axes List"), they will be allowed to walk.

    - This is true regardless of whether the equipped weapon has durability or not.
    - This does not extend to [Partners](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Partners "Partners"), and NPCs.
  - It is possible to move a very small distance upon loading the skill as the user will finish their current running animation before stopping.
    - Monsters with long running animations, such as [Zombies](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Zombie "Zombie"), will appear to be moving for a long period of time before stopping upon loading Counterattack, giving the illusion that the monster is using a different skill.
- Most skills can be loaded during Counterattack's animation. The exceptions are [Mana Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Shield "Mana Shield"), [Flash Launcher](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Flash_Launcher "Flash Launcher"), [Bullet Storm](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bullet_Storm "Bullet Storm"), [Shooting Rush](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shooting_Rush "Shooting Rush"), all Archery Skills, and Counterattack itself.
- Counterattack has a cooldown of 7 seconds, which occurs after successfully countering an attack.
- Counterattack has a shorter cooldown when equipped with [Knuckles](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Knuckles_List "Knuckles List") (4 seconds). However, Counterattack with Knuckles costs more Stamina than Normal Counterattack.
- Players armed with Dual Guns use the damage of the Gun and [Bullets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Mana_Bullets "Category:Mana Bullets") if there is ammo remaining in the clip. If there are no bullets remaining in the clip, [bare-handed](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bare_Hands "Bare Hands") damage is dealt instead.

  - Counterattack with Dual Guns also consumes two bullets in the chamber if there is ammo remaining in the clip.
  - Counterattack with Dual Guns does not automatically prompt the character to use Reload if there is no ammo remaining in the clip.
  - Contrary to its appearance, loaded Dual Gun Counterattack is a melee hit and triggers Melee [Auto Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Auto_Defense "Auto Defense").
- Players armed with [Lances](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance "Lance") are unable to use Counterattack. Instead, they may use [Lance Counter](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance_Counter "Lance Counter").
- Prerequisite for learning [Rage Impact](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rage_Impact "Rage Impact") (at rank 9).

## [Skill Issues](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Current_Bugs "Current Bugs")

- Counterattack does not cancel when receiving a knockback or knockdown.
- Counterattack with Knuckles uses an abnormally large amount of Stamina to load.
  - You will only need the the normal amount of stamina to load counterattack with knuckles. If you currently have 2 stamina and rank F Counterattack, you will still be able to load Counterattack with Knuckles without needing the abnormal 20 Stamina.
- When mounted by a [Lime](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lime_Sprite "Lime Sprite"), [Yellow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Yellow_Sprite "Yellow Sprite"), or [Rainbow Sprite](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rainbow_Sprite "Rainbow Sprite"), or the [Eagle](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Eagle_Owl "Eagle Owl") or [Snowy Owl](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Snowy_Owl "Snowy Owl"), it is possible for enemies that [Dual Wield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Wield "Dual Wield") to land a successful hit during Counter.

## Obtaining the Skill

#### [Tutorial](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Tutorial_Quests "Beginner Tutorial Quests")

Rewarded for completing the Tutorial Quest [Battle in Erinn](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Tutorial_Quests#Battle_in_Erinn "Beginner Tutorial Quests").

#### Other Methods

Or, alternatively:

- Attack an enemy that is using Counterattack.

Or, alternatively:

- Talk to [Dilys](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dilys "Dilys") located inside [Tir Chonaill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Chonaill "Tir Chonaill")'s with the [Skills Keyword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skills_Keyword "Skills Keyword") to obtain the [Counterattack Keyword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Counterattack_Keyword "Counterattack Keyword"). Talk to [Trefor](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Trefor "Trefor") located north of Tir Chonaill with the newly acquired keyword to gain training for the skill.

## Training Method

- "Counter an enemy's special attack." means to counter [Smash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Smash "Smash"); countering other skills does not contribute to this.
- "Learn from the enemy's counter attack." means to get hit by an enemy's Counterattack.
