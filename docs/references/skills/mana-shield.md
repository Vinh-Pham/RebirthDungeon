## Description

| [![Mana Shield.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/f/fe/Mana_Shield.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Mana_Shield.png) | Creates a powerful protective field using the power of mana in order to defend against enemy attacks. It is effective against both physical attacks and magic attacks. The more understanding you have in using mana, the less mana you will use to defend. |

## Details

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/7/77/Mana_Shield_Activating.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Mana_Shield_Activating.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Mana_Shield_Activating.png "Enlarge")

Mana Shield activating.

- Mana Shield is a toggleable skill that invokes the [Mana Shield Status](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Status_Effects "Stats"), causing any damage taken to deduct from the player's [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana "Mana") pool instead of [Health](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Health "Health") and also renders the player immune to [Injury](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Injury "Injury").

  - All damage received is displayed in blue instead of white. A blue effect will also occur when the player is struck.
    - The blue effect becomes invisible if [Mini Effects](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Options_Dialog#Performance "Options Dialog") is enabled.
  - A blue ring below the player's feet will indicate Mana Shield's activation.
  - Mana Shield consumes Mana on activation based on rank, then slowly drains Mana over time at a rate of 0.5 Mana per second.
  - The damage taken with Mana Shield enabled is calculated as: `Damage Taken / Mana Shield Efficiency`
    - Mana Shield Efficiency is calculated as the higher of either:
      - `[(Magic Attack * .01) + Magic Weapon Bonus] + Healing Wand Bonus + Set Effect Bonus`
    - or
      - `[Mana Shield Rank Efficiency + Reforge Efficiency] + Healing Wand Bonus + Set Effect Bonus`
        - Magic Weapon Bonus from either a [Wand](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wands_List "Wands List") or [Staff](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Staff_List "Staff List") adds 1.0 to efficiency.
        - Healing Wands add an additional 0.5 to efficiency beyond other Magic Weapons. Healing Staffs _do not_.
      - Mana Shield Enhancement [set effect](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Status_Effects "Status Effects") will add an additional 0.3 to efficiency when active.
      - Magic Attack _does not_ stack with Rank Efficiency or Mana Shield reforging.
      - Mana Shield _does not_ use any of the forms of Mana Reduction available to reduce damage.
    - Mana Shield _does not_ take the player's [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Defense.2FProtection "Stats"), [Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Protection "Protection"), [Magic Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Defense "Magic Defense"), and [Magic Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Protection "Magic Protection") into calculation for damage mitigation, though Critical Chance is calculated normally.

      - [Hidden Shield Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shields_List "Shields List") is still taken into calculation however.
    - If the damage received exceeds the player's Mana (after calculating efficiency), Mana Shield becomes automatically deactivated and the portion of the damage that was not blocked will be received as HP damage.
  - [Poison Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Poison_Attack "Poison Attack"), [Mirage Missile](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mirage_Missile "Mirage Missile")'s shock damage, [Life Drain](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Life_Drain "Life Drain")'s draining portion, [Dischord](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dischord "Dischord")'s sustained damage, [Puppet's Snare](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Puppet%27s_Snare "Puppet's Snare")'s damage over time ticks, [Meteor Strike](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Meteor_Strike "Meteor Strike")'s leftover flames(?), [Devil's Cry](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Devil%27s_Cry "Devil's Cry")'s damage effect, and [Black Dragon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Black_Dragon "Black Dragon")'s [Stomp](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stomp_(Monster) "Stomp (Monster)") bypasses Mana Shield, ignoring the player's MP while directly affecting HP.
- Mana Shield causes an [MP Elixir](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/MP_Elixir "MP Elixir")'s effect to expire 5 times faster than normal. Disabling Mana Shield will remove the penalty, but will not restore lost time.

  - However a [Recovery Booster](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Recovery_Booster "Recovery Booster")'s time will not be affected.
- Mana Shield has no effect on the [Dark Knight](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dark_Knight "Dark Knight") Disarm phase; HP is still drained rather than MP.
- Using Mana Shield while in [Deadly](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Deadly_(Status) "Deadly (Status)") status will not prevent [incapacitation](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Knocked_Unconscious "Knocked Unconscious") and will still drain MP.

  - Being drained to 0 HP by Life Drain and then receiving Life Drain's knockback damage with Mana Shield active, however, will prevent incapacitation.
- Mana Shield cannot be activated while loading another skill, stunned, pushed back, or knocked down.
- Mana Shield can be deactivated at almost anytime, even during the middle of an attack cycle.
  - However, it cannot be deactivated while talking to an NPC.
- _Currently capped at rank 6._

## Skill Issues

- When using a [Fountain](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dungeon_Fountains "Dungeon Fountains") with Mana Shield enabled and it gives you [wounds](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wound "Wound"), your [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana "Mana") will rapidly decrease and will go below the threshold (i.e. Negative Mana).

## Training Method

- All the training requirements will only be fulfilled if the player is hit with the Mana Shield status active.

### Training Tips

- At higher levels, Mana Shield requires you to be hit by specific types of attacks.
  - [Melee](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Melee "Melee") attacks as well as [Critical Hits](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical_Hit "Critical Hit") are exceedingly easy to build up if you have a [Mount](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Mounts "Category:Mounts"). Go to any low level dungeon like [Alby](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Alby_Dungeon "Alby Dungeon") or [Ciar](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ciar_Dungeon "Ciar Dungeon") and look around for a large [Mimic](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mimic "Mimic") pit. Get on your mount and touch all of the Mimics. They will not awaken immediately. Once you un-mount your pet, the Mimics will simultaneously awaken and aggro you together. If you have reasonably high [MP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/MP "MP") and Mimics do only 1 point in damage to you (depending on your [Magic Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Attack "Magic Attack") and rank of Mana Shield), you can sit back and watch the melee hits pile up. It is also possible, if you are Human or Giant, to activate [Final Hit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Final_Hit "Final Hit") or [Wind Guard](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wind_Guard "Wind Guard"), respectively, after gathering a large group of enemies and let them continually attack you.
  - For [Ranged Attacks](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack"), it's the same, but look for an orb room of [Goblin Archers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Goblin_Archer "Goblin Archer") instead. Otherwise, you may look for [Shadow Archers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shadow_Archer "Shadow Archer") in [Defeat the Shadow Warrior](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defeat_the_Shadow_Warrior "Defeat the Shadow Warrior") (Basic), as they do low damage.
  - For [Magic](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic "Magic") attacks, you may want to go to [Ceo Island](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ceo_Island "Ceo Island") or [Coill Dungeon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Coill_Dungeon "Coill Dungeon") and attract one of the elemental [Sprites](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Sprite "Sprite"). It would be helpful to have [elemental protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elements "Elements") (such as equipment with Magic Resistance or Elementals) in order to maintain low damage. Otherwise, [Rafting](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rafting "Rafting") may be another option and allow the [Hobgoblin Exorcists](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hobgoblin_Exorcist "Hobgoblin Exorcist") to strike you with their bolts.
- [Bandits](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bandit "Bandit") from [Commerce](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Commerce "Commerce") is another option, as the enemies use varieties of attacks (Imps use Magic, Goblin Archers use Ranged, and Humans, Goblins, Giants and Ogres use Melee). It would be wise to locate the Rookie types as they deal the lowest damage of all the Bandits.
- Alternatively, for all of these. Use [Dorren's Request](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dorren%27s_Request "Dorren's Request") as you can simply hit the orbs you need as required.
- If you lack a reasonable amount of [MP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/MP "MP"), lure monsters to [Mana Tunnels](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Tunnels "Mana Tunnels"). The tunnel will constantly regenerate your MP and will hopefully sustain your MP when taking damage.

  - You may want to use [Rock Throwing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rock_Throwing "Rock Throwing") or [Taunt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Taunt "Taunt") to prevent killing them.
  - The use of [Enthralling Performance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enthralling_Performance "Enthralling Performance") can help reset a monster's anchor location closer to the tunnel, so they do not flee when lured.
