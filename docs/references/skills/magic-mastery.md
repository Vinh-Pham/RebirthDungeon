## Description

| --- | --- |
| [![Magic Mastery.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/2/2f/Magic_Mastery.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Magic_Mastery.png) | Magic is a way of resonating mana that exists in this world with nature, manipulating it with the powers of the caster. An immensely delicate process that requires a high level of concentration, it is no exaggeration to say that those who are proficient in magic casting are that much closer to the laws that make up this world. The greater the variety of magic learned and used, the greater the general understanding of magic. Under the divine protection of Magic Mastery, the wizard's power and knowledge to control mana will grow deeper. |

## Details

- Passively increases [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana "Mana"), [Intelligence](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Intelligence "Intelligence"), [Magic Balance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Balance "Magic Balance"), and [Blaze](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blaze "Blaze")'s Damage.
- Prerequisite for learning [Blaze](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blaze "Blaze") (at rank C), and [Spellwalk](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spellwalk "Spellwalk") (at rank 1).

## Obtaining the Skill

#### Tutorial

Rewarded for completing [To The Outside World](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tutorial#To_The_Outside_World "Tutorial")

- The skill will automatically be ranked to F.

#### Other Methods

- Talk to [Lassar](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lassar "Lassar") with the Skills keyword.

## Training Method

- [Fireball](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fireball "Fireball"), [Ice Spear](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ice_Spear "Ice Spear"), [Thunder](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Thunder "Thunder"), [Party Healing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Party_Healing "Party Healing") and [Blaze](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blaze "Blaze") are required to train Ranks 5-1, though only two of the five spells are necessary to achieve the minimum proficiency to rank the skill.

  - Giants, however, only use Fireball and Thunder for their trainings in Ranks 5-1 instead of all five spells. Bolt Magic alone is sufficient enough to gain the proficiency to the next rank.
- "Use Party Healing" does not require the user to restore some amount of HP.
