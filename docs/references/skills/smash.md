## Details

- Attacks the target with one strong blow.
- At Rank 5, this skill can cause [Splash Damage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Splash_Damage 'Splash Damage').

    - The Radius and Angle of the splash is dependent on the weapon used.

- Knocks down the opponent and causes them to fly back a distance of 1000.
- Bypasses the [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense 'Defense') skill.
- [Normal Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Normal_Attack 'Normal Attack'), [Counterattack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Counterattack 'Counterattack'), [Lance Counter](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance_Counter 'Lance Counter'), [Combo: Counter Punch](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combo:_Counter_Punch 'Combo: Counter Punch'), [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill 'Windmill'), [Charge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Charge 'Charge'), and [Lance Charge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance_Charge 'Lance Charge') can intercept Smash.

    - If the splash from Smash hits an enemy using Counterattack, Smash will be cancelled and the user will hit by the Counterattack.

- When used with a [Two-Handed Weapon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Two-Handed_Weapons 'Category:Two-Handed Weapons'), the [Damage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Damage 'Damage') and [Critical](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical 'Critical') rate increases by 20% and 5%, respectively.

    - [Knuckles](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Knuckles_List 'Knuckles List'), [Scythes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Scythes_List 'Scythes List'), and the [Chef's Passion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Chef%27s_Passion "Chef's Passion") do not receive this bonus, however.

- If two parties try to Smash each other, the person with the better connection and/or most up-to-date attack order will be dealing the damage.
- A few monsters automatically strike the player if Smash is used against them as first hit. See [Instinctive Reaction](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Instinctive_Reaction 'Instinctive Reaction') for more information.
- Smash cannot be used with a [Atlatl](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Atlatls_List 'Atlatls List'), [Bow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bows_List 'Bows List'), [Crossbow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crossbows_List 'Crossbows List'), or [Dual Gun](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Guns_List 'Dual Guns List') equipped.
- Actions other than loading skills or using [Tumble](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tumble 'Tumble') or [Assault Slash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Assault_Slash 'Assault Slash') may not be taken until the entire animation completes.
- Attempting to Smash an enemy with Windmill loaded will cause Windmill to immediately execute, effectively countering the smash.
    - This will also occur against players but only if the player has [Reserve Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Options_Dialog#Control 'Options Dialog') enabled.
- While being attacked by Smash, the target is immune to all damage except from [Self-Destruct](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Self-Destruct 'Self-Destruct').
- When using a [Shuriken](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shuriken 'Shuriken'), you are required to be directly in the middle of the target to execute smash.
- Smash has a chance to inflict a debuff. Debuff activation is indicated by an additional animation during the smash.
    - The rate is based on the [player's race](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Player_Races 'Category:Player Races').

        - Monsters, [Partners](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Partners 'Partners'), and NPCs cannot apply debuffs.

    - The effect is based on the equipped weapon:
        - [Swords](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Swords_List 'Swords List') and [Axes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Axes_List 'Axes List') (excluding [Rapiers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Rapiers 'Category:Rapiers')) inflict Bleed, dealing damage over time. The inflicted target will be colored red.

            - Health reduction effect stacks with other damage over time effects such as [Poison Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Poison_Attack 'Poison Attack') or [Mirage Missile](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mirage_Missile 'Mirage Missile'), ignores [defensive stats](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Defense.2FProtection 'Stats'), [Passive Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Passive_Defense 'Passive Defense'), [Invulnerability](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Invulnerability 'Invulnerability'), and [Mana Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Shield 'Mana Shield') damage reduction during damage calculation, and can lower a target's HP past 0 and can kill while also ignoring [Deadly](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Deadly_(Status)> 'Deadly (Status)') status.
            - Bleed cannot score [criticals](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical 'Critical').

        - [Blunt Weapons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blunt_Weapons_List 'Blunt Weapons List') inflict Daze, preventing the opponent from using [Magic](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Magic_Skills 'Category:Magic Skills') and [Alchemy Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Alchemy_Skills 'Category:Alchemy Skills'). The inflicted target will be colored yellow.

            - This can be applied on players, unlike [Somersault Kick](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Somersault_Kick 'Somersault Kick').
    - The debuff may also apply to any enemy hit by the skill's [splash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Splash_Damage 'Splash Damage').
    - The debuff's duration can be refreshed and reapplied.
- Prerequisite for learning [Bash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bash 'Bash') (at rank 9).

## Obtaining the Skill

#### [Tutorial](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Tutorial_Quests 'Beginner Tutorial Quests')

Rewarded for completing the Tutorial Quest [Battle in Erinn](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Tutorial_Quests#Battle_in_Erinn 'Beginner Tutorial Quests').

#### Other Methods

Or, for humans,

- Talk to [Lassar](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lassar 'Lassar') located inside [Tir Chonaill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Chonaill 'Tir Chonaill')'s Magic School with the [Skills Keyword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skills_Keyword 'Skills Keyword'). You will receive the book _[Smash Basics](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Smash_Basics 'Smash Basics')_.

Or, for humans,

- Take all 11 parts of [Ranald](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ranald 'Ranald')'s classes and complete his _[Basic Combat 3 Mission](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Classes_and_Training 'Classes and Training')_.
