## Description

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| [![Ice Spear.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/e/e4/Ice_Spear.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Ice_Spear.png) | The Ice Spear skill uses MP to gather water particles in the air and turn them into a spear shaped block of ice. The ice spear is created above the targeted object to take advantage of its weight and acceleration by gravity. Ice spear has greater piercing power compared to ice bolt, and therefore can incur damage over enemies in the surrounding area. |

## Details

- Requires a [Wand](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wands_List "Wands List") or [Staff](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Staves_List "Staves List") to use.

  - An [Ice-Type Wand](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wands_List#Ice "Wands List") or [Divine Tribolt Wand](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Divine_Tribolt_Wand "Divine Tribolt Wand") will increase the damage of Ice Spear by 5%.
- An intermediate magic spell that casts compressed [Ice](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elements "Elements") using a magic weapon which is then thrown in the form of a spear, freezing impacted opponents before the ice shatters and damages them.
- Ice Spear can be charged up to 5 times, each charge increasing damage and [splash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Splash "Splash") up to a maximum of 6.5 charges worth of damage at 5 charges.

  - At 4 or less charges, Ice Spear will do its base damage times the number of charges.
- This skill can be [Auto Charged](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Auto_Charge "Auto Charge").
- The user cannot move with Ice Spear loaded.
  - The user will lose one charge when knocked back.
- When Ice Spear is cast on a target, all enemies along the line of sight to the intended target will be frozen. After a certain time, Ice Spear will explode, dealing heavy damage and knocking all affected enemies down.
  - The initial impact (freeze) deals no damage.
  - The width of the path and the radius increase with rank.
  - Ice Spear will have no effect if the target becomes invincible when the freezing time has passed (e.g. [Dungeon Recovery Rooms](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dungeon_Room_Types#Recovery_Rooms "Dungeon Room Types")).
  - Frozen enemies will be immobilized and will not be able to load any skills. They can still be attacked, but take 50% less damage.
- Monsters will not [aggro](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Aggro "Aggro") immediately upon freezing; they will only aggro after the ice explodes. However, they can still aggro while frozen.
- Monsters with Level 2 or higher [Mana Deflector](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Deflector "Mana Deflector"), or are using [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense "Defense") with a [Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shields_List "Shields List"), will resist the freezing effect.

  - If the target resists an Ice Spear, they will turn light blue. It will still explode after the "freezing" time has passed.
  - When Ice Spear is cast on an enemy with Mana Deflector multiple times, the explosions will stack.
  - Certain enemies are completely immune to the freezing effect, even if they do not have Mana Deflector or a shield. For a list of monsters that are immune, see [here](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skill_Immune_Monsters "Skill Immune Monsters").
- Monsters that are killed by Ice Spear will cause other monsters nearby to freeze as well, creating a chain reaction. Chained explosions gradually get weaker.
  - This chaining effect can happen up to sixteen times.
  - Novice Rank does not spread after the explosion. Only monsters within the direct path of fire are frozen along with the initial target.
  - Use of this spell under Mana Crystallization will ignore the damage reduction.
- Like [Thunder](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Thunder "Thunder") and [Fireball](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fireball "Fireball"), Ice Spear ignores [Magic Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Protection "Magic Protection") when calculating [Critical](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical "Critical") chance.
- Between Thunder and Fireball, Ice Spear has the quickest charge time but does the least amount of damage. [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana "Mana") use varies depending on skill rank and the player's [Race](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Race "Race").

  - At 5 charges, Ice Spear becomes the most Mana-costly spell for [Giants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant "Giant") and [Humans](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human "Human") at Rank 5+. For [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf "Elf"), Ice Spear will always use less Mana than both Thunder and Fireball.
- Ice Spear's explosion delay is affected by the Wand if applicable.
- It is possible to Chain Attack an Ice Spear, since it takes a short amount of time to cast it. This is done by attacking the enemy in rapid succession with Ice Spear once the explosion occurs.
  - Ice Spear can be used to continuously freeze (and lock) monsters with [Advanced Heavy Stander](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Advanced_Heavy_Stander "Advanced Heavy Stander"), if they lack Mana Deflector, as method of dealing with monsters that have the attribute.
- Can be used during [Assault Slash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Assault_Slash "Assault Slash")'s animation.
- Every 100 [Intelligence](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Intelligence "Intelligence") decreases Mana Usage and Casting Time of Ice Spear by 2%.

  - The Casting Time is calculated as: `(Base Cast Time) / [(1 + Int / 5000) * (1 + (Wand Speed Boost)) * (1 + (Vivace Boost) * (1 + (Magic Weapon Mastery Boost))]`
- Obtaining this skill allows one to create Alchemist [Ice Spear Crystals](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ice_Spear_Crystal "Ice Spear Crystal") via [Mana Crystallization](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Crystallization "Mana Crystallization").
- Rank F Ice Spear is a prerequisite to obtaining the quest for learning [Hail Storm](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hail_Storm "Hail Storm").

## Obtaining the Skill

#### [Memory Book](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book "Memory Book")

Rewarded for clearing [Generation 3: Dark Knight](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Generation_3:_Dark_Knight "Generation 3: Dark Knight") and speaking to [Blaanid](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blaanid "Blaanid") during [Clear Storyline Chapter 1](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book#Fourth_Memoir "Memory Book") in the Fourth [Memoir](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book "Memory Book").

- The skill will have training requirements up to **Rank 9** completed.

#### Other Methods

- Ice Spear can also be learned by purchasing a [Ice Spear Scroll](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ice_Spear_Scroll "Ice Spear Scroll") For 1000 [4th Vouchers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/4th_Voucher "4th Voucher") from [Eavan](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Eavan "Eavan") via the [Dungeon Guide](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dungeon_Guide "Dungeon Guide").
