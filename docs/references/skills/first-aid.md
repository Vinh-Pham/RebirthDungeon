## Description

|                                                                                                                                      |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [![First Aid.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/1/11/First_Aid.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:First_Aid.png) | In battle, you often sustain a wound that cannot be easily treated with natural or magical healing. Medical treatment is needed to recover from such a wound and this First Aid skill can help you. You are also able to heal others as well as yourself. If you want to administer it to someone else, make them sit down first before using this skill. |

## Details

- Restores one's own or an allied unit's [wounds](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wound 'Wound').

    - [HP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/HP 'HP') is not restored.
    - 1 [Bandage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bandage 'Bandage') is used per attempt.

        - Bandages are used starting from the bottom-right corner.
        - Higher quality bandages will take priority over lower quality bandages regardless of placement.

    - At Rank 1, First Aid affects multiple people over an area of effect.

- First Aid functions at 100% efficiency while the target is using [Rest](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rest 'Rest'), otherwise it only functions at 50% efficiency.
- It is possible to mend [NPCs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:NPCs 'Category:NPCs'), however they must be manually clicked. This is vital in some [Shadow Missions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shadow_Mission 'Shadow Mission') and [Theatre Missions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Theatre_Mission 'Theatre Mission') which require players to keep NPCs alive.
- First Aid will fail if the target moves.
- While First Aid is usable at Novice rank, it has **no effect whatsoever** other than the training requirement.
- Can be loaded during [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill 'Windmill')'s animation.

## Obtaining the Skill

#### [Memory Book](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book 'Memory Book')

Complete the quest [Life Basics](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book#Third_Memoir 'Memory Book') in the Third [Memoir](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book 'Memory Book').

#### Other Methods

- Receive treatment from [Dilys](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dilys 'Dilys') in [Tir Chonaill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Chonaill 'Tir Chonaill').

Or,

- Complete the quest [The Basics of Holy Arts](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Talent_Quests#The_Basics_of_Holy_Arts 'Beginner Talent Quests').
