## Details

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/9/99/RankBCampKit.jpg)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:RankBCampKit.jpg)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:RankBCampKit.jpg "Enlarge")

Camp Kit with Rank B Campfire

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/8/84/Campfire_rank_3.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Campfire_rank_3.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Campfire_rank_3.png "Enlarge")

Campfire at Rank 5

- Allows one to create a campfire using 5 [Firewood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firewood "Firewood") or a [Campfire Kit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Campfire_Kit "Campfire Kit").
- When using [Rest](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rest "Rest") near a campfire its effects are increased, and consuming [food](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Food "Food") will prompt the player to share it with other resting players.

  - The regeneration bonuses of a [chair](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Chair "Chair") will override the regeneration bonuses of the campfire; the bonuses do not stack.
- Using any [Archery Skill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Archery_Skills "Category:Archery Skills") with a [Bow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bows_List "Bows List") next to a campfire will light up the arrowhead, increasing its damage by 50%.

  - [Crossbows](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crossbows_List "Crossbows List") and [Atlatls](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Atlatls_List "Atlatls List") are unaffected by this.
  - Scoring a [Critical](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical "Critical") with a lit arrowhead rewards "the Fire Arrow" [title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Title "Title").
  - The [fire](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elements "Elements") element is conferred to the attack, overwriting any other elements that have been enchanted onto the firer's equipment.
- A campfire may act as a source of heat required by some [Cooking](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Cooking "Cooking") methods.
- Some items can be burned at a campfire by dragging and dropping the item onto the campfire, rewarding the player with experience and items. For more information, see [Campfire Spirit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Campfire_Spirit "Campfire Spirit").
- [Enchants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enchant "Enchant") can be extracted from an item by burning it with a [Mana Herb](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Herb "Mana Herb") and a [Holy Water of Lymilark](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Holy_Water_of_Lymilark "Holy Water of Lymilark") in the user's possession. For more information, see [Enchant Burning](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enchant_(System)#Enchant_Burning "Enchant (System)").
- Building a campfire with higher grade firewood ( [Average Firewood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Average_Firewood "Average Firewood"), [Fine Firewood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fine_Firewood "Fine Firewood"), [Finest Firewood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Finest_Firewood "Finest Firewood"), and [Special Firewood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Special_Firewood "Special Firewood")) increases the campfire's effectiveness.
- Campfires can be built in the open field, [Dungeons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dungeons "Dungeons"), [Shadow Missions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shadow_Mission "Shadow Mission") and [Theatre Missions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Theatre_Mission "Theatre Mission").
- Campfires cannot be built inside buildings.
- Campfires act as obstacles; monsters and players cannot walk through them, and area of effect attacks (such as [Fireball](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fireball "Fireball") or [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill "Windmill")) will not affect targets on the other side.
- The duration of the campfire decreases in raining or snowing [Weather](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Weather "Weather").
- At Rank B, the size of the flame increases and the appearance of the [Camp Kit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Camp_Kit "Camp Kit") changes.
- Starting at Rank 5, scented [Candles](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Candles "Candles") may be burnt on a campfire, changing the campfire's appearance. Resting near the campfire for one minute will provide a special effect for 36 minutes based on the candle that was burnt.

## Obtaining the Skill

#### [Memory Book](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book "Memory Book")

- Complete the quest [Life Basics](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book#Third_Memoir "Memory Book") in the Third [Memoir](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book "Memory Book").

#### Other Methods

- Read the book _[Campfire Manual](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Campfire_Manual "Campfire Manual")_
  - The book is sold by [Aeira](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Aeira "Aeira") for 800 gold, but can be acquired from [Deian](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Deian "Deian") by speaking to him with the [Campfire Skill Keyword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Campfire_Skill_Keyword "Campfire Skill Keyword") and completing his timed quest.

| How to Get Quest | Talk to Deian about Campfire Skill |
| Briefing | Eh, so you want to know about the Campfire skill... Okay, then, _hunt 5 Gray Wolves_ wandering in the southern plains and I'll give you my secret book about Campfires. (Understanding it is another story, ha ha...) - Deian - |
| Objectives | - Hunt 5 [Gray Wolves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gray_Wolf "Gray Wolf")<br>- Talk to Shepherd Boy |
| Rewards | - [Book on Campfires](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Campfire_Manual "Campfire Manual") |
| Additional Information | This is a timed quest that expires in 1 day.<br>When Deian says "you did help me with my sheep", he refers to the quest he originally gave, no longer in the game, where the player had a timed mission to defend a flock of sheep from attacking Gray Wolves, and rewarded you based on how many of Deian's sheep survived. That has been replaced by this simple hunting quest. |
