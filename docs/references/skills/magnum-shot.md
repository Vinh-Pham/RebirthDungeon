## Details

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/6/65/Magnum_Shot_Splash.gif)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Magnum_Shot_Splash.gif)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Magnum_Shot_Splash.gif "Enlarge")

Magnum's splash effect. The red effect comes from danning the skill.

- Requires a [Bow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Bows "Category:Bows") or [Crossbow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Crossbows "Category:Crossbows") and the respective [Missile](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Missiles_List "Missiles List") to use.
- A long-range, single-hit powerful attack.
- Magnum Shot will always knock the target down upon a successful hit.
  - Will _not_ knock down opponents using [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense "Defense") with a [Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shields_List "Shields List") equipped, given the shot does not kill or place the target into [Deadly](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Deadly_(Status) "Deadly (Status)") status.
  - Targets hit by Magnum Shot will be stuck down for a long period of time.
- Uses up one missile per shot.
- Aiming speed is slower than that of normal [Ranged Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") and does not increase from ranking Ranged Attack.
- Weapon speed does not affect how quickly a player can fire Magnum Shot. However, it does affect how quickly the player can load Magnum Shot consecutively.
- When used with a Bow, the user is slowed to a walk.
  - This does not apply to crossbows.
- Elves can use Magnum Shot while [Mounted](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Mounts "Category:Mounts"), but the Mount will be slowed to a walking pace while the skill is active.

  - The slowdown can be avoided by setting a waypoint before loading Magnum Shot.
- Starting from Rank 5, Magnum Shot can cause [Splash Damage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Splash_Damage "Splash Damage").

  - The splash radius can be compared to that of a [Mace](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mace "Mace") (at Rank 1, that of a [Claymore](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Claymore "Claymore")) which affects all enemies, including sleeping [Mimics](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mimic "Mimic"), in a radial area.
  - [Damage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Damage "Damage") and [Wounds](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wound "Wound") inflicted by splash damage are calculated individually per enemy.
  - The splash impact area is where the enemy was standing the moment you fired, even if the enemy runs around between the time of firing and the time of impact.
  - Enemies hit by splash will not immediately [aggro](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Aggro "Aggro"), even if their AI allows them to. Instead, they will be alert (!). From there, if the enemy can multi-aggro, they will attack.

    - If the type of enemy does not have an alert state (e.g. [Skeleton](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skeleton "Skeleton")), they will remain unaware of your presence.
    - If a shielded enemy is hit by splash while [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense "Defense") is loaded, it will retaliate, and may or may not change aggro.
- The player will temporarily be invincible right after firing Magnum Shot. However, this is an extremely brief moment.
- As a Ranged ability, the effects are shunted by [Natural Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Natural_Shield "Natural Shield") and the missile can be dodged by [Evasion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Evasion "Evasion").
- [Giants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giants "Giants") cannot learn this skill.
- See [Shot of God (SoG)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Solo_Battle_Tactics#Shot_of_God "Solo Battle Tactics"), [Standard Archery Tactics](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Solo_Battle_Tactics#Standard_Archery_Tactic_.232 "Solo Battle Tactics"), [Magnum-Counter](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Solo_Battle_Tactics#Magnum-Counter "Solo Battle Tactics"), [Magnum Mill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Solo_Battle_Tactics#Magnum_Mill "Solo Battle Tactics"), [Magnum+Def+N](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Solo_Battle_Tactics#Magnum.2BDefense.2BN "Solo Battle Tactics"), [Magic Stun Magnum](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Team_Battle_Tactics#Magic_Stun_Magnum "Team Battle Tactics") for strategies to use in battle.

## Obtaining the Skill

#### [Memory Book](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book "Memory Book")

Rewarded to [Humans](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human "Human") and [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elves "Elves") for clearing [Generation 14: Romeo and Juliet](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Generation_14:_Romeo_and_Juliet "Generation 14: Romeo and Juliet") and speaking to [Blaanid](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blaanid "Blaanid") during [Clear Storyline Chapter 4](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book#Fourth_Memoir "Memory Book") in the Fourth [Memoir](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book "Memory Book").

- The skill will have training requirements up to **Rank 9** completed.

#### Other Methods

- Complete the quest [The Basics of Archery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Talent_Quests#The_Basics_of_Archery "Beginner Talent Quests").

Or,

- For humans and elves, learn [Ranged Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") rank Novice or higher (by equipping a [Bow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Bows "Category:Bows") or [Crossbow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Crossbows "Category:Crossbows")), then talk to [Duncan](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Duncan "Duncan") using the [Skills Keyword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skills_Keyword "Skills Keyword").
