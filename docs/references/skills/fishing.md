## Description

| [![Fishing.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/1/19/Fishing.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Fishing.png) | Fishing is a useful skill to use when catching fish in the water. You can easily start fishing with Fishing Rod and Bait, but you need more than that to fish even better. You must know how to adequately attach the Bait, have the judgment to choose a good place, and have the patience to wait until a fish is caught once all the preparations is done. Once fish is caught, you must control the single fishing line and quickly snatch it up as the fish will challenge you with its strength. This is why many people refer to experienced fishermen as the masters of life. |

## Details

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/7/75/Fishing_Using.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Fishing_Using.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Fishing_Using.png "Enlarge")

Player using Fishing Skill

- Requires a [Fishing Rod](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Fishing_Rods "Category:Fishing Rods") or [Fishing Chair](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Fishing_Chairs "Category:Fishing Chairs") and [Fishing Bait](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Fishing_Baits "Category:Fishing Baits") equipped in order to Fish.

  - Every _attempt_ to pull out a hooked fish or item will use 1 Bait.
  - Fishing Bait can have special effects, including decreased lure time and increased catch size. See [Fishing Baits List](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fishing_Baits_List "Fishing Baits List") for more information.
  - Using a Fishing Chair while equiped with a [Reforged](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Reforge "Reforge") Fishing Rod will still use the Fishing Rod's Reforges.
- Allows the player to fish at most bodies of water for items.
  - For a list of items by location, see [Fished Items List](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fished_Items_List "Fished Items List").
  - For a list of all fish by location, see [Fish List](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fish_List "Fish List").
- Higher Ranks of this Skill decreases the time in-between catches, increases the Auto-Fishing Success Rate, and increases the chance to catch larger-sized fish.
  - Fishing has no effect on the type of item acquired.
  - Rank F Fishing is required to fish at [Reus River](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Reus_River "Reus River").
  - Auto-Fishing Success Rate is affected by the [Weather](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Weather "Weather") and [Rain Casting](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rain_Casting "Rain Casting"), multiplying the base Success Rate of Auto-Fishing by x1.00 + 0.01 per stage of Rain.
  - Auto-Fishing Success Rate is affected by [Production Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Production_Mastery "Production Mastery"), adding a flat +1% per Rank.
  - Auto-Fishing Success Rate is _not_ affected by [Dexterity](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dexterity "Dexterity").
  - Auto-Fishing Success Rate can also be increased by:
    - [Fishing Chairs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Fishing_Chairs "Category:Fishing Chairs") (x1.10 of base)
    - [Seal](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Seal "Category:Seal") Pets
    - [Fishing Titles](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fishing_Titles "Fishing Titles"), such as [SAO Nishida 2nd Title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/SAO_Nishida_2nd_Title "SAO Nishida 2nd Title"), [Ice Fisher 2nd Title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ice_Fisher_2nd_Title "Ice Fisher 2nd Title"), [Shenon 2nd Title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shenon_2nd_Title "Shenon 2nd Title"), etc.
    - [Equipment Combination Effects](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Equipment_Combination_Effects "Equipment Combination Effects"), such as the [Adonis Set Effect](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Adonis_Set "Adonis Set").
  - Some Fishing [2nd Titles](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles#2nd_Titles "Titles"), like the ones mentioned above, can also help with increasing the chances of catching bigger fish.
- If the player's inventory is full while fishing, any item received will not go to the temporary and, instead, will immediately drop to the ground.
  - Dropped items from fishing will not be able to be picked up by anyone except the person who fished them.
  - Dropped items from fishing will eventually disappear at a rate based on their resell value, with low-value items disappearing in a short time and expensive items taking longer to disappear.
    - If you plan to fish idle for a long period of time, be sure to check back occasionally in case you have fished up something or else it will disappear.
- Most equipment fished up will have 0 [Durability](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Durability "Durability"), but can be repaired.

  - This does not necessarily apply to event items obtained through fishing.
  - This does not apply to equipment that are irreparable, such as a [Gargoyle Sword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gargoyle_Sword "Gargoyle Sword").
- You may not move while loading this skill, and you may not load this skill while standing on top of someone who is using this skill.

### Ice Fishing

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/6/60/Icefishing01.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Icefishing01.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Icefishing01.png "Enlarge")

A player Ice Fishing.

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/d/d4/Ice_Fishing_Area.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Ice_Fishing_Area.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Ice_Fishing_Area.png "Enlarge")

General overview of ice fishing areas.

- In [Physis](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Physis "Physis"), the only areas where you can Fish are frozen solid.

  - Fishing must be at least Rank F in order to go Ice Fishing.
- Ice Fishing is only available north and south of the Reus River Mana Tunnel.
  - You may also use [Ice Fishing Holes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ice_Fishing_Hole "Ice Fishing Hole") built on [Homesteads](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Homesteads "Homesteads") located in Physis.

#### Making a Fishing Hole

- To make a Fishing Hole for Ice Fishing, you will need to make a hole in the ice. There are 2 methods to do this:
  - [Giants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant "Giant") are able to use [Stomp](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stomp "Stomp") create to a hole.

    - Using a [Summon Golem](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Summon_Golem "Summon Golem")'s [Stomp](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stomp_(Monster) "Stomp (Monster)") will not suffice.
  - [Humans](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Humans "Humans") and Giants can use [Land Maker](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Land_Maker "Land Maker") with a [Snowfield L-Rod](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Snowfield_L-Rod "Snowfield L-Rod").

    - Humans will need to be allied with the Giants and trade in a Snowfield L-Rod for Giants for one that can be equipped by Humans.
  - [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf "Elf") have no options to _initiate_ Ice Fishing on their own, and must use a Fishing Hole another race created.
- There are gaps in the area of the Reus River where Ice Fishing may occur, so you may need to move around to find an eligible spot.
- Different locations of the River have different rewards.
  - Locations either reward [Broken Logs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Broken_Log "Broken Log") or [Firewood](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firewood "Firewood").

    - Locations that reward [Broken Logs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Broken_Log "Broken Log") have a _significantly_ higher spawn rate of [Sturgeon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Sturgeon "Sturgeon").

      - The most reliable location is directly south of the "M" for the Reus River Mana Tunnel on the Minimap.
- Fishing Holes will last 2 real-life hours before closing.
  - The Fishing Skill will be canceled if the Fishing Hole does not exist when a Fish would be caught.

## Obtaining the Skill

#### [Memory Book](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book "Memory Book")

Complete the quest [Life Basics](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book#Third_Memoir "Memory Book") in the Third [Memoir](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book "Memory Book").
