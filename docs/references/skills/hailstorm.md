## Details

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/b/bc/Hail_Storm_Start-Up.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Hail_Storm_Start-Up.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Hail_Storm_Start-Up.png 'Enlarge')

Hail Storm on start-up.

- Requires a [Staff](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Staves_List 'Staves List') to use.
- During start-up, a blue circle will appear under the user and an icy wind surrounds the player during ice crystal formations.
- Generates large ice crystals to be hurled at opponents.
    - When the user fires an ice crystal, a new one will be created after a set period.
    - Ice crystals can be charged by waiting before launching them.
        - The speed at which the Crystal charges depends on rank.
        - Sufficient charges on the crystal will cause the enemy to be pushed back regardless of passive defense.
        - Crystals will do their base damage multiplied by the number of charges.
            - At 5th charge Hail Storm receives a 6.25x damage multiplier.
        - Larger Crystals take slightly longer to hurl at opponents than smaller ones.
    - Ice Crystals have a splash effect starting at 3 charges.
        - All enemies affected by this receive full damage from the skill.
        - The radius of the blast increases with the number of charges.
- Takes 50 [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana 'Mana') to cast, then consumes 4 Mana per second after it has loaded.

    - If the user runs out of Mana after it has loaded, the skill will be canceled.

- The skill will end 40 seconds after it has finished loading.
    - When the skill has 10 seconds left, the icon will blink in a manner similar to [Final Hit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Final_Hit 'Final Hit').
- The user will be unable to move when using Hail Storm, unless they use [Spellwalk](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spellwalk 'Spellwalk').
- If the user is knocked back while the skill is already active and has at least 2 charges, the user will lose all but 1 charge.
    - If the user is knocked back during the initial start-up or when they only have 1 charge, the skill will cancel.
- Every 100 [Intelligence](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Intelligence 'Intelligence') decreases Mana Consumption and Casting Time of Hail Storm by 2%.

    - The Casting Time can be calculated by _`(Base Casting Time) / [(1 + Int / 5000) * (1 + (Vivace Boost))]`_

- Enabling [Mini Effects](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Options_Dialog#Performance 'Options Dialog') causes the casting effects and the ice crystals to become invisible.

## Training Method

- The [Shyllien Nature Reserve](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shyllien_Nature_Reserve 'Shyllien Nature Reserve') has very tightly clustered enemies in abundance. You can train "Attack an enemy with Hail Storm" fairly quickly by attacking [Mutant Rabbits](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mutant_Rabbit 'Mutant Rabbit').

    - Use AOE pet summon effects to keep enemies away from you.
    - Expect to use a lot of mana due to being attacked constantly.

- "Attack an enemy with Hail Storm" can also be trained on [Barrier Spikes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Barrier_Spikes 'Barrier Spikes') in [Provocation](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Provocation 'Provocation').
