## Description

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| [![Herbalism.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/7/76/Herbalism.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Herbalism.png) | Herbalism is an essential skill when identifying and gathering herbs in the wilderness. There are exceptional cases of the uninitiated in Herbalism successfully gathering herbs by luck. However, gathering herbs is a very delicate process and beginners in Herbalism can often ruin intact herbs. Rare herbs tend to grow only in the deepest regions of a dungeon and, therefore, you should regularly study the different kinds of herbs and practice a careful gathering skill. |

## Details

- Passively increases the success rate of and decreases the time required when gathering Herbs from [Herb Patches](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Herb_Patch "Herb Patch"), while also granting a chance for the Herb Patch to drop two at once.

  - Gathering from [Herb Pig](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Herb_Pig "Herb Pig"), [Clover Gnu](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Clover_Gnu "Clover Gnu"), and [Clover Pig](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Clover_Pig "Clover Pig") [Pets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pets "Pets") is unaffected by Herbalism's bonuses.
  - Requires [Bare Hands](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bare_Hands "Bare Hands") to gather.

    - Somehow equipping a hand equipment (such as picking it up from the ground) will cause gathering to fail.
      - The error message will say there are no Herbs left regardless of Herb count.
- Herb Patches can be found in various places.
  - See [Herb Patch](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Herb_Patch#Found_In "Herb Patch") for more information on Herb Patches.
- Rank 9 Herbalism is required to use the Zone of Renewal [Technique](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Technique "Technique").

- As the Skill Rank of Herbalism increases, the success rate of Gathering different types of Herbs increases.
  - There is a 25% bonus when the player's Skill Rank is high enough to know _how to pick_ that Herb type.
  - There is a 50% bonus when the player's Skill Rank is high enough to know _how to identify_ that Herb type.

    - Identification also changes the Herb Patch's name from its color to the Herb's proper type name
      - The color of the Herb can still be seen when holding the `Alt` key.
  - These bonuses stack, increasing the success rate by a possible total of 75%.
- Rank F Herbalism is required to spot [Base Herb](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Base_Herb "Base Herb") Patches in the Overworld Field.

  - Note: Rank F Herbalism is _not_ required to gather from them.
  - Even at Rank F these Patches are invisible and the spot only shows a floating name labeled "Base Herb".
    - Holding the `Alt` key hides the Herb Patch name entirely.

## Obtaining the Skill

#### [Memory Book](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book "Memory Book")

Complete the quest [Life Basics](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book#Third_Memoir "Memory Book") in the Third [Memoir](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book "Memory Book").

- The skill will have training requirements up to **Rank 9** completed.

  - This quest will require you to collect 5 [Base Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Base_Herb "Base Herb"), which can be gathered _without requiring the Herbalism skill._
  - You can find Base Herbs in most [Uladh Dungeons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Uladh_Dungeons "Uladh Dungeons"), as well as from [Base Herb Homestead Patches](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Base_Herb_Patch "Base Herb Patch") and [Herb Pets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Herb_Pet_Collection_Rates "Herb Pet Collection Rates").

    - Note: Base Herb Patches will show up as "Green Herbs" until you've acquired at least Rank F in Herbalism.

#### Other Methods

- All Races can complete the quest [The Basics of Medicine](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Talent_Quests#The_Basics_of_Medicine "Beginner Talent Quests").

  - This quest is obtained upon rebirthing into the Apothecary Talent.

Or,

- For elves only, talk to [Atrata](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Atrata "Atrata") in [Filia](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Filia "Filia") with the [Skills Keyword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skills_Keyword "Skills Keyword").
- For giants only, talk to [Kirine](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Kirine "Kirine") in [Vales](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vales "Vales") with the [Skills Keyword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skills_Keyword "Skills Keyword").

Or,

- Complete a Healer's House [Part-Time Job](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Part-Time_Job "Part-Time Job") for [Manus](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Manus "Manus") or [Dilys](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dilys "Dilys") bringing your total of successfully completed jobs to 10 or more. When they offer to teach the skill, agree. An owl will then deliver a quest involving [gathering](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gathering "Gathering") a single [Base Herb](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Base_Herb "Base Herb") for the respective NPC.

  - You can do the first nine (or more) part-time jobs at other Healer's Houses. Doing one Healer's House job for [Manus](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Manus "Manus") or [Dilys](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dilys "Dilys") after doing nine elsewhere suffices.
  - Despite what the quest briefings say, you can gather the herb from any Base Herb patch, not necessarily one from the mentioned dungeon. Gathering the herb from a Clover [Gnu](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Clover_Gnu "Clover Gnu") or [Pig](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Clover_Pig "Clover Pig") pet, or receiving one as a monster drop will also work. Having a Base Herb already in one's inventory, receiving it from another player, or looting it from the ground _will not_ count.

## Training Method

### Tips

- An empty patch will still contribute to "Try to pick a Herb". When the herbs have all been picked, hold down the ALT key and click the name of the herb patch.
- Growing [Bloody Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bloody_Herb "Bloody Herb"), [Mana Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Herb "Mana Herb"), [Sunlight Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Sunlight_Herb "Sunlight Herb"), [Gold Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gold_Herb "Gold Herb"), [White Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/White_Herb "White Herb"), [Mandrakes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mandrake "Mandrake"), and [Poison Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Poison_Herb "Poison Herb") in your [Homestead](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Homestead "Homestead") is recommended. They can be harvested once a day in real time and you can have as many herb patches as you like.

  - If permission is enabled from the owner, you can pick herbs from another player's homestead.
- The [Polar Hornets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Polar_Hornet "Polar Hornet") in [Silva Forest](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Silva_Forest "Silva Forest") may leave patches of [White Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/White_Herb "White Herb") upon defeat and those will count towards skill training.
- At later ranks, you can go to [Alby Advanced](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Alby_Advanced "Alby Advanced") dungeon to find [Mandrakes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mandrake "Mandrake"), [Antidote Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Antidote_Herb "Antidote Herb"), and [Poison Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Poison_Herb "Poison Herb"). You can buy a pass from [Researcher](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Researcher "Researcher") at [Poulnabrone](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Poulnabrone "Poulnabrone") for 5,000g.

  - Alternatively, you roam inside [Solea](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Solea "Solea") looking for excavation rooms which have herb patches and ore veins that must be found with either an [L-Rod](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/L-Rod "L-Rod") or [Awakening of Light](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Awakening_of_Light "Awakening of Light").
- Picking herbs from [Herb Pigs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Herb_Pig "Herb Pig") and [Clover Gnus](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Clover_Gnu "Clover Gnu") count towards "Succeed at picking a Herb" training requirements for the herb that is picked.

  - If no herb is picked, or a base herb is picked, this will count towards the "Try to pick a Herb" requirements for base herbs only.
  - Herb Patches must be used to fulfill "Try to pick a Herb" requirements for all other herb types.
