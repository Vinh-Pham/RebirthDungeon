## Basic Information

**Humans** are the most common race in all of [Erinn](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Erinn "Erinn"), originally the only race available when the game first started until the introduction of [Elf](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf "Elf") and [Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant "Giant") races. They are native to [Uladh](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Uladh "Uladh"), but eventually discovered [Iria](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Iria "Iria") and [Belvast Island](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Belvast_Island "Belvast Island") and some adventured there. Humans are defined by their versatility, as they are not limited to any [skill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Skills "Category:Skills") and have balanced [stats](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats "Stats") compared to the other races, but in turn they do not excel as much in anything.

Players starting as a human can choose to start in either [Tir Chonaill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Chonaill "Tir Chonaill") on the [Uladh](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Uladh "Uladh") continent, or in [Qilla Base Camp](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Qilla_Base_Camp "Qilla Base Camp") on the [Iria](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Iria "Iria") continent. There is no advantage/disadvantage of choosing one over the other, although the set of [beginner quests](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Beginner_Quests "Category:Beginner Quests") varies greatly.

"Human" is also a general term for all the [player races](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Player_Races "Category:Player Races") in the [Mainstream Quests](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mainstream_Quests "Mainstream Quests").

## General Information

- Giants and Humans share the same base speed, but run slower than [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf "Elf").

  - By exact measurements, Giants and humans run at approximately 87.9% of Elf speed.
- Can transform into [Paladin](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Paladin "Paladin"), which is obtained after completing [Generation 2](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Generation_2 "Generation 2").

  - Can also have the option to change their transformation into [Dark Knight](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dark_Knight "Dark Knight"), which is obtained after completing the [Dark Knight Quest](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dark_Knight_Quest "Dark Knight Quest") after completing [Generation 3](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Generation_3 "Generation 3"). However, the transformation change is irreversible.
- Humans can also support other races by speaking to [Castanea](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Castanea "Castanea") for [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elves "Elves") and [Krug](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Krug "Krug") for [Giants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant "Giant") using the [Elf and Giant Keyword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf_and_Giant_Keyword "Elf and Giant Keyword").

  - Upon supporting Elves:
    - All Elf NPCs in [Filia](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Filia "Filia"), except for [Yoff](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Yoff "Yoff"), offer discounted prices of 5% to 20% depending on the NPC, rounded down, and applied consecutively with [Alban Heruin](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Alban_Heruin "Alban Heruin") (Wednesday)'s 5% discount.
    - ["a Friend of Elves"](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles#Friend_Titles "Titles") title will be given.
    - The [Hurray for Castanea!](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Journal#Content "Journal") Journal Achievement will be given.
    - When spoken to, Castanea will bless the player with 10 [Dexterity](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dexterity "Dexterity") and [Health](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Health "Health"), and the blessing will remain as long as the player does not leave [Connous](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Connous "Connous"), log out, or get [Knocked Unconscious](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Knocked_Unconscious "Knocked Unconscious").
    - An [Elf Assistant Character Card](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character_Cards#Assistant_Character_Cards "Character Cards") will be given.
  - Upon supporting Giants:
    - All Giant NPCs in [Vales](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vales "Vales") offer discounted prices of 8% to 12% depending on the NPC, rounded down, and applied consecutively with Alban Heruin (Wednesday)'s 5% discount.
    - ["a Friend of Giants"](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles#Friend_Titles "Titles") title will be given.
    - The [Hurray for King Krug!](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Journal#Content "Journal") Journal Achievement will be given.
    - When spoken to, Krug will bless the player with 10 [Strength](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Strength "Strength") and [Will](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Will "Will"), and the blessing will remain as long as the player does not leave [Vales](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vales "Vales"), log out, or get Knocked Unconscious.
    - An [Giant Assistant Character Card](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character_Cards#Assistant_Character_Cards "Character Cards") will be given.
    - The human player gains the ability to mount a Giant player's shoulder if they are in the same [party](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Party "Party").
  - The player can stop supporting by speaking to the race leaders with the same keyword.
    - Upon withdrawing support, discounts are no longer given, the given title cannot be worn, and the race leader will no longer bless the player.
    - Players can re-support a race as many times as they want once per real-life day.
      - A second assistant character card will not be given if the player re-supports a race.
    - Players can only give support once per real-life day.
  - While supporting a race, if the human player enables [PVP Permission between the Elf and the Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Options_Dialog#Etc. "Options Dialog"), any opposing race player who also has that option enabled will become a [PVP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/PVP "PVP") target.
  - Race support is account-wide. This means that all human characters on an account will support a race if one of them does so, and vise versa when withdrawing support.
  - Race support provides no penalty when the player encounters the opposing race.
    - Originally, when a supporting human entered the opposing race's town, the [Elf Guards](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf_Guard "Elf Guard") and [Giant Guards](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant_Guard "Giant Guard") would be hostile to them. The opposing NPCs other than [Atrata](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Atrata "Atrata") and [Taunes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Taunes "Taunes") would also refuse to talk to the human player and cause the Guards to aggro them.

## Combat Information

### Combat Advantages

- Humans are capable of learning all [skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Skills "Category:Skills") other than [Elf](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf "Elf")\- and [Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant "Giant")\- only ones.
- Humans are not restricted from any [Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talent "Talent").
- Humans are capable of [Dual Wielding](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Wielding "Dual Wielding") most [One-Handed Swords](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:One-Handed_Swords "Category:One-Handed Swords").
- Humans can learn [Final Hit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Final_Hit "Final Hit"), a melee skill that allows consecutive attacks while dual wielding.
- Humans can learn [Arrow Revolver](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Arrow_Revolver "Arrow Revolver"), an archery skill that allows them to load five arrows simultaneously.
- Most human skills and their AP costs are balanced, usually having nearly equal power and/or less AP costs than other races.
- Humans can wear most equipments outside of Elf- and Giant- only ones.
- Only Humans can maintain their [Resting](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Resting "Resting") skill at Novice rank (Elves and Giants must level Resting in their respective [beginner quests](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Beginner_Quests "Category:Beginner Quests")), taking full advantage of the [Dire Wolf](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dire_Wolf_(Enchant) "Dire Wolf (Enchant)") enchant.

### Combat Disadvantages

- Many human skills are exceeded by those from other races.
  - [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elves "Elves") have stronger [Archery Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Archery_Skills "Category:Archery Skills") with less cooldown.
  - [Giants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant "Giant") have stronger [Melee Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Melee_Skills "Category:Melee Skills").
- Human [stat](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stat "Stat") growth is equally balanced, whereas other races gain more stats in certain areas.
- Due to their slow speed, humans have difficulty outrunning enemies without a [mount](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Mounts "Category:Mounts").

## Trivia

- Before the [Human movespeed update](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Patches/Chapter_8 "Patches/Chapter 8"), Humans moved the slowest of all playable races, running at base [speed](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Movement_Speed "Stats"), whereas other races run at a multiplier of a human's speed.

  - Many monsters could also outrun a human while they would not outrun a Giant Elf player.
