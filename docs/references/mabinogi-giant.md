## Basic Information

**Giants** are a tall and strong race of humanoids native to the town of [Vales](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vales "Vales") in the [Physis](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Physis "Physis") region of Iria. They specialize in [Melee](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Melee_Skills "Category:Melee Skills") combat, focusing on the stats of [Health](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Health "Health"), [Strength](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Strength "Strength") and [Will](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Will "Will"). However, they have disadvantages in [Magic Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Magic_Skills "Category:Magic Skills") and lack [Ranged Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Archery_Skills "Category:Archery Skills"). They gain the ' [a Descendant of the Giants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/A_Descendant_of_the_Giants "A Descendant of the Giants")' title upon creation.

## General Information

- Giants and [Humans](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human "Human") share the same runspeed, but run slower than [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf "Elf").

  - By exact measurements, Giants and humans run at approximately 87.9% of Elf speed.
- Giants can carry a Human on their shoulders, acting like a mount for Giant-allied Human players, but during this time they cannot fight, perform actions, talk to NPCs, craft, or mount pets.
- Giants have a different success animation and sound than [Humans](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human "Human") and [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf "Elf").
- Giants can knock down certain trees in Physis and use them as a [maul](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Broken_Log "Broken Log").
- Giants cannot equip most clothing, armor and helmets. In some cases, they have their own version of equipment.
- When a Giant summons a mount, the pet is re-sized to be able to carry its Giant master.
- Most two-person mounts cannot support two Giants.
  - Most mounts summoned by Human or Elf players cannot be mounted by Giants.
  - A Giant summoned two-person mount can only carry one Giant at a time. It can, however, carry both a Giant and a Human or Elf rider.
  - Mimic [Pets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pets "Pets"), Flying Carpet Pets, a [Partner](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Partner "Partner")'s [Noble Carriage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Partner%27s_Noble_Carriage "Partner's Noble Carriage"), and the [Flying Food Truck](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Flying_Food_Truck "Flying Food Truck") are exceptions to this rule; two (or more) Giants may ride them at once.
- Can transform into [Beast](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beast "Beast"), which is obtained after completing [Generation 2](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Generation_2 "Generation 2").
- Giants have unique weapons only they may use: [Atlatls](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Atlatls_List "Atlatls List"), [Giant Swords](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Swords_List#Giant-Only "Swords List"), [Large Shields](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shield_List#Large_Shields "Shield List"), [Giant Blunt Weapons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blunt_Weapons_List#Giant-Only "Blunt Weapons List"), and [Giant Axes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Axes_List#Giant-Only "Axes List").

## Combat Information

### Combat Advantages

- Giants have a higher potential [HP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/HP "HP"), [Will](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Will "Will"), [Str](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Str "Str"), and [Stamina](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stamina "Stamina") capacity than Humans and Elves.
- They can [Dual Wield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Wield "Dual Wield") **only** [One-Handed Blunt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:One-Handed_Blunt_Weapons "Category:One-Handed Blunt Weapons") weapons.
- [Two-Handed Swords](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Two-Handed_Swords "Category:Two-Handed Swords") and [Lances](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lances "Lances") are single-handed for Giants, meaning they can be used with [Shields](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shields_List "Shields List") or [Guard Cylinders](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Guard_Cylinders_List "Guard Cylinders List").

  - For example, a Giant can equip a [Dustin Silver Knight Sword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dustin_Silver_Knight_Sword "Dustin Silver Knight Sword") with a [Vales Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vales_Shield "Vales Shield").
- Giants have a reduced minimum distance with [Lances](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance "Lance") as opposed to Humans and may equip Shields with them.
- They are the only race able to equip large [Shields](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shields_List "Shields List"). The [Massive Despair Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Massive_Despair_Shield "Massive Despair Shield"), [Frosted Borealis Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Frosted_Borealis_Shield "Frosted Borealis Shield"), and [Vales Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vales_Shield "Vales Shield") benefit the most from [Shield Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shield_Mastery "Shield Mastery") and reduces all damage received to 1 when coupled with the [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense "Defense") skill.

  - For starting Giant players the [Vales Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vales_Shield "Vales Shield") is superior to most shields, boasting 3 [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense "Defense") and 2 [Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Protection "Protection"), and is much easier to obtain than Shields of similar quality for Humans/Elves.
- Giants have access to the [Colin Plate Set](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Colin_Plate_Set "Colin Plate Set"), which grants the Giant both Stamina Reduction and Explosion Resistance when the full set is equipped.
- Giant weapons generally have higher splash range than Human/Elf weapons.
- [Heavy Armor Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Heavy_Armor_Mastery "Heavy Armor Mastery") gives minimal Dexterity penalties to a Giant at high ranks (-10% Dex at Rank 1).
- [Smash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Smash "Smash") deals more damage than the other races (600% at Rank 1) and their debuff chance with a sword is slightly higher (60% at Rank 1).
- [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense "Defense") skill adds more [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Defense.2FProtection "Stats") than the other races (+80 Defense and +45 Protection at Rank 1).
- [Counterattack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Counterattack "Counterattack") deals more damage than the other races (200% of enemy's damage and 200% of character's damage at Rank 1).
- [Charge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Charge "Charge") deals twice the amount of damage compared to a Human/Elf, and the usage of this skill is possible _without_ a shield/guard cylinder and without the [Reforge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Reforging "Reforging").
- Giants can equip [Atlatl](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Atlatl "Atlatl") and [Javelins](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Javelins "Javelins") which enables them to use [Throwing Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Throwing_Attack "Throwing Attack"), a ranged attack which is similar to [Magnum Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magnum_Shot "Magnum Shot") but much stronger and has 99% accuracy at Rank 1.
- [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill "Windmill") has a slightly shorter cooldown compared to other races (3 seconds at Rank 9+).
- Only Giants can learn [Stomp](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stomp "Stomp"), which is similar to [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill "Windmill") or [Rage Impact](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rage_Impact "Rage Impact"), but with a narrower range and no cooldown. The mastery title for Stomp is also the highest Strength-giving title.
- Only Giants can learn [Wind Guard](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wind_Guard "Wind Guard"), which greatly increases a Giant's defensive stats to the point where they are almost virtually immune to all damage (+100 Defense and Protection and +60 Magic Defense and Magic Protection at Rank 1).
- Only Giants can learn [Taunt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Taunt "Taunt"), which allows Giants to attract monsters' attention in place of their teammates, and tank for them due to Giant's high defensive stats.
- The number of hits/kills needed to train certain combat skills, such as Windmill, is lower for Giants than for Humans or Elves.
- [Combo Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combo_Mastery "Combo Mastery") provides more damage and more cooldown reduction than the other races (40% additional damage and 20% decreased cooldown at Rank 1) and does not receive penalties from wearing [Heavy Armor](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Heavy_Armor_List "Heavy Armor List").
- [Tumble](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tumble "Tumble") provides further distance than the other races (1000 at Rank 1).

### Combat Disadvantages

- Larger [Hitbox](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hitbox "Hitbox")
- Giants use more [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana "Mana") to cast [Icebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Icebolt "Icebolt"), [Firebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firebolt "Firebolt"), [Lightning Bolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lightning_Bolt "Lightning Bolt"), [Fireball](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fireball "Fireball"), [Thunder](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Thunder "Thunder"), and [Ice Spear](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ice_Spear "Ice Spear").
- Giants receive less Mana and [Intelligence](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Intelligence "Intelligence") from [Magic Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Mastery "Magic Mastery").
- Giants gain less Mana and Intelligence from aging and leveling.
- Giants cannot learn [Play Dead](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Play_Dead "Play Dead").
- Giants cannot learn [Rock Throwing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rock_Throwing "Rock Throwing").
- Giants cannot equip [Bows](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bows_List "Bows List") or [Crossbows](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crossbows_List "Crossbows List") and cannot learn [Archery Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Archery_Skills "Category:Archery Skills").

  - This prevents Giants from having the [Archery Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Archery_(Talent) "Archery (Talent)") and learning [Spider Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spider_Shot "Spider Shot").

## Obtaining a Giant

- A [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human "Human") player will be given an Assistant Giant Card, which acts like a Free Basic Card, by allying with the Giant race. To do so simply:

  - Talk to [Krug](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Krug "Krug"), and you will obtain the alliance keyword if you do not have it already. Talk to him with it to obtain the Assistant Giant Card.
- You may break the alliance with the Giants at any time and still keep the Giant card/character.
- Only one Assistant Giant Card is given per account (i.e. you cannot use a 2nd human character and get another card by allying with it too).
- Only one free Giant card will be given to each human character created with a "Free" character card. It is possible to obtain another by deleting all characters and pets on the account and obtaining a new Free Character Card.
- It does not matter if you already have an Assistant Elf card. You can get both races as Assistant characters, however you can only ally once per 24 hours.
- You cannot rebirth into a different race, even if you have a character card.
- You will not receive an Assistant Giant card if you have an existing Giant character.
- You may also obtain a Giant through use of an Ace, Hero or Elite Hero card.

## History

Giants live in the snowfields of [Physis](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Physis "Physis") in the town of [Vales](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vales "Vales"), to the north of the [Rano](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rano "Rano") region of [Iria](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Iria "Iria"). According to King [Krug](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Krug "Krug"), they existed before the [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf "Elf") and they feel that they have stolen their land because all of Iria once belonged to them. Another source states that at one point, Giants and Elves worked together, but the reason for this breaking apart and hatred for each other remains officially unknown.

According to [Kousai](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Kousai "Kousai"), during the time of the Birth of Iria, the " [Great Irinid](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Neamhain "Neamhain")" created Giants and Elves.

When the [Dragons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dragon "Dragon") raged, the Giants received a blessing; they were given [sharp fangs of the Coyote](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beast "Beast"). The Giants and Elves worked together to seal the Dragons in [Zardine](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Zardine "Zardine").

However, the two races eventually became greedy and wanted the [Heart of Courcle](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Heart_of_Courcle "Heart of Courcle"), the object that Irinid used to build Iria, for themselves, believing that the object would make them powerful as the Irinid. War had established between the two factions for right of the artifact. Irinid, angered, immediately cursed all of Iria and Physis was left in a cold barren blizzard and starved the Giants.

As the [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human "Human")/ [Fomorian](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fomorian "Fomorian") war rages on, both Giants and Elves were given an opportunity to ally with Humanity. However, all but [Weide](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Weide "Weide") refused the offer. Nonetheless, the Giants forged their alliance and [Karpfen](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Karpfen "Karpfen") was sent to represent the Giants.

While [Chapter 3](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Chapter_3 "Chapter 3") continues, the feud between the Giants and Elves drastically decreases. When the Heart of Courcle is found, the Giant/Elf war happened to be over.

During [The Saga: Iria](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/The_Saga:_Iria "The Saga: Iria"), the two races agree to work together to protect Erinn from threats, albeit with minor disputes.

## Trivia

- According to [Krug](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Krug "Krug"), when spoken with the [Twin Sword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Twin_Sword_Keyword "Twin Sword Keyword") Keyword, he states that Giants have a difficulty [Dual Wielding](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Wielding "Dual Wielding") [One-Handed Swords](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:One-Handed_Swords "Category:One-Handed Swords").
- The [Lance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Lances "Category:Lances") Teaser labels the weapons as "the Power of Giants."
- [Aranwen](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Aranwen "Aranwen") says that training with a Lance is "like fighting a Giant."
- According to the [Physis Wooden Lance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Physis_Wooden_Lance "Physis Wooden Lance") description, a rumor states that there is a Giant who knocks down trees every night and uses the wood to create Lances.

  - This could be referencing [Argel](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Argel "Argel"), who trains by hitting a tree repeatedly.
- On early stages of Giant model was a dwarf type. Due to the different model besides Human/Elf, many animations were tested/scrapped in Korean test server.
  - Back then, only male giant was selectable.
  - Originally, female giant was **never planned for selection due to the 'beard' issues.** This explains why [Kirine](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Kirine "Kirine") has a different polygon than the female giant.
  - Due to the difficulties of developing separate models, both female and male giant's model and animations were renewed in May, 2010 of Korean time. (Male models were mostly renewed based on the female model.)
- Before the [Human movespeed update](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Patches/Chapter_8 "Patches/Chapter 8"), Giants moved 16% faster than [Humans](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Humans "Humans").
