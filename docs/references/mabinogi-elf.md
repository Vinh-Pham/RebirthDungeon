## Basic Information

**Elves** are a humanoid race with long, pointy ears native to the town of [Filia](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Filia 'Filia') in the [Connous](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Connous 'Connous') region of [Iria](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Iria 'Iria'). In general, this race has advantages in Magic and Archery, but disadvantages in Melee combat. Their specialized stats are [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana 'Mana'), [Intelligence](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Intelligence 'Intelligence'), and [Dexterity](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dexterity 'Dexterity'). They gain the ' [a Descendant of the Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/A_Descendant_of_the_Elves 'A Descendant of the Elves')' title upon creation.

## General Information

- At base, an Elf runs 1.14x faster than [Humans](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Humans 'Humans') and [Giants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giants 'Giants').
- Elves are granted the ability to [Hide](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hide 'Hide'). While this is enabled, the Elf's [mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana 'Mana') will be depleted at a constant rate. When an Elf is using this [action](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Action 'Action'), they can be seen by anyone except monsters, in or out of PVP. Monsters cannot see or target hidden Elves, with a few exceptions.
- Elves may transform into a [Falcon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Falcon 'Falcon') after completing [Generation 2](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Generation_2 'Generation 2').
- Elves use different amounts of AP for certain skills compared to a [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human 'Human') or [Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant 'Giant').

## Combat Information

### Combat Advantages

- Elves have a slightly higher potential [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana 'Mana'), [Intelligence](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Intelligence 'Intelligence'), and [Dexterity](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dexterity 'Dexterity') capacity than Humans and Giants.
- Elves have their own special [ranged attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf_Ranged_Attack 'Elf Ranged Attack') skill, with different AP costs and lower stamina cost per rank. It also shoots two arrow simultaneously and Elves are allowed to run and fire while moving, unlike the Human's version.
- Elves' [Magnum Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magnum_Shot 'Magnum Shot') has a shorter cooldown than the human version (.5 seconds at rank 5, as opposed to a Human's 1.5), but costs 203 AP compared to a Human's 200.
- Elves are the only race that can shoot arrows while [mounted](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Mounts 'Category:Mounts').

    - This is not true when the mount is in flight.

- Elves can learn [Mirage Missile](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mirage_Missile 'Mirage Missile'), a Ranged skill that spreads an infectious HP-reducing status.
- Elves can learn [Final Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Final_Shot 'Final Shot'), a time-limit Ranged skill that boosts aiming speed and provides a limited teleportation ability.
- Only Elves can use [Quivers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Quiver 'Quiver') which hold up to 500 arrows in a 1x3 space.
- Elves use less MP to cast [Ice Spear](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ice_Spear 'Ice Spear') than Humans and Giants.

### Combat Disadvantages

- Elves have a lower potential amount of [Will](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Will 'Will') and significantly lower [Strength](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Strength 'Strength') in comparison to other races.
- Their Archery and basic Magic skills require more [AP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/AP 'AP') to rank up.

    - Elves' Magnum Shot costs 203 AP compared to a Human's 200.
    - Elves' [Icebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Icebolt 'Icebolt') costs 112 AP compared to a Human's and Giant's 92.
    - Elves' [Firebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firebolt 'Firebolt') costs 148 AP compared to a Human's and Giant's 109.
    - Elves' [Lightning Bolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lightning_Bolt 'Lightning Bolt') costs 152 AP compared to a Human's and Giant's 122.

- [Elf Ranged Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf_Ranged_Attack 'Elf Ranged Attack') has less aim speed per rank compared to the Human version (200% at Rank 1 as opposed to Human's 300%).
- Elves' [Heavy Armor Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Heavy_Armor_Mastery 'Heavy Armor Mastery') dexterity reduction penalty remains a constant 50% at all ranks past F.
- Elves' [Support Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Support_Shot 'Support Shot') has a slightly longer cooldown than Human's (1 second as opposed to a Human's 0.5 seconds).
- Elves cannot equip [Two-Handed Weapons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Two-Handed_Weapons 'Category:Two-Handed Weapons'), with the exceptions of [weapons in this category](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Two-Handed_Weapons_Elves_Can_Equip 'Category:Two-Handed Weapons Elves Can Equip').

    - Their inability to wield [Lances](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lances_List 'Lances List') also prevents them from learning Lance Skills as well as having the [Lance Combat Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talent#Lance_Talent_Combat 'Talent').

        - The lack of Lance Combat Talent also prevents Elves from learning [Berserk](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Berserk 'Berserk').

- Elves cannot [Dual Wield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Wield 'Dual Wield') any weapon with the exception of [puppets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Puppets 'Category:Puppets').

    - [Chef's Passion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Chef%27s_Passion "Chef's Passion") uses Dual Wielding [Normal Attacks](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Normal_Attack 'Normal Attack') and animations, allowing elves to pseudo-dual wield with it. However, it lacks all other dual wielding properties.

- Elven [Melee Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Melee_Skills 'Category:Melee Skills') are less effective, but require less AP to rank compared to Humans and Giants.

    - Elves' [Combat Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combat_Mastery 'Combat Mastery') gives only half as much Strength as it would for a Human and provides less Dexterity and Min and Max Damage as well, but has a total AP cost of 129 compared to Human's and Giant's 198.
    - Elves' [Smash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Smash 'Smash') has the lowest debuff success rate (40% across all ranks).
    - Elves' [Counterattack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Counterattack 'Counterattack') returns less of the enemy's damage, to a maximum of 150% at Rank 1 compared to a Human's 200%, but has a total AP cost of 112 compared to Human's and Giant's 141.
    - Elves' [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill 'Windmill') has only 0.8x the damage multiplier (200% at Rank 1) of a Human's or Giant's, but has a total AP cost of 169 compared to Human's and Giant's 177.
    - Elves' [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense 'Defense') gives a substantially less amount of base Defense (15 at Rank 1) compared to Humans and Giants, but has a total AP cost of 121 compared to Human's and Giant's 173.

- Elves' [Combo Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combo_Mastery 'Combo Mastery') provides the lowest amount of increased damage relative to the other races (20% at Rank 1).

## Obtaining an Elf

- A human player will be given an Assistant Elf Card, which acts like a Free Basic Card, by allying with the elven race. To do so simply:
    - Talk to [Castanea](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Castanea 'Castanea'), and you will obtain the alliance keyword (if not already). Use it on her to obtain the Assistant Elf Card.
    - Break any alliance with the [Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant 'Giant') race by using the "Elf and Giant" keyword with [Krug](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Krug 'Krug') at Vales. If you attempt to speak to [Castanea](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Castanea 'Castanea') in Filia while Giant-Allied, she will refuse to speak to you and send Elf Guards, if you do manage to avoid them to be able to talk to her in the first place.

        - Castanea no longer summons Elf Guards and will speak to you even if you are Giant-Allied.

    - Unlike free cards you begin with, the Assistant Elf Card does not allow you to choose premium features.
        - However, you can use the free cards you start with to create an Elf with premium features. Otherwise, in order to have premium features, you must use the [Beauty Shop](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beauty_Shop 'Beauty Shop')'s services.
    - You will not be given a second Assistant Elf Card if you delete the assistant Elf.
        - You will be given another free card identical to the one you started with if you delete every non-pet character on the account.
    - Only one Assistant Elf Card is given per account (i.e. you cannot use a 2nd human character and get another card by allying with it too).
- You may break the alliance at any time and still keep all the Elf cards and characters you may own.
- It doesn't matter if you already have an Assistant Giant card. You can get both races as Assistant characters, however you can only ally once per 24 hours.
- You **cannot rebirth into a different race**, even with character cards.
- You may also obtain an Elf through use of an Ace, Hero or Elite Hero card.

## History

Elves originate from and commonly live in the [desert of Connous](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Connous 'Connous') in the [town of Filia](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Filia 'Filia'), to the northeast of the [Rano region](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rano 'Rano') of the [Iria continent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Iria 'Iria'). According to [Castanea](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Castanea 'Castanea'), the Leader of the Elves, the Elf race once came across an illness that caused memory loss. Thereafter was a man who labeled certain things to help Elves with their memory issue. "This is a chicken. It lays eggs," is one example of many. [The Memory Tower](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Tower 'Memory Tower'), which stands in the middle of Filia on a small lake, collects the memories of all the Elves in [Filia](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Filia 'Filia') and shares them. However, memories may also be destroyed at will. It is unclear how Elves access this information or how they come upon controlling the Memory Tower. Additionally, after an unknown amount of time, a regular Elf may transform into a [Desert Ghost](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Desert_Ghost 'Desert Ghost') due to a curse placed on them by the [Irinid](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Irinid 'Irinid').

According to [Kousai](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Kousai 'Kousai'), during the time of the Birth of Iria, the " [Great Irinid](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Irinid 'Irinid')" created Elves and Giants.

When the [Dragons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dragon 'Dragon') raged, the Elves received a blessing; they were given [wisdom of the Falcon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Falcon 'Falcon'). The Elves and Giants worked together to seal the Dragons in [Zardine](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Zardine 'Zardine').

However, the two races eventually became greedy and wanted the [Heart of Courcle](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Heart_of_Courcle 'Heart of Courcle'), the object that Irinid used to build Iria, for themselves, believing that the object would make them powerful as the Irinid. War had established between the two factions for right of the artifact. Angered, the Irinid immediately cursed all of [Physis](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Physis 'Physis') and Connous. Connous, originally a green prairie and a lush forest, turned into a heated sand dune desert. In addition, [Tuatha de Danann](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tuatha_de_Danann 'Tuatha de Danann') Elves were given a curse that transformed them into [Desert Ghosts](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Desert_Ghost 'Desert Ghost'), who wander the desert of Connous and are hostile to anyone that comes their way.

The elves additionally suffer from memory loss. This is caused by Castanea, who wipes the memories of Elves and those who knew about the Irinid's Curse as a means of protecting her race from knowing the dark truth and the despair it would bring.

It was known that black-haired Elves were considered an oblivion or an omen of doom to their own race, as a black-haired Elf would be present every time disaster strikes. The black-haired Elves, such as [Atrata](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Atrata 'Atrata'), were hunted by a mysterious cult of elves who attempted to exterminate them. However, Castanea instead decided to spare her, but wiped her memory clean instead.

A more notable case is with [Yvona](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Yvona 'Yvona'), who is also a black-haired Elf. She claims to have encountered various issues in her life.

As the [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human 'Human')/ [Fomorian](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fomorian 'Fomorian') war raged on, both Elves and Giants were given an opportunity to ally with Humanity. Nonetheless, the Elves forged their alliance and [Granat](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Granat 'Granat') was sent to represent the Elves.

While [Chapter 3](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Chapter_3 'Chapter 3') continued, the feud between the Elves and Giants drastically decreases. When the Heart of Courcle is found, the Elf and Giant war seemed to have been forgotten.

During [The Saga: Iria](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/The_Saga:_Iria 'The Saga: Iria'), the two races agree to work together to prevent threats in Erinn, albeit with minor disputes.
