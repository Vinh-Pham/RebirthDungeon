## General Overview

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/6/6c/Mabinogi_2021-2_Stat_Window.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Mabinogi_2021-2_Stat_Window.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Mabinogi_2021-2_Stat_Window.png 'Enlarge')

An example of a player's stats.

### Base Stats

- Base Stats are the stats obtained from [ranking skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats_and_Skills 'Stats and Skills'), [taking classes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Classes_and_Training 'Classes and Training'), [reading books](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:In-game_Books 'Category:In-game Books'), [Age](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character#Age 'Character'), [Level](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Level 'Level'), and the long-term effects of [Food](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Food_Effects_(Long_Term)> 'Food Effects (Long Term)').

    - Stats from skills, classes, and books are permanent and will be carried through [Rebirth](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rebirth 'Rebirth').

        - However, [deranking](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skill_Reset_Capsule 'Skill Reset Capsule') skills will remove any stat gained.

    - Stats from age, level, and food are semi-permanent, remaining with the character until rebirth.
        - These stats are calculated to one decimal place, despite only whole numbers being shown.
        - For example, a player with 102 strength levels up and gains 0.5 more strength. Although their strength still shows as 102, the player would have 1 more max melee weapon damage than before.

- These stats are displayed in white in the character window. If these stats are modified in any method other than the ones described above, they will be shown in parenthesis beside the new modified stats.
- Base Stats directly affect [Combat Power](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combat_Power 'Combat Power').

### Stat Modifiers

_For stats gained when aging or leveling up, see [Level](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Level 'Level')._

- Stat Modifiers are modifiers that increase or decrease base stats. They include equipment, skill effects, [Enchants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enchants 'Enchants'), the special effects of [Cooked Food](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Food_Effects_(Short_Term)> 'Food Effects (Short Term)'), [Potion Poisoning](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Potion_Poisoning 'Potion Poisoning'), Red [Dungeon Fountains](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dungeon_Fountains 'Dungeon Fountains'), [Titles](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles 'Titles'), [Talents](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talent 'Talent'), [Reforges](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Reforge 'Reforge'), and [Totems](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Totem 'Totem').

    - This list of modifiers is for the primary statistics of STRength, INTelligence, DEXterity, Will and Luck. Secondary statistics such as defense and protection behave differently.
        - If an enchant boosts Strength by 10 will cause a flow-on effect increasing Defense by 1. The enchant would not impact Base Strength, but the flow-on increase in defense goes into _Base Defense_.

- The outcome from Stat Modifiers is displayed in a color in the character window. The color is yellow if the stat is higher than the base, but turns red if the stat is lower than base.

    - This only applies to health, mana, stamina, strength, intelligence, dexterity, will, luck, and defense.

- Stat Modifiers do not affect the player's [Combat Power](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combat_Power 'Combat Power').
- Stat Modifiersmay not lower any stat below 0.

    - Potion Poisoning is an exception, and can lower any stat to -100% of its base value. However, the character stat window still displays it as 0.

- Ranking [Light Armor Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Light_Armor_Mastery 'Light Armor Mastery') and [Heavy Armor Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Heavy_Armor_Mastery 'Heavy Armor Mastery') provides a passive boost to Defense, Protection, Magic Defense and Magic Protection, but only while wearing Light or Heavy Armor respectively. The game treats these passive boosts as Stat Modifiers, so they are not included in Base Stats. For example, when trying to obtain Base Defense of 150 to activate [Dampen Shock](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dampen_Shock 'Dampen Shock'), ranking either Armor Mastery will not help.

### Age

- Age determines how old the player character is. This directly affects the character's height; the younger the character the smaller they appear and the older the character the taller they appear.
- When creating a character, the player is allowed to choose an age between 10 and 17.
    - Players may not [rebirth](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rebirth 'Rebirth') to an age that exceeds their current age.
- Character's age will increase by 1 every [Samhain](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Samhain 'Samhain') (Saturday) at EDT: 3:00pm

UTC: 7:00pm

AEST: 5:00am

12:00pm PDT100.

- The player must log-in, change channels, or re-log in order for the age up to occur.
- After an age up, the player gains stats. See [here](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Level#Character_Growth 'Level') for the stats given on age up.

    - Players will stop gaining stats after age 25.
        - In the case of [pets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pets 'Pets'), they will stop receiving stats after age 11.

- The player's height will stop increasing after reaching age 17.
    - In the case of pets, their maximum size is age 5. After age 5, their paws/hind legs will gradually decrease in size.
- Pet's [Companion Bonus](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Companion_Bonus 'Companion Bonus') will decrease per age up until age 11.
- [Partners](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Partners 'Partners') will never age.
- After reaching 25 years of age, the character's age will be shown as "# past 25".
- After reaching age +25, the player will no longer receive any stats or AP for increasing age, nor will the game indicate that their age has increased.
- Reaching age 18 rewards "the Adult" [title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Title 'Title'). Reaching age 30 rewards "the All-Knowing". Reaching age 40 rewards "the Old".
- Characters can age indefinitely.

### Wounds

- **Wounds** reduce the player's effective health and cannot be healed by Life Potions or most other form of HP recovery. They are displayed in the 　40/100　　life bar as black100.
- Wounds are inflicted based on the attacker's [Injury](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Injury) rate of the [Damage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Damage) dealt and the target's [Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Defense.2FProtection) stat.

    - All [Magic](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Magic_Skills 'Category:Magic Skills'), [Alchemy](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Alchemy_Skills 'Category:Alchemy Skills'), and [Puppetry](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Puppetry_Skills 'Category:Puppetry Skills') Skills, as well as [Counterattack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Counterattack 'Counterattack') and [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill 'Windmill') will never cause wounds.

- The maximum amount of wounds the player can have is 100% of their life.
- Wounds are healed by the following means:
    - [Rest](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rest 'Rest')heals wounds over time.

        - Resting by a [Campfire](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Campfire 'Campfire') will heal wounds more quickly.
        - Resting in a dungeon without a campfire will not heal wounds.

    - Drinking [HP Elixirs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/HP_Elixir 'HP Elixir') or Wound Remedy [Potions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Potion 'Potion'), receiving [First Aid](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/First_Aid 'First Aid'), [Recovering Touch](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Recovering_Touch 'Recovering Touch'), or [Transforming](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Transformations 'Transformations') instantly heals wounds.

        - First Aid becomes more effective if the target is resting.

    - ["the Master of Party Healing"](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles#Skill_Titles 'Titles') title, when equipped, will cause [Healing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Healing 'Healing') and [Party Healing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Party_Healing 'Party Healing') to heal wounds.
    - [Leveling up](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Experience 'Experience'), using [Nao Soul Stones](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Nao_Soul_Stone 'Nao Soul Stone') or [Guardian Soul Stones](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Guardian_Soul_Stone 'Guardian Soul Stone'), blue [Dungeon Fountains](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dungeon_Fountains 'Dungeon Fountains'), or receiving healing treatment from a Town Healer [NPC](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/NPC 'NPC') will restore life and wounds completely.

### Hunger

- **Hunger** reduces the effectiveness of stamina regeneration and recovery by 20% and causes the player to use more stamina above the listed threshold. They are displayed in the stamina bar as 　　Current Stamina　　　　Effective Stamina　　　　Stamina from Hunger　　　　Used Stamina from Hunger　　dark orange when above hunger or black when below hunger100.
- When using [Meditation](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Meditation 'Meditation'), Hunger gradually increases at the rate of 1 point per 100 seconds.

    - Hunger is represented by Food %, which is equal to _`(Max Stamina - Hunger) / Max Stamina`_.
    - The Food percentage will not drop past 50%.
        - For summoned [pets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pets 'Pets') and [partners](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Partners 'Partners'), their lowest is 10%.
        - Players with higher maximum stamina will take longer to reach 50% hunger.

- Hunger can be replenished by eating [Food](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Food 'Food'), [Transforming](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Transformations 'Transformations'), consuming [Full Recovery Potion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Full_Recovery_Potion 'Full Recovery Potion'), or changing channels/logging on and off.

## Damage

_A measure of the damage you deal to enemies with physical attacks._

- **Damage** is the amount of [HP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Life) an enemy will lose when physically attacked.

    - The HP loss inflicted on an enemy is not permanent and may regenerate over time if no further attack is made (assuming the enemy has not been killed).
    - A portion of the player's damage will also cause [Wounds](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Wounds) to the enemy based on [Injury](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Injury) rate.

- A player's damage stat is displayed as a range between a minimum and maximum value. Any given attack on an enemy will inflict an amount of damage whose value is somewhere within that range.
    - The average damage value inflicted is determined by their [Balance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Balance) stat
    - When a weapon is equipped, the attack of that weapon is added to a player's Damage stat.
        - Without a weapon, the player uses [Bare Hands](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bare_Hands 'Bare Hands').
- A player's minimum damage cannot exceed their maximum damage. If the minimum damage would be higher than the maximum, the minimum damage will be lowered to the player's maximum damage.
    - The interaction between max damage and the effects of: [Battlefield Overture](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Battlefield_Overture 'Battlefield Overture'), [Fateweaver](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fateweaver 'Fateweaver'), and [Physical Power Potion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Physical_Power_Potion 'Physical Power Potion'), is defined as such:

`Total Buffed Damage = Max Damage * (1 + Power Pot)* (1 + Battlefield Overture * [1 + Power Pot]) * (1 + Fateweaver * [1 + Power Pot])`

- See also: [Damage Modifiers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Damage_Modifiers 'Damage Modifiers') and [Stats/Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats/Protection 'Stats/Protection').

## Magic Attack

_A measure of the bonus damage you deal to enemies with magical attacks. The higher the INT, the higher the M. ATT_

- **Magic Attack**is the boost a player will receive to all magic damage.

    - The damage increase depends on the skill's Spell Constant.

- The formula for determining Magic Attack is:
    - _`((Intelligence - 10) / 5) + Upgrade Bonus + Reforge Bonus + Enchant Bonus`_
- See also: [Magic Attack Modifiers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Attack_Modifiers 'Magic Attack Modifiers')

### Magic Damage

- **Magic Damage** is the actual damage dealt to a target by a spell.
- The formula for Magic Damage is as follows: _`{Base Damage + [Wand Bonus * (Charge Multiplier)] + (Spell Constant * Magic Attack) + (Damage Enchants * Damage Constant)}_
_* [1 + (Bolt Mastery Bonus + Elemental Mastery Bonus + Magic Weapon Mastery Bonus + Staff Elemental Level Bonus + Hebona Set Bonus + Rosemary Gloves Bonus)]_
_* Charge Multiplier * Full Charge Multiplier * Thunder Multiplier * Distance Multiplier`_
    - This formula does not apply to [Blaze](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blaze 'Blaze').
    - [Fusion Bolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fusion_Bolt 'Fusion Bolt') uses this formula twice, one for each separate bolt, when calculating damage.
    - Base Damage is the spell's base damage as shown in the skill window.
        - [Reforge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Reforge 'Reforge') effects that add maximum or minimum damage to the spell add directly to this value.
    - [Wand](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wands_List 'Wands List')Bonus is a constant for bolt spells while using a wand of the same element.

        - The value for wand [Classic Spirit Weapons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Classic_Spirit_Weapons 'Classic Spirit Weapons') depends on the Social level of the spirit, up to a value of 30.0.
        - For non-spirit wands, the values are as follows:
            - [Icebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Icebolt 'Icebolt') with any non-spirit Ice Wand: 10.0
            - [Firebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firebolt 'Firebolt') with any non-spirit Fire Wand: 5.0
            - [Lightning Bolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lightning_Bolt 'Lightning Bolt') with any non-spirit Lightning Wand: 7.0
        - All other cases, including with [Bare Hands](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bare_Hands 'Bare Hands') or [Staff](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Staves_List 'Staves List'): 0

    - Charge Multiplier is the number of charges. This only applies to [Firebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firebolt 'Firebolt'), [Ice Spear](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ice_Spear 'Ice Spear'), [Hail Storm](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hail_Storm 'Hail Storm'), and [Lightning Rod](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lightning_Rod 'Lightning Rod'); all other magic spells will always be calculated as if they have 1 charge.
    - Spell Constant is a constant that varies by spell. The values are as follows:
        - [Healing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Healing 'Healing'): 0.03 (min), 0.04 (max)
        - [Party Healing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Party_Healing 'Party Healing'): 0.04 (min), 0.05 (max)
        - [Icebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Icebolt 'Icebolt'): 0.35 (min), 0.4 (max)
        - [Lightning Bolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lightning_Bolt 'Lightning Bolt'): 0.3 (min), 0.6 (max)
        - [Firebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firebolt 'Firebolt'): 0.35 (min), 0.5 (max)
        - [Ice Spear](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ice_Spear 'Ice Spear'): 0.45 (min), 1.4 (max)
        - [Thunder](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Thunder 'Thunder'): 0.4 (min), 2.1 (max)
        - [Fireball](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fireball 'Fireball'): 8.0 (min), 12.0 (max)
        - [Hail Storm](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hail_Storm 'Hail Storm'): 1.6 (min), 3.2 (max)
        - [Lightning Rod](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lightning_Rod 'Lightning Rod'): 7.0 (min), 12.0 (max)
        - [Meteor Strike](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Meteor_Strike 'Meteor Strike'): 70.0 (min), 160.0 (max)
        - [Shockwave](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shockwave 'Shockwave'): 2.0 (min), 5.0 (max)
    - [Magic Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Magic_Attack) is the player's Magic Attack stat as shown in the character window.
    - [Damage Enchants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Damage_Modifiers 'Damage Modifiers')is the sum of all bonuses from enchants to minimum and/or maximum damage, depending on what is being calculated.

        - The bonus maximum and minimum damage from [Battlefield Overture](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Battlefield_Overture 'Battlefield Overture') counts as enchanted damage.

    - Damage Constant is a constant that lowers the effects of damage enchants and varies by spell. The values are as follows:
        - Icebolt: 0.04 (min), 0.08 (max)
        - Firebolt: 0.2 (min), 0.4 (max)
        - Lightning Bolt: 0.22 (min), 0.44 (max)
        - Ice Spear: 0.05 (min), 0.1 (max)
        - Fireball: 0.25 (min), 0.5 (max)
        - Thunder: 0.25 (min), 0.5 (max)
        - Hail Storm: 0
        - Meteor Strike: 0
        - Lightning Rod: 0
        - Shockwave: 0
    - [Elemental Mastery](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elemental_Mastery_(disambiguation)> 'Elemental Mastery (disambiguation)'), [Bolt Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bolt_Mastery 'Bolt Mastery'), and [Magic Weapon Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Weapon_Mastery 'Magic Weapon Mastery') Bonuses are the percent bonus applied by the respective mastery skill, used as a decimal (e.g. 15% is 0.15).

        - Reforge effects for the mastery's damage and [title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Title 'Title') bonuses (if applicable) for the mastery's efficiency/damage add directly to this value.

    - Staff Elemental Level Bonus is a bonus applied when using a bolt spell with a staff that has been elementally charged with the same element. The bonus values are as follows:
        - Level 0: 0
        - Level 1: 0.05
        - Level 2: 0.1
        - Level 3: 0.15
    - [Hebona Set](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hebona_Set 'Hebona Set')Bonus is a bonus applied to Icebolt and Firebolt when affected by the Icebolt Enhancement and Firebolt Enhancement status effect from the Hebona Set.

        - See [Equipment Combination Effects](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Equipment_Combination_Effects 'Equipment Combination Effects') for details on how set bonuses work.
        - The bonus is 0.15 when the enhancement is active, otherwise the bonus is 0 if there is no enhancement.

    - [Rosemary Gloves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rosemary_Gloves 'Rosemary Gloves')Bonus is the percent bonus applied to Icebolt, Ice Spear, and Hail Storm if equipped with a Rosemary Gloves.

        - The bonus is 0.15 when equipped, otherwise the bonus is 0 when not equipped.

    - Full Charge Multiplier is a multiplier for being fully charged that varies by spell. The values are as follows:
        - Firebolt: 1.3
        - Ice Spear: 1.3
        - Hail Storm: 1.25
        - Lightning Rod: 1.5~3.3 (varies by rank)
            - See [Lightning Rod's summary table](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lightning_Rod#Summary 'Lightning Rod') for values per rank.
        - Everything else: 1.0
    - Thunder Multiplier is a multiplier for Thunder that varies by number of charges and whether it is the final hit. The multiples are:
        - 1.0 for all hits of Thunder before the final hit.
        - 1.5 for the final hit of Thunder if charged with less than 5 charges (including a single charge).
        - 2.0 for the final hit of Thunder if charged with all 5 full charges.
    - [Distance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Distance 'Distance')Multiplier is a multiplier for Shockwave and Meteor Strike that decreases as the target's distance increases from the center of the spell.

        - For Shockwave, the multiplier starts at 1.0 when near the center and scales linearly down to 0.1 when at the edge of the area of effect.
        - For Meteor Strike, the multiplier is 1.0 near the center of impact, 0.75 when halfway between the center and the edge, and 0.5 when near the edge.

        - [Homestead Pinkie Figure](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Homestead_Pinkie_Figure 'Homestead Pinkie Figure') or [Homestead R.S.A. Tent Figure](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Homestead_R.S.A._Tent_Figure 'Homestead R.S.A. Tent Figure') \- 1%
        - [Forget-Me-Not Picture](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Forget-Me-Not_Picture 'Forget-Me-Not Picture') \- Up to 1%
        - Various event titles - Varies
        - [Spirit Weapon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spirit_Weapon 'Spirit Weapon') Analysis Skill - Up to 10%
        - [Spirit Weapon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spirit_Weapon 'Spirit Weapon') Enrage Skill - Up to 10%

### Damage Calculations

The final damage dealt on a monster is calculated in the following steps:

1\. Base damage plus all additional damages (from enchants, stats except [Int](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Int 'Int'), skills)

2\. Skill Multipliers (if applicable)

3\. Bonus Damage Multiplier (if applicable)

4\. Death Mark Damage Multiplier (if applicable)

5\. Rage Impact Multiplier (if applicable)

6\. Support Shot Multiplier (if applicable)

7\. Enemy Defense Deduction

8\. Enemy Effective Protection Deduction

9\. Shield Melee Damage Reduction (if applicable)

10\. Elemental Multiplier (if applicable)

11\. Passive Defense (if applicable)

12\. Skill Resistance\* (if applicable)

- Skill Resistance is a hidden monster status that reduces damage taken from specific skills.
    - Notable example of monsters with skill resistance include: [Form of Nightmares](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Form_of_Nightmares 'Form of Nightmares'), various [Tech Duinn](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tech_Duinn 'Tech Duinn') Monsters, [Talvish (Monster)](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talvish_(Monster)> 'Talvish (Monster)')

## Injury

_The chance that an attack you deal to an enemy will injure them. Injuries cannot be treated with potions or healing magic._

- **Injury** rate is the percentage of damage that is inflicted as [Wounds](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Wounds) on the enemy.

    - For example, if a player has an Injury Rate of 10~~20% and hits 100 damage on an enemy, the enemy will receive 10~~20 points of wound.

- Injury rates are most useful on enemies that naturally recover HP very quickly. This includes Dungeon [Recovery Rooms](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dungeon_Room_Types#Recovery_Rooms 'Dungeon Room Types').
- Every 10 [Dex](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Dexterity) adds +1.0% to Maximum Wound Rate and +0.5% to Minimum Wound Rate.
- Every 10 [Will](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Will) adds +2.0% to Maximum Wound Rate and +0.5% to Minimum Wound Rate.
- Maximum and Minimum Injury Rate is reduced by 1.0% for every _Effective_ [Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Defense.2FProtection) point on the target.
- [Magic](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Magic_Skills 'Category:Magic Skills'), [Alchemy](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Alchemy_Skills 'Category:Alchemy Skills'), [Puppetry](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Puppetry_Skills 'Category:Puppetry Skills') Skills, [Counterattack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Counterattack 'Counterattack'), [Lance Counter](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance_Counter 'Lance Counter'), [Combo: Counter Punch](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combo:_Counter_Punch 'Combo: Counter Punch'), [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill 'Windmill'), and [Doppelganger](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Doppelganger 'Doppelganger') are not affected by the injury stat as they cannot cause wounds.
- See also: [Injury Modifiers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Injury_Modifiers 'Injury Modifiers').

## Critical

_The chance that an attack you deal will inflict deadly damage to your enemies. Your Critical rate is affected by your Luck and Will._

- **Critical** determines the chance to score the [Critical Hit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical_Hit 'Critical Hit') Passive Skill, which greatly increases the damage of the attack that activates it.

    - Critical is capped at 999.9%.
        - Final Critical Rate is based off of enemy Protection and Maximum Critical Rate.

- Critical is based on the player's [Will](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Will) and [Luck](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Luck) Stats, the equipped [Weapon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Weapons 'Category:Weapons') and [Title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles 'Titles'), the equipped Weapon's [Enchants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enchant 'Enchant') and/or [Reforges](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Reforge 'Reforge'), and skills.

    - Every 10 Will adds +1.0% to Critical.
    - Every 5 Luck adds +1.0% to Critical.
    - [Axe Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Axe_Mastery 'Axe Mastery'), [Crossbow Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crossbow_Mastery 'Crossbow Mastery'), [Lance Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance_Mastery 'Lance Mastery'), and [Dual Wield Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Wield_Mastery 'Dual Wield Mastery') as well as their skill's [Master Titles](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Master_Titles 'Master Titles') adds Critical when equipped with an [Axe](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Axes_List 'Axes List'), [Crossbow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crossbows_List 'Crossbows List'), [Lance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lances_List 'Lances List'), or [Dual Wield Swords](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Swords_List 'Swords List') respectively.

        - Skill bonuses may not show up on the player's character stat window depending on the Skill.

    - [Bare Hands](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bare_Hands 'Bare Hands') adds 10% to Critical.
    - Most [Two-Handed Weapons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Two-Handed_Weapons 'Category:Two-Handed Weapons') increases the Critical Rate of [Smash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Smash 'Smash') by 5%.
    - `[(Will - 10) / 10] + [(Luck - 10) / 5] + Weapon Critical + Enchant Bonus + Reforge Bonus + Skill Bonuses + Title Bonuses`
        - The chance to score a critical hit for [Magic Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Magic_Skills 'Category:Magic Skills') instead uses the above formula but without factoring in the Weapon's Critical.

- Critical is reduced by 2.0% for every _Effective_ [Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Defense.2FProtection)/ [Magic Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Defense.2FProtection) point (actual damage reduction) on the target.

    - `(Attacker's Critical %) - (Target's Effective Protection * 2)`
    - [Windmill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Windmill 'Windmill'), [Stomp](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stomp 'Stomp'), [Crash Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crash_Shot Strike](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Charging_Strike 'Charging Strike'), [Focused Fist](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Focused_Fist 'Focused Fist'), [Act 4: Rising Action](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Act_4:_Rising_Action 'Act 4: Rising Action'), [Act 7: Climactic Crash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Act_7:_Climactic_Crash 'Act 7: Climactic Crash'), [Flash Launcher](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Flash_Launcher 'Flash Launcher'), [Bullet Storm](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bullet_Storm 'Bullet Storm'), [Shooting - When using an attack that hits multiple targets, the chance of landing a [Critical Hit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical_Hit 'Critical Hit') is only calculated against the selected target.

    - [Healing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Healing 'Healing') and [Party Healing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Party_Healing 'Party Healing') use their own mechanics to score Critical Healing, separate from [Critical Hit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical_Hit 'Critical Hit') and Critical.

    - Counterattack, Lance Counter, and Combo: Counter Punch uses the enemy's Critical rather than the player's.

- Critical Hits increase the [Stun](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stun 'Stun') time, [Knockdown](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Knockdown 'Knockdown') targets rather than Pushing them back, may reset the enemy's AI, and may change the enemy's [Aggro](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Aggro 'Aggro').

### Maximum Critical Rate

- The Maximum Critical Rate for [Critical Hit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical_Hit 'Critical Hit') is normally capped at 30%.

    - Certain Skills may exceed this cap during calculation.
        - For example, [Way of the Gun](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Way_of_the_Gun 'Way of the Gun') causes all Dual Gun Normal Attacks to be a critical hit, regardless of the player's Critical stat or the enemy's Protection stat.

- Maximum Critical Rate can be increased:
    - Multiple [Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talent 'Talent') levels add up to +7.5% Maximum Critical Rate.
    - Every 50 Total [Arcana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Arcana 'Arcana') Levels adds +2% Maximum Critical Rate (up to +10%).
    - A [Spirit Weapon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spirit_Weapon 'Spirit Weapon')'s Spirit Unleashing Enhancement adds +5% Maximum Critical Rate.
    - [Homestead Cailleach's Barrier Figure](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Homestead_Cailleach%27s_Barrier_Figure "Homestead Cailleach's Barrier Figure") adds +1% Maximum Critical Rate.
- The highest possible Maximum Critical Rate is: 53.5%.

### Critical Damage

- Critical Damage is affected by the player's [Critical Hit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical_Hit 'Critical Hit') rank, [R-type Special Upgrades](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Special_Upgrades 'Special Upgrades'), Homestead [Figures](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Figures 'Figures'), [Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talent 'Talent') levels, [Spirit Weapon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spirit_Weapon 'Spirit Weapon') Attunement Enhancement, and certain Skills (i.e. [Combo: Counter Punch](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combo:_Counter_Punch 'Combo: Counter Punch'), [Spider Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spider_Shot 'Spider Shot')).

    - Critical Damage is calculated as: `(Base Damage Range Roll) + [Max Damage * Total Critical Damage]`.

        - Total Critical Damage is the sum of all Critical Damage modifiers.

    - Landing a [Critical Hit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical_Hit 'Critical Hit') with [Mana Crystallization](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Crystallization 'Mana Crystallization'), [Shock](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shock 'Shock'), [Shadow Spirit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shadow_Spirit 'Shadow Spirit'), or [Spear of God](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spear_of_God 'Spear of God') will not increase the damage dealt.

## Balance

_The likelihood that your attacks will deal damage close to the max damage you are capable of. The more Balance you have, the higher the chance. Balance is affected by Dexterity and skill ranks._

- **Balance** affects the distribution of [Damage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Damage) output from the player.
- The formula for calculating Balance from current Dexterity has diminishing returns.
    - The dexterity required to hit a certain balance can be calculated as `(20.34565 / (2^(BALANCE/-8.728944))) - 9.814582`.dex2baleh:round(-9.814582 + (20.34565 / (pow(2,(B/-8.728944)))))
    - Desired Balance:
    - Dexterity Required: 12
    - Balance received from Dex caps out at 50%.
- For [Combat Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combat_Mastery 'Combat Mastery') and [Ranged Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ranged_Attack 'Ranged Attack'), each rank gives an additional 1% Balance when using Melee or Archery attacks, respectively.
- [Enchants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enchant 'Enchant') that affect Balance influence both Melee and Archery by the amount shown; and magic by a fraction of that amount.
- [Sword Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Sword_Mastery 'Sword Mastery') increases Balance by 1% every rank when using a [Sword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Swords_List 'Swords List'). This value is doubled when [Dual Wielding](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Wielding 'Dual Wielding').
- Intelligence influences [Magical Balance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Magic_Balance).
- Balance is capped at 80%.
    - This limit can be increased by 1% from Total [Arcana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Arcana 'Arcana') level Bonuses.
- Balance can be roughly represented as a bell shaped distribution. The equation for approximating median damage is: `([Max Damage - Min Damage] * Balance) + Min Damage`.

    - The Median Damage represents where the peak would be in a regular bell shaped distribution but is not necessarily the mean or mode.
    - As Balance can be approximately represented by a bell shaped distribution, then the following pattern tends to occur: 50% of the time, the player will land a damage that is greater than the median damage (before applying Defense, Protection, Elements, and Skill Multipliers) calculated by the above formula. Conversely, the other 50% of the time, the player will hit less than the mean damage.
    - For the purposes of randomizing damage the distribution goes beyond maximum or minimum damage depending on whether the balance is greater than or less than 50% respectively. From whichever value would give a greater range to the balance/median point, the distribution extends out approximately equally beyond the maximum or minimum damage value. All values that would occur beyond maximum/minimum are instead rounded to the maximum/minimum. This means that the probability that a given damage figure would be greater or less than maximum or minimum damage respectively is added to the probability of rolling maximum or minimum damage respectively. This generally means that with extremely low or high balance values the minimum or maximum damage respectively ends up being the mode(most common) damage value.
        - Thus even with 100% magic balance balance a player will roll maximum damage half the time and the other half roll damage values down to their minimum at a decreasing probability.
            - Therefore in that situation the mean(average) damage is actually less than the median damage. Theoretically the only time the mode, median and mean would be the same is at 50% balance. When balance is less than 50% average damage is higher than median damage and when balance is greater than 50% average damage is lower than median damage.

### Magic Balance

- **Magic Balance** determines the average damage of magic attacks.
- It is actually a hidden stat, and not displayed on the character screen.
- Magic Balance is capped at 100%.
- The formula for Magic Balance is as follows:
    - `[(Int-10) / 4] + 30 + (magic_mastery_correction) + [(enchant_bonuses)(spell_constant)]`[\[3\]](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#cite_note-3)
        - Magic Mastery Correction is a special constant that depends on the player's [Magic Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Mastery 'Magic Mastery') rank.

            - Each additional Magic Mastery rank adds +1 to this constant. Rank F Magic Mastery is equal to 1; Rank 6 is equal to 10. Rank Novice is 0.

        - Enchant Bonuses are enchants that affect normal damage balance. The total damage balance effects of a player's enchants are tallied up and multiplied by the Spell Constant.
        - Spell Constant is a special number that varies from spell to spell. The spell constants are as follows:
            - [Icebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Icebolt 'Icebolt'): 0.4
            - [Firebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firebolt 'Firebolt'): 0.4
            - [Lightning Bolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lightning_Bolt 'Lightning Bolt'): 0.2
            - [Ice Spear](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ice_Spear 'Ice Spear'): 0.5
            - [Thunder](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Thunder 'Thunder'): 0.4
            - [Fireball](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fireball 'Fireball'): 0.5
- Only [Wands](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wands_List 'Wands List') affect the balance of spells; other weapons have no effect on spell casting.
- See also: [Balance Modifiers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Balance_Modifiers 'Balance Modifiers').

### Defense/Protection

_A measure of your physical damage mitigation. The more Defense you have, the less damage you take. The higher STR, the higher DEF._

_Reduces incoming physical damage by #%. The higher the protection, the lower the chances of taking critical physical attacks._

_A measure of your magical damage mitigation. The more Defense you have, the less damage you take. The higher the Will, the higher the Magic Defense._

_Reduces incoming magical damage by #%. The higher the magic protection, the lower the chances of taking critical magic attacks. Magic protection increases as Intelligence increases._

- **Defense (Def)** reduces physical [damage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Damage) received by the specified amount.

    - Every 10 [Str](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Strength) contributes to 1 Defense.
    - The [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense 'Defense') skill passively adds to base Defense.
    - Certain skills, [Tethra](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tethra_(Sword)> 'Tethra (Sword)'), and [Armor Pierce](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Armor_Pierce) ignores Defense.
    - Not to be confused with the [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense 'Defense') skill.

- **Protection** reduces physical damage received by a percentage. It also reduces the enemy's effective [Injury](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Injury) and physical [Critical](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Critical) rates.

    - The values for protection (Protection in the character screen) to _Effective_ Protection (the actual damage, injury, and critical reduction) can be found [here](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats/Protection 'Stats/Protection').
    - Physical Critical Chance is reduced by 2.0% for every _Effective_ Protection point on the target.

        - Certain skills ignore this.

    - Maximum and Minimum Injury Rate is reduced by 1.0% for every Protection point on the target.
    - [Life Drain](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Life_Drain 'Life Drain'), [Shadow Spirit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shadow_Spirit 'Shadow Spirit'), [Wings of Rage](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wings_of_Rage 'Wings of Rage'), [Dance of Death](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dance_of_Death 'Dance of Death') and [Celestial Spike](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Celestial_Spike 'Celestial Spike') are the only skills that can completely ignore Protection.
    - Protection experiences diminishing returns after 32, meaning more Protection is needed to reduce 1% more damage as values get higher.
    - Hard Capped at an _Effective_ Protection value of 90.0%, meaning that both Base Stats and Stat Modifiers cannot contribute beyond this number (without special exceptions).

- **Magic Defense**reduces magical and alchemy damage received by the specified amount.

    - Every 10 [Will](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Will) contributes to 1 Magic Defense.

- **Magic Protection**reduces magical and alchemy damage received by a percentage. It also reduces the enemy's effective magic/alchemy Critical rates.

    - Every 20 [Int](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Intelligence) contributes to 1 Magic Protection.
    - The values for protection to Effective Protection can be found [here](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats/Protection 'Stats/Protection').
    - Magic Protection experiences diminishing returns after 31, meaning more Magic Protection is needed to reduce 1% more damage as values get higher.
    - Magical Critical Chance is reduced by 2.0% for every Magic Protection point on the target.
        - Certain skills ignore this.
    - Hard Capped at an _Effective_ Magic Protection value of 90.0%, meaning that both Base Stats and Stat Modifiers cannot contribute beyond this number (without special exceptions).

- The following also affect the above stats:
    - Most [Equipment](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Equipment 'Category:Equipment') worn.

        - For [Bodywear](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Clothing/Armor 'Category:Clothing/Armor'), [Heavy Armor](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Heavy_Armor_List 'Heavy Armor List') contributes the most to defensive stats, followed by [Light Armor](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Light_Armor_List 'Light Armor List'), then [Clothing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Clothing_List 'Clothing List').
        - Most [Accessories](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Accessories_List 'Accessories List'), [Headgear](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Headgear 'Category:Headgear'), [Gloves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gloves_List 'Gloves List'), and [Shoes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shoes_List 'Shoes List') add no or miniscule amounts of defensive stats.
        - [Robes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Robes_List 'Robes List') and [Wings](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wings_List 'Wings List') do not contribute to any defense stat.
        - [Shields](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shields_List 'Shields List') contribute to Defense and Protection. [Guard Cylinders](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Guard_Cylinders_List 'Guard Cylinders List') contribute to Magic Defense and Magic Protection. Both add hidden melee/archery Defense and Protection against critical that is not displayed in the character's stat window.
        - [Marionettes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Marionettes_List 'Marionettes List') also contribute miniscule amounts of Defense and Protection.
        - There are very few equipment, or [Magic Armor](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Magic_Armor 'Category:Magic Armor'), that affects Magic Defense and Magic Protection.

    - Certain skills, such as [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense 'Defense'), [Healing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Healing 'Healing'), [Party Healing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Party_Healing 'Party Healing'), [Wind Guard](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wind_Guard 'Wind Guard'), and [Enduring Melody](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enduring_Melody 'Enduring Melody'), temporarily increase the Defense, Protection, Magic Defense, and/or Magic Protection stat while active.

        - Similarly, the Techniques [Blunting Field](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blunting_Field 'Blunting Field') and [Dampen Shock](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dampen_Shock 'Dampen Shock') passively increase the player's protection/magic protection and defense/magic defense, respectively, while they are equipped in the currently activated set.

    - All [Lances](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance 'Lance'), the [Masterpiece Bow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Masterpiece_Bow 'Masterpiece Bow'), the [Bhafel Slayer](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bhafel_Slayer 'Bhafel Slayer'), the [Bhafel Hunter](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bhafel_Hunter 'Bhafel Hunter'), the [Black Dragon Knight's Bow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Black_Dragon_Knight%27s_Bow "Black Dragon Knight's Bow"), and all [Crossbows](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crossbow 'Crossbow') possess a [Piercing Level](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Piercing_Level 'Piercing Level'), which has the ability to ignore some Defense and Protection.
    - [Brionac](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Brionac 'Brionac'), when awakened, will permanently remove 3 protection from almost all Boss (not CP-level) type monsters with each hit.

        - See Brionac page for more information.

    - [Fateweaver](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fateweaver 'Fateweaver')'s Aspect of Fortification will remove 10% of a monsters current protection.
    - [Frozen Blast](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Frozen_Blast 'Frozen Blast'), [Spinning Uppercut](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spinning_Uppercut 'Spinning Uppercut'), and [Devil's Dash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Devil%27s_Dash "Devil's Dash") can temporarily lower the opponent's Defense and Protection.
    - [Hydra Transmutation](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hydra_Transmutation 'Hydra Transmutation') can temporarily lower the opponent's Magic Defense and Magic Protection.
    - [Berserk](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Berserk 'Berserk') reduces the user's Defense, Protection, Magic Defense, and Magic Protection to zero.
    - [Mana Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Shield 'Mana Shield')does not take the user's Defense, Protection, and Magic Protection stats into calculation.

        - Magic Defense and Hidden Shield Defense are still taken into calculation, however.

    - [Heavy Armor Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Heavy_Armor_Mastery 'Heavy Armor Mastery'), [Light Armor Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Light_Armor_Mastery 'Light Armor Mastery'), and [Shield Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shield_Mastery 'Shield Mastery') increases Defense, Magic Defense, Protection, and Magic Protection while wearing the respective equipment.
- Protection is calculated as: `(Monster Base Protection - Flat Protection Reduction) * (1 - Percentage Protection Reduction) - 5 * Player Piercing Level`
- Damage received is calculated as: `Damage * (100 - Damage Reduction from Protection) / 100 - Defense` or `Damage * (100% - Damage Reduction from Protection%) - Defense`
    - For example, if one had 20 Defense and 6 Protection (10% Damage Reduction) and received 500 damage, the damage would be reduced to be `500 * (100 - 10) / 100 - 20 = 430`.
    - Any additional Defense and/or Protection from skills is applied after the first series of Defense/Protection calculations.
        - For example, if one had 20 Defense and 6 Protection (10% Damage Reduction), activated Human Defense Rank 1 (65 Defense and 35 Protection \[38% Damage Reduction\]), and received 500 damage, the damage would be reduced to `{[500 * (1.00 - 0.10) - 20] * (1.00 - 0.38)} - 65 = 202`.
    - Contrary to popular belief, it is not possible to have _negative_ Defense and/or Protection.

        - If the defensive stat ignored and/or reduced is greater than the enemy's defensive stat, damage calculations will simply use 0 for the stat.
- [Passive Defenses](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Passive_Defense 'Passive Defense') reduces damage by a certain percentage, however this is not to be confused with Protection.
- Certain monsters are [immune](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Invulnerability 'Invulnerability') to traditional forms of attack, however this is not to be confused with Protection.
- See also: [Defense Modifiers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense_Modifiers 'Defense Modifiers') and [Protection Modifiers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Protection_Modifiers 'Protection Modifiers').

## Armor Pierce

_A measure of your ability to ignore enemy defenses with physical attacks. The higher the Dexterity, the higher the Defense Penetration._

- **Armor Pierce**, also known as **Defense Penetration**, is the amount of enemy [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Defense.2FProtection) ignored when performing an attack.
- The amount of Defense ignored is equal to the amount of one's Armor Pierce.
    - If the Defense ignored by Armor Pierce is greater than the enemy's Defense, damage calculations will simply use 0 for the stat.
- The formula for Armor Pierce is: _`((Dexterity - 10) / 15) + Skill Bonus`_
    - [Axe Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Axe_Mastery 'Axe Mastery') and [Crossbow Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crossbow_Mastery 'Crossbow Mastery') adds Armor Pierce when equipped with an [Axe](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Axes_List 'Axes List') or [Crossbow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crossbows_List 'Crossbows List'), respectively.
- Not to be confused with [Piercing Level](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Piercing_Level 'Piercing Level').

## Movement Speed

- **Movement Speed** is the speed at which the character moves.
- It is actually a hidden stat, and not displayed on the character screen.
- The speed at which a [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human 'Human') moves is defined as "normal" movement speed.

    - As of the [Renovation: Season 5: Close Combat and Lance Changes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Patches/Chapter_7#Renovation:_Season_5:_Close_Combat_and_Lance_Changes 'Patches/Chapter 7'), human movement speed has been increased to the same speed as [Giants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant 'Giant'), which was 1.18x faster than humans.
    - [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf 'Elf') move 1.14x faster than humans. Before the Renovation: Season 5 update it was 1.32x faster than humans.
    - Movement speed boosts always increases in proportion to the character's race.

- Movement speed can be temporarily increased by [Awakening of Light](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Awakening_of_Light 'Awakening of Light'), [Wave Sweeper](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wave_Sweeper 'Wave Sweeper'), certain [Titles](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles 'Titles') (e.g. "Homestead", "the Busy", "Outlaw Hunter", "Is Gone with the Wind", and "Snow Showers and Flowers"), [Shylock's Step](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shylock%27s_Step "Shylock's Step"), [Movement Speed Potions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Buff_Potions 'Category:Buff Potions'), and [March Song](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/March_Song 'March Song').

    - In [Commerce](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Commerce 'Commerce'), only the speed of the Backpack and Handcart are increased. The Hyper Speed Burst and Movement Speed Increase Potions affect all transport mounts including the Wagon and Pack Elephant.
    - If the hyper potion is not giving the speed bonus, dismounting and remounting will resolve the problem.
    - Most movement speed buffs do not stack with each other; only the buff that gives the biggest bonus will take effect. The movement speed set bonus from Fleet Feet Shoes, however, will stack.

- Movement Speed has no effect while [transformed into a creature](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Transformation_Mastery 'Transformation Mastery') or [flight speed](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Flying_Mounts 'Category:Flying Mounts').
- Movement speed affects the running speed of [Charge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Charge 'Charge') and [Lance Charge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance_Charge 'Lance Charge').

    - Racial bonuses does not affect Charge speed, however.

- [Dischord](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dischord 'Dischord') and [Mirage Missile](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mirage_Missile 'Mirage Missile') temporarily decreases movement speed of the impacted target.
- [Fantastic Chorus](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fantastic_Chorus 'Fantastic Chorus')'s Sorrow Sonata decreases the movement speed of targets within the skill's area of effect.
- [Spellwalk](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spellwalk 'Spellwalk') decreases the user's movement speed except at Rank 1.

According to [testing done](https://www.reddit.com/r/Mabinogi/comments/1dy0vze/priority_groups_for_running_speed_by_neptunyan/) by reddit users u/Neputunyan and u/Kaibaria, There are currently 10 different types of movement speed modifier groups. ## Status Effects

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/4/42/Detailed_Status_Effect_Window.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Detailed_Status_Effect_Window.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Detailed_Status_Effect_Window.png 'Enlarge')

The detailed status effect window.

**Status effects** are special conditions that a character is put into or applied onto the enemy via using a certain skill, ability or from an in-game effect.

- They usually appear below your character's Health bar.
    - Not all status effects have an icon or any visual indication.
- Information on the effect's duration (if applicable) can be viewed in the Status Effect window.
- All status effects are temporary under normal conditions.
    - Some may disappear during logout or changing channels, some, like Potion Poisoning, will not.

**[See here for a full list.](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats/Status_Effects 'Stats/Status Effects')**
