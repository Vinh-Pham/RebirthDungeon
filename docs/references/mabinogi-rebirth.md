## Description

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/4/45/Rebirth.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Rebirth.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Rebirth.png 'Enlarge')

A character being reborn.

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/8/8e/RebirthBeautyWindow.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:RebirthBeautyWindow.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:RebirthBeautyWindow.png 'Enlarge')

The second step of a character rebirth.

[Nao](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Nao 'Nao') guides [Milletians](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Milletian 'Milletian') through the process of **rebirth**.
To be reborn means to abandon an old body and be born into a new one, in her own words. However, the soul itself is not altered.

- Characteristics such as physical features, age, and level that were associated with the old body can be bestowed onto the new one.
- **Warning:** If you rebirth after 12:00PM Saturday(PST), before you log-in and receive your aging AP points, you will not Age nor gain the AP for aging.

## Benefits of Rebirth

- Rebirthing is the primary way to get a higher [cumulative level](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Level 'Level') and [exploration level](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Exploration_Level 'Exploration Level').

    - It also makes it easier to get [Ability Points](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ability_Points 'Ability Points').

## Restrictions

- You cannot change your Character's name.
    - Instead, see [Character Name Change Coupon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character_Name_Change_Coupon 'Character Name Change Coupon').
- You cannot rebirth into another [Race](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Race 'Race').
- You cannot rebirth to an older age.
- If you are under Cumulative Level 5,000, you can rebirth once a day.
- If you are between Cumulative Level 5,000 ~ 7,999, you can rebirth once every two days.
- If you are between Cumulative Level 8,000 ~ 9,999, you can rebirth once every four days.
- If you are above Cumulative Level 10,000, you can rebirth once every six days.
- Rebirthing into a [Hero Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hero_Talent 'Hero Talent') requires 39 Pon.

    - Events such as the [Master Plan Events](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Master_Plan_Events 'Master Plan Events') may make this option free for a limited time.

- If you are above Cumulative Level 5,000 and have [Basic VIP Membership](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Basic_VIP_Membership 'Basic VIP Membership'), you can rebirth one day _faster_ than you would otherwise.

## What Stays

- [Titles](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles 'Titles')
- [Skill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skills 'Skills') ranks, along with the stats and [Combat Power](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combat_Power 'Combat Power') that are associated with them
- [Journal](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Journal 'Journal') achievements and [Collection journal](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Collection_Journal 'Collection Journal') entries
- [Part-Time Job](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Part-Time_Job 'Part-Time Job') information
- [Ability Points](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Ability_Points 'Stats')
- [Keywords](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Keywords 'Keywords') and Quest progress
- [Guild](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Guild 'Guild')
- Equipment, items, [Gold](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gold 'Gold'), [Ducat](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ducat 'Ducat'), and items in the bank
- Your character's birthday
- [Homestead](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Homestead 'Homestead') information

## What Doesn't Stay

- Stats earned from Aging and Leveling Up.
- [Long-term food stat effects](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Food_Effects_(Long_Term)> 'Food Effects (Long Term)').
- Stats and weight gained from eating food, [catering](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Catering 'Catering'), drinking [weight loss potions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Diet_Potion 'Diet Potion'), or resting in the [Hot Springs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hot_Springs 'Hot Springs').
- Your [Shine of Eweca](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shine_of_Eweca 'Shine of Eweca') and race-specific [Celestial Body Skill (Ladeca)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Celestial_Body_Skills 'Category:Celestial Body Skills') ranks, if you choose to reset your level.
- The effects of any [Age Potion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Age_Potion 'Age Potion') or [Tin Potion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tin_Potion 'Tin Potion') if used before Rebirth.

## What You May Change

### Options

- Age, Character Level and Exploration Level.
    - Choosing Ages 11 ~ 17 **does not** give you the extra AP as it does for a completely new character.
    - Resetting your level and exploration level to 1 advances their cumulative totals by 1 (even when resetting from level 1 to level 1).
    - You cannot reset one or the other; it has to be all or neither.
        - See [Forgetful Potion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Forgetful_Potion 'Forgetful Potion') for resetting your current level without rebirthing.
- Gender.
    - All beauty options that are not free will need to be paid for again, even if they are the same.
        - This can be circumvented by using the [Dressing Room's Beauty Slot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dressing_Room#Beauty_Slots 'Dressing Room') feature. The features unavailable to the character's new gender will be grayed out.
- You may choose to keep your current equipment on.
    - Unchecking the equipped equipment box will automatically dress you in the [Newbie Wear](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Newbie_Wears 'Newbie Wears') of your race.

        - Its color can be changed depending on your age.
- Appearance.
    - Pons are required to select Premium Appearances.
        - You cannot choose [Beauty Coupon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beauty_Coupon 'Beauty Coupon') options from the rebirth UI should you wish to change your appearance.

### Keep Equipped Equipment

- Checking this option will let you keep your currently equipped clothes (which will not be pictured in the rebirth process) prior to getting to the rebirth screen.
- Leaving it unchecked will put your current clothes back in your inventory, and you will wear an outfit set dependant on your race once the rebirth process is over:
    - Humans: [Beginner Long Pants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Long_Pants 'Beginner Long Pants') or [Beginner Skirt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Skirt 'Beginner Skirt') and [Leather Shoes (Type 1)](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Leather_Shoes_(Type_1)> 'Leather Shoes (Type 1)')
    - Elves: [Newbie Wear (M)](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Newbie_Wear_(M)> 'Newbie Wear (M)') and [Comfy Ankle Boots](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Comfy_Ankle_Boots 'Comfy Ankle Boots')
    - Giants: Giant Newbie Wear [(M)](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant_Newbie_Wear_(M)> 'Giant Newbie Wear (M)') or [(F)](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant_Newbie_Wear_(F)> 'Giant Newbie Wear (F)') and [Traditional Giant Shoes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Traditional_Giant_Shoes 'Traditional Giant Shoes')
- The color of the clothes can be changed by selecting a different age.

### Rebirth Location

- You may choose to start at your last location or [Iria](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Iria 'Iria').

    - Humans may also rebirth to [Tir Chonaill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Chonaill 'Tir Chonaill').

        - Which NPC you meet with is determined by where you rebirth.
            - You can meet [Tin](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tin 'Tin') if you rebirth in [Tir Chonaill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Chonaill 'Tir Chonaill').
            - Humans can meet [Ffion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ffion 'Ffion'), Giants can meet [Meriel](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Meriel 'Meriel'), and Elves can meet [Vena](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vena 'Vena') if you rebirth in [Iria](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Iria 'Iria').

## How to be Reborn

[![CharacterWindowRebirthMain.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/a/af/CharacterWindowRebirthMain.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:CharacterWindowRebirthMain.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:CharacterWindowRebirthMain.png 'Enlarge')

[![CharacterWindowRebirthInfo.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/6/64/CharacterWindowRebirthInfo.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:CharacterWindowRebirthInfo.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:CharacterWindowRebirthInfo.png 'Enlarge')

You can rebirth in two ways:

1. Choose the 'Rebirth' option on the character selection screen.
2. From in-game, via the character window > additional info tab > Rebirth

You can rebirth as long as the "Availability" in the Additional Info tab of the Character window reads "Available". You can also use a [Rebirth Potion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rebirth_Potion 'Rebirth Potion') to be able to rebirth immediately.

You will enter the [Soul Stream](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Soul_Stream 'Soul Stream'). Talk to [Nao](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Nao 'Nao') to proceed and optionally get more information on the process. Click "Rebirth" when you are done listening to her to be taken to the Rebirth window. From here, proceed on as if you were simply creating a new character.

- If you wish to make any changes to your character, you must check mark the corresponding options on the first page. They are all checked by default.
    - You can go back at any time if desired.
    - You do not need to make any modifications to proceed with rebirthing if you do not wish it, even if the boxes are checked.

The last process of a Rebirth is choosing your [Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talent 'Talent'). You will be asked to confirm whether you want to rebirth into the selected talent. If you chose a Hero talent, a cost of 39 [Pon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pon 'Pon') will be prompted for you to agree on. If you lack the pon, you can return to the first screen and buy pons from there.

- If you rebirth into a Hero Talent, you will be asked to choose a character to [support.](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hero_Support 'Hero Support')

If it is your first Rebirth, you will be given [the Reborn title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles 'Titles') , which gives +6 STR, INT, DEX, Will, and Luck.

## Trivia

- Rebirth, also known as reincarnation, is a real-world religious concept that roughly matches the game's depiction.
    - According to disputed accounts such as Julius Caesar's _Commentarii de Bello Gallico_, Gaulish druids believed in the reincarnation of the soul.
- According to [Marlowe](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Marlowe 'Marlowe'), [Shakespeare](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shakespeare 'Shakespeare') had once rebirthed from a [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human 'Human') to an [Elf](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf 'Elf'), a [Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant 'Giant'), and back, despite that rebirthing between races is not possible.
- Prior to Generation 13, the player was required to wait until the real-time day that they last rebirthed on.
- Prior to the [New Beginnings](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Patches#New_Beginnings 'Patches') update, [Character Cards](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character_Cards 'Character Cards') rebirths were only free when a character reached age 20.
- Rebirthing into a Hero Talent and choosing to rebirth in Tir Chonaill or Iria and talking to one of the tutorial helpers allows you to receive one of ten random [Fixed Color Dye Ampoules](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fixed_Color_Dye_Ampoule 'Fixed Color Dye Ampoule') and a [Nao's Gift Collection Book](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Nao%27s_Gift_Collection "Nao's Gift Collection").

    - Each NPC in the rebirth areas has a different selection of dyes.
    - The ten random dyes are shown one at a time, and asked if you would take that dye. If you had not chosen by the 9th dye, you are forced to take the 10th dye.
    - This may be disabled during events that make Hero Rebirths free.
