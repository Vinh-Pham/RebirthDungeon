## Details

- Passively increases the damage of [Critical Hits](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Critical "Stats").
- Rank F Critical Hit is required for the player to score a critical hit outside of [Counterattack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Counterattack "Counterattack"), [Lance Counter](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lance_Counter "Lance Counter"), and [Combo: Counter Punch](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combo:_Counter_Punch "Combo: Counter Punch").
- Rank 1 Critical Hit is required to complete a quest objective to learn [Chain Burst](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Chain_Burst "Chain Burst").

## Obtaining the Skill

- The Practice Rank of Critical Hit is received upon advancing [Combat Mastery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combat_Mastery "Combat Mastery") or [Ranged Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ranged_Attack "Ranged Attack") to Rank F.

  - In order to advance Critical Hit to rank F, the player must receive a Critical Hit from an opponent or deal a Critical Hit with [Counterattack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Counterattack "Counterattack").

    - Because of the nature of the skill, a player **cannot** deal a Critical Hit before Rank F outside of Counterattack.
    - The [Gray Wolf](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gray_Wolf "Gray Wolf") that spawns around south [Tir Chonaill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Chonaill "Tir Chonaill") are easy, low level enemies that can deal critcal hits to learn from. Mimics in dungeons are another option.

## Training Method

- Receiving a Critical Hit while using the [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defense "Defense") skill or achieving a Critical Hit while playing as a [Summon Golem](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Summon_Golem "Summon Golem") will not count towards training.

### Tips

- Critical Hit can be a daunting skill to advance because it depends on gaining and receiving critical damage, which players cannot directly control. To gain more critical hits, you should fight using weapons with high attack speed and critical rate such as [Dagger](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dagger "Dagger"), [Spiked Knuckle](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spiked_Knuckle "Spiked Knuckle"), or [Bear Knuckle](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bear_Knuckle "Bear Knuckle"). You should also pick mobs with a low [Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Protection "Protection") rate. The Foxes in [Tir Chonaill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Chonaill "Tir Chonaill") or [Raccoons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Raccoon "Raccoon") in [Dugald Aisle](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dugald_Aisle "Dugald Aisle") are obvious choices.
- To receive critical hits, the best method requires reasonable [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Defense.2FProtection "Stats") and a mountable pet. If you can lower your [Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Protection "Protection") by enchant or title, it will also help. Go to [Ciar](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ciar "Ciar") Normal and look around for a [Mimic](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mimic "Mimic") pit. The more Mimics you find in one room, the better this will work.

  - [Mount](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Mounts "Category:Mounts") your pet (if you have one) and touch all of the Mimics. They will not awaken immediately. Unmount your pet and all Mimics will aggro you simultaneously. Let them knock you around for a while and you will gain critical hits fairly often. If the Mimics do only 1 damage, you can semi-afk while this process is going on, coming back occasionally to refill your HP. You may also wish to remove all of your unnecessary equipment to conserve durability.
  - If you do not have a mount, then it can be quite difficult to open all the Mimics as the opened ones will consistently stalk and attack you. Plus, they move a lot faster than [Humans](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human "Human"). Otherwise, try to recruit someone who does.
- In the [Shadow Mission](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shadow_Mission "Shadow Mission"), [Provocation](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Provocation "Provocation"), after you complete the mission, equip your critical hit weapons and attack the [Barrier Spikes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Barrier_Spikes "Barrier Spikes"), leaving it on auto attack. On Basic difficulty, they have no Protection, and the ones in this mission are nearly indestructible. You will continue to score critical blows which count towards training, regardless of whether you have no stamina, or have no weapon durability.
- Alternatively, you may enter [Tir Na Nog](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Na_Nog "Tir Na Nog") to battle against [Zombies](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Zombie "Zombie") using [Flame Burst](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Flame_Burst "Flame Burst"), as it ignores Protection.
- Giants may use [Stomp](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stomp "Stomp") for an area of effect attack and for its ability to ignore Protection rate when calculating Critical Hit Rate.
- [Way of the Gun](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Way_of_the_Gun "Way of the Gun") can be used to achieve critical hits, as it causes [Dual Gun](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dual_Guns_List "Dual Guns List") [Normal Attacks](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Normal_Attack "Normal Attack") to be critical hits for the duration of said skill.
