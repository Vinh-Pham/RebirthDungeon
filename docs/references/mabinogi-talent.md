## Basic Information

- The **talent** system is a [stat](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Status 'Status'), [title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Talent#Talent_Title), and [skill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Skills 'Category:Skills') specialization system.
- You may change talents upon [character creation](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character 'Character') or [rebirth](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rebirth 'Rebirth').

    - Characters that were created before the [Generation 17, Season 1 Update](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Patches#Generation_17.2C_Season_1:_Grandmaster 'Patches') may choose their first talent upon logging in.
    - The talent you select will have an effect on:
        - Which skills receive double skill training and a training exp bonus when applicable
            - Some skills receive skill training benefits from multiple talents.
        - Base stat bonus – Stats gained upon selecting the talent
        - Leveling stat bonus – Stats gained upon leveling up
        - Aging stat bonus – Stats gained upon aging until the age of 20

- Base, leveling, and aging stat bonuses stack with [base character growth stats](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Age#Character_Growth 'Age').
- Talents themselves also provide a stat boost when ranked up. These stats are permanent regardless of which talent is selected.
    - These stats do not contribute towards [Combat Power](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combat_Power 'Combat Power').

## Life-based Talents

_For Gathering Bonus Experience, see [Gathering](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gathering 'Gathering')._

- All Life skill-based Talents receive:
    - 2x Production Experience (Experience points gained when successfully producing or gathering an item).
    - Experience for [Gathering](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gathering 'Gathering').
    - +10% [Part-Time Job](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Part-Time_Job 'Part-Time Job') completion reward.
    - Information regarding the [Smuggler](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Smuggler 'Smuggler')'s location and the Trade Good will scroll across the top of the screen for [Commerce](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Commerce 'Commerce').

        - When the Smuggler appears, the commerce post displays how much he is willing to pay for an item.
- The Mercantile Talent can randomly receive a [Special Quest](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Part-Time_Jobs#Life-based_Talents_Quest 'Part-Time Jobs') to obtain pieces of the [Belmont Set](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Belmont_Set 'Belmont Set').
- Some Talents can craft Talent-only items:
    - [Blacksmiths](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Talent#Blacksmith) will have the ability to use Talent-only [Blueprints](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blueprint 'Blueprint').
    - [Tailors](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Talent#Sewing) will have the ability to use Talent-only [Sewing Patterns](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Sewing_Pattern 'Sewing Pattern').
    - [Carpenters](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Talent#Carpentry) will have the ability to craft Talent-only weapons through Carpentry.
- All items created through [Blacksmithing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blacksmithing 'Blacksmithing'), [Carpentry](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Carpentry 'Carpentry'), and [Tailoring](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tailoring 'Tailoring') will receive a slight stat boost when in specific talents.

    - Shields will receive an additional 1 Defense.
    - Armor will receive an additional 1 Protection.
        - **Warning**: Light Armors crafted through Tailoring require the Blacksmith Talent (and similarly, its Grandmaster).
    - Clothing will receive an additional 1 Defense.
    - Helmets, gauntlets, and boots will receive an additional 1 Durability.
    - Weapons will receive an additional 1 Minimum Damage and 2 Maximum Damage.

## Talent Levels

- Talents start at level 1 and max out at level 16.
- Talents level similarly to skills (where level 1 is rank F and level 15 is rank 1) but do not require [AP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Ability_Points 'Stats') and are based on the ranks of the skills that talent specializes in.
- Each skill gives a different amount of talent experience based on the player's race and the skill's rank. The accumulation of a certain amount of experience in a talent causes the talent to rank.
    - [Deranking](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skill_Reset_Capsule 'Skill Reset Capsule') skills will cause one to lose talent experience and level if the accumulated experience goes below the acquired amount.
    - Perfect training or mastering a skill does not provide additional talent experience.
- Level 16 (Grandmaster) cannot be reached through talent experience. Instead, the player must reach level 15 (Master) and then take several tests from [Lezarro](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lezarro 'Lezarro').

    - For more information on Grandmaster status, see [Grandmaster](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Grandmaster 'Grandmaster').
    - Lowering Talent level during or after the Grandmaster tests will cause the player to lose their Grandmaster standing until they re-level the Talent.

- Talent levels affect what difficulties can be selected for non- [Generation](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mainstream_Quests 'Mainstream Quests') [Shadow Missions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shadow_Mission 'Shadow Mission') and [Theatre Missions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Theatre_Mission 'Theatre Mission').

    - Basic: One level 1 (Fledgling) talent
    - Intermediate: One level 5 (Naive) talent
    - Advanced: One level 7 (Senior) talent
    - Hard: One level 11 (Expert) talent
    - Elite: One level 1 (Fledgling) talent, requires an elite pass from the party leader
    - Lord: One level 15 (Master) talent, requires a [Lord Pass](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lord_Mission_Pass_(All)> 'Lord Mission Pass (All)') from _each party member_

- A level 11 (Expert) talent is required to enter [Abyss Dungeons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Abyss_Dungeons 'Category:Abyss Dungeons').
- A level 15 (Master) talent is required to wield its affiliated [Demonic Weapon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Demonic_Equipment 'Category:Demonic Equipment') (e.g. - [Demonic Hellfire Cylinder](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Demonic_Hellfire_Cylinder 'Demonic Hellfire Cylinder') requires Level 15 [Battle Alchemy Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Talent#Battle_Alchemy)).
- Level 15 active [Tailoring](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Talent#Tailoring), [Smithing](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Talent#Smithing), and [Carpentry](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Talent#Carpentry) talents are required to make [Demonic Scales](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Demonic_Scale 'Demonic Scale'), [Demonic Ingots](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Demonic_Ingot 'Demonic Ingot'), and [Demonic Wood Boards](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Demonic_Wood_Board 'Demonic Wood Board'), respectively.
- There are [journal](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Journal 'Journal') entries for reaching level 2 (Novice), level 5 (Naive), level 8 (Advanced), and level 11 (Expert) for each talent, which reward [AP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/AP 'AP').

    - In order of the above levels, 30, 40, 60, and 100 AP is rewarded.

## Beginner's Talent

- A newly created character receives additional benefits upon selecting their first talent.
    - A newly created character cannot choose the [Adventurer](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Talent#Adventurer) talent.
- A [beginner title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles#Beginner_Titles 'Titles') respective to the talent is given upon character creation.

    - Choosing an Ace talent provides all of the beginner titles of the related Milletian talents.
    - The title will be removed at cumulative level of 100.

- Beginner equipment respective to the talent is given upon character creation.
    - Choosing an Ace talent provides all of the beginner equipment of the related Milletian talents.
- Specially-enchanted [equipment](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Equipment 'Category:Equipment') and Exclusive Enchant Scrolls can be earned at cumulative levels of 10, 20, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, and 100, via quests.

## Trivia

- The implementation of the Talent system replaced the [Destiny](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Destiny_(System)> 'Destiny (System)') system entirely, although some traits of the Destiny system still lie in the Talent system.
- Some NPCs and monsters, such as those in the [Grandmaster Mission](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Grandmaster_Mission 'Grandmaster Mission'), possess talent titles that are currently unavailable to players.
