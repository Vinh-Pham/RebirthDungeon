# Placeholder art

- Tiny Dungeon by Kenney: https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon
- Tiny Town by Kenney: https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town

Downloaded 2026-09-20. Both packs are CC0 1.0; their original licenses accompany the files. Original individual 16×16 tiles are used without modification. Tile filenames retain source IDs; town tiles have a `town_` prefix.

## Fonts

The regular and bold Roboto fonts are supplied by the pinned Druid dependency, under the Apache 2.0 license included in `fonts/LICENSE-Roboto.txt`. The local `.font` resources configure rendering and character coverage; they do not alter the font files.

## Audio

`assets/audio/*.wav` contains original synthesized placeholder tones composed for this project. There is no sampled third-party music or sound effect. Town/dungeon loops and click/hit/victory cues are controlled independently through Music and Sound Effects settings.
