## Details

- Creates various [Potions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Potion 'Potion') through [Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Herb 'Herb'). For a list of items created through Potion Making, see [Potion Making List](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Potion_Making_List 'Potion Making List').
- The player must have a [Potion Concoction Kit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Potion_Concoction_Kit 'Potion Concoction Kit') equipped in order to use this skill.
- Unlike many [Life Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Life_Skills 'Life Skills'), Potion Making is not affected by [Dexterity](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dexterity 'Dexterity').
- Potion Making uses 2.5 [Stamina](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stamina 'Stamina'), but cannot be used when Stamina is below 3. [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana 'Mana') is drained when making more advanced potions such as Wound Recovery or 100-Point Potions.

    - Mana use scales accordingly with how many potions are being made. Potion making will stop if there is insufficient mana to make potions.

- It is possible to make more than one potion at once, however disproportionate amounts will result in producing only enough potions for which you have all the ingredients for. (Ex: 5 Mana Herbs, 2 Base Herbs, and 3 Base Potions will only produce two MP 100 Potions if successful.)
- Potion making provides one of the two ways to repair [Classic Spirit Weapons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Classic_Spirit_Weapons#Spirit_Weapon_Repair 'Classic Spirit Weapons').
- An additional 1~5 potions will be created if the player has the Grandmaster Apothecary's Unique Effect activated.
    - The EXP yield will only be for 1 potion.
- Rank 9 Potion Making is required to use the [Fateweaver](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fateweaver 'Fateweaver') [Technique](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Technique 'Technique').
- Starting at Dan 1, the player will be able to create RE potions.

## Obtaining the Skill

- Learned automatically upon equipping a [Potion Concoction Kit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Potion_Concoction_Kit 'Potion Concoction Kit').

Or,

- Complete the quest [The Basics of Medicine](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Talent_Quests#The_Basics_of_Medicine 'Beginner Talent Quests').

## Training Method

### Tips

- It is advised to use [Fragmentation](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fragmentation 'Fragmentation') to retain the herbs.
- Ranks D to 6 can be effectively trained by making [Wound Remedy 10 Potions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wound_Remedy_10_Potion 'Wound Remedy 10 Potion'). They fulfill the Gold, Bloody and Mana herb ingredient requirements all at once.
- It is highly recommended that one stands next to a [Mana Tunnel](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Tunnel 'Mana Tunnel') when attempting to make many potions in one sitting. This provides accelerated Mana regeneration.

    - It is also recommended to use [Meditation](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Meditation 'Meditation') for a fast MP recovery. A high rank Meditation will almost prevent Potion Making from decreasing your MP. However keep in mind that [Stamina](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stamina 'Stamina') regeneration is halted when Meditation is left on.
    - Playing [Enduring Melody](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enduring_Melody 'Enduring Melody') for a little while after each set of potions will both regenerate stamina, even while meditating, and further augment mana regeneration.

        - Playing a high-ranked [Fantastic Chorus](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fantastic_Chorus 'Fantastic Chorus') can regenerate Mana incredibly quickly.

- One can also use [Demigod](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Demigod 'Demigod') to produce unlimited mana for potions for up to 4 minutes at rank 1 [Awakening of Light](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Awakening_of_Light 'Awakening of Light').
- Certain rare herbs can be obtained through simpler means:
    - [Poison Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Poison_Herb 'Poison Herb'), [Antidote Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Antidote_Herb 'Antidote Herb'), [Mandrakes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mandrake 'Mandrake') and [Gold Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Gold_Herb 'Gold Herb') can all be bought from the Rafting Reward Shop.

        - Poison Herbs are also obtainable through [Shyllien Ecology](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shyllien_Ecology 'Shyllien Ecology') and [Synthesis](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Synthesis 'Synthesis').
        - Mandrakes can also be acquired through Synthesis.
        - Gold Herbs, Mandrakes, White Herbs, and Poison Herbs are also available to build on [Homesteads](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Homestead 'Homestead').

    - [White Herbs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/White_Herb 'White Herb') can be acquired by killing [Polar Hornets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Polar_Hornet 'Polar Hornet') in [Silva Forest](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Silva_Forest 'Silva Forest') of [Physis](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Physis 'Physis') and picking the patches that spawn when they die.
