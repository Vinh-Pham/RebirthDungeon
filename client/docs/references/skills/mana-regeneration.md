## Details

- Requires a [Wand](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wands_List 'Wands List') or [Staff](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Staves_List 'Staves List') equipped in order to use.
- Channels for two seconds to restore a portion of the user's [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana 'Mana').

    - Mana restoration is based on a percentage of the user's maximum mana.
    - Inspiration will be cancelled if the user loads another skill or gets attacked.

- The user is unable to move while using Inspiration.
- This skill enters cooldown immediately upon use.

## Obtaining the Skill

- Talk to [Stewart](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stewart 'Stewart') with the [Skills Keyword](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skills_Keyword 'Skills Keyword') with Rank E [Meditation](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Meditation 'Meditation') learned.

    - To obtain Rank E Meditation quickly, obtain [Icebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Icebolt 'Icebolt'), [Firebolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Firebolt 'Firebolt'), and [Lightning Bolt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lightning_Bolt 'Lightning Bolt') then speak to Stewart with the skills keyword to obtain Meditation at Rank F. You can buy the F -> E skill book directly from him.

## Training Method

### Tips

- Players can burn their [Mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana 'Mana') faster to meet the "Use the skill at 10% or less Mana" requirement by doing any of the following:

    - Using [Harmonic Saint](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Harmonic_Saint 'Harmonic Saint') skills

        - Among those, [Hand of Restoration](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hand_of_Restoration 'Hand of Restoration') and [Hymn of Resurrection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hymn_of_Resurrection 'Hymn of Resurrection') take 50 and 200 MP respectively for each load and have no charging time. They can thus can be spammed in quick succession without even using the skill.

    - While [Mana Shield](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Shield 'Mana Shield') is active, receiving a powerful blow.

        - For instance, like [Smash](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Smash 'Smash') in [PvP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/PvP 'PvP') and/or [EvG](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/EvG 'EvG').
        - The [Mutant Maneating Plants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mutant_Maneating_Plant 'Mutant Maneating Plant')'s Fireball skill is especially strong if you have a lot of MP.
        - Certain monsters, such as [Apostles](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Apostle 'Apostle'), can quickly empty Mana with Mana Shield up.

    - Under the [Respite](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Respite 'Respite') Aftereffect, casting magic.
    - Casting and standing idle with [Meteor Strike](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Meteor_Strike 'Meteor Strike').

- Training is given as soon as the skill is activated, so cancelling it immediately afterwards can keep your mana low.
- Players should consider using AP training, since the AP cost remains quite low even at high ranks.
