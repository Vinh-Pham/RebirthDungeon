## Details

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/c/ce/Meteor_Strike_Meteor.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Meteor_Strike_Meteor.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Meteor_Strike_Meteor.png 'Enlarge')

The Meteor, moments before colliding the ground.

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/7/75/Meteor_Strike_Fire.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Meteor_Strike_Fire.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Meteor_Strike_Fire.png 'Enlarge')

Fire leftover from impact.

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/6/6e/Meteor_Strike_Start-Up.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Meteor_Strike_Start-Up.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Meteor_Strike_Start-Up.png 'Enlarge')

Meteor Strike on start-up.

- Requires a [Staff](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Staves_List 'Staves List') to use.
- Summons a large meteor over a designated area, creating an explosion as it crashes into the ground. The meteor leaves flames in its wake, damaging enemies that walk over it.
    - Enemies can run out of the fire to avoid damage so long as they stay out of it.
    - Meteor Strike requires a targetable enemy in order to use.
    - Meteor Strike can be cast over [props](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Props 'Props') and physical obstacles.

        - An exception is [Rabbie Phantasm Dungeon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rabbie_Phantasm_Dungeon 'Rabbie Phantasm Dungeon') and [Crom Bas](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crom_Bas 'Crom Bas'), which blocks players from casting it through walls and cages.
- When casting, a magical circle will appear beneath the user. When it is done loading, a red targeting circle will appear on the ground beneath the mouse cursor, allowing the user to designate the location.
    - To target a specific monster directly, simply Left Alt + Left Click or use Ctrl Targeting and target the desired monster.
        - Doing so will aim for the center of its hitbox in its last position.
- After launching the spell, the sky will temporarily become cloudy until the meteor lands. A white flash will also occur when the meteor lands, affecting every player within the area regardless of their distance from the meteor.
- The skill will continue to drain the user's [mana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana 'Mana') after loading until the meteor has been launched.
- Ignores [Magic Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Protection 'Magic Protection') when calculating critical rate.
- The skill damages enemies through 2 means:

1. Physical and magical damage through the impact.
    - Both the physical and magical damage through impact are calculated and displayed as one value.
    - Impact damage ignores [Heavy Stander](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Heavy_Stander 'Heavy Stander') and [Mana Deflector](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mana_Deflector 'Mana Deflector'), but not [Invulnerability](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Invulnerability 'Invulnerability').
    - Impact damage is affected by [Magic Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Attack 'Magic Attack').
2. Ranged damage through the fire.
    - Residual damage can lower a target's HP past 0 and can kill.
    - Like other damage over time effects, residual damage bypasses Invulnerability.
    - Residual damage is affected by the target's [Natural Shield](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Natural_Shield_(Monster)> 'Natural Shield (Monster)').
    - Residual damage is based on the skill's rank and cannot be increased.
    - Residual damage cannot score [criticals](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical 'Critical').
    - The flames linger around for 10 seconds, dealing damage every 2 seconds for a total of 5 ticks.

- Base Casting Time is 2 seconds. Meteor Strike will land once cast after 2 seconds.
    - Non-player Meteor Strikes have a Casting Time of 4 seconds and Travel Time of 5 seconds instead.
- Can not be used while [Rafting](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rafting 'Rafting') or [Hot-Air Ballooning](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hot-Air_Ballooning 'Hot-Air Ballooning').
- Enabling [Mini Effects](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Options_Dialog#Performance 'Options Dialog') causes the casting effects, the meteor, the explosion, and the leftover flames to become invisible.
- Rank 1 Meteor Strike is required to use the Time Shift [Technique](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Technique 'Technique').

## Obtaining the Skill

- Create or [rebirth](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rebirth 'Rebirth') a character and select the [Druid Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talent#Hero_Talents 'Talent') to be the active talent.

    - Requires 39 [Pon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pon 'Pon')

Or,

- Complete the \[Ultimate Fire\] quest.

## Training Method

### Tips

- For early ranks, you can easily group monsters with [Act 6: Crisis](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Act_6:_Crisis 'Act 6: Crisis') and then cast Meteor Strike, effectively training multiple enemy hits. This is best done in a party with the finish rule set to Anyone.

    - Another player may be required to assist with this method.

- Having another player use [Enthralling Performance](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Enthralling_Performance 'Enthralling Performance') will allow multiple monsters group up in a single area, allowing for easier training with multiple hits.
- Getting a [Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant 'Giant') player use [Taunt](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Taunt 'Taunt') and [Wind Guard](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wind_Guard 'Wind Guard') to group many monsters around them, letting you hit them all with Meteor easily.
- Areas of interest for large amount of spawns may include:
    - [Brown Foxes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Brown_Fox 'Brown Fox') at northern [Tir Chonaill](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Tir_Chonaill 'Tir Chonaill'), on the way to Alby Dungeon.
    - The lobby area and the middle rooms of [Sulfur Spiders of the Shadow Realm](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Sulfur_Spiders_of_the_Shadow_Realm 'Sulfur Spiders of the Shadow Realm').

        - Summoning area of effect summon [pets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pets 'Pets') and/or using [Lullaby](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lullaby 'Lullaby') beforehand will help keep the spiders in place as the player casts Meteor Strike.

    - [The Other Alchemists](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/The_Other_Alchemists 'The Other Alchemists')' middle room.
    - [Fennec Foxes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Fennec_Fox 'Fennec Fox') outside [Filia](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Filia 'Filia')'s southeastern Outskirts.
    - [Their Method](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Their_Method 'Their Method')'s boss room (Will not work for getting more than 12 mobs hit at once)

        - However, it is possible to create [Barrier Spikes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Barrier_Spikes 'Barrier Spikes') in order to fulfill the condition, granted the player has high enough a rank.

    - [Raccoons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Raccoon 'Raccoon') north of the Logging Camp in [Dugald Aisle](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dugald_Aisle 'Dugald Aisle').
    - [Shyllien Nature Reserve](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shyllien_Nature_Reserve 'Shyllien Nature Reserve') Outskirts 2 in front of the giant crystal deposit.
    - [Hillwen Mine](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hillwen_Mine 'Hillwen Mine')B3 in front of the giant ore deposit.

        - Keep in mind that in both the Hillwen Mine and Shyllien Nature Reserve have extremely powerful mobs and may overwhelm you if precautions are not taken.

    - [Polar Hornets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Polar_Hornet 'Polar Hornet') in Silva Forest, south of [Vales](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Vales 'Vales').

        - [Flame Burst](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Flame_Burst 'Flame Burst') works well to lock the hornets' aggro and gather a large number into one location.

    - [Offering](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Offering 'Offering')
        - Beware that after the 3rd wave spawns the mission will automatically fail on next spawn.
- For damage requirements, it may require targeting multiple weaker monsters at once while simultaneously landing a critical hit.

## Trivia

- Using Meteor Strike with the [Halloween Party Prefix Title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Titles#Event_Prefix_Titles 'Titles') changes the meteor's appearance into a giant [Trick Pumpkin](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Trick_Pumpkin 'Trick Pumpkin').
- Meteor Strike requires the **most** [AP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/AP 'AP') out of all skills within the game; 31 more than [Crash Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crash_Shot 'Crash Shot').
- Meteor Strike's base cooldown used to be _30 minutes,_ until it was shortened to _15 minutes_ with the [Renovation Patch](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Patches#Renovation 'Patches') in 2015.

    - The [Demolition Deepglow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Demolition_Deepglow_Staff 'Demolition Deepglow Staff') and [Lord of Ruination](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Lord_of_Ruination_Staff 'Lord of Ruination Staff') Staves' effects further decrease the cooldown of Meteor Strike by _250 seconds_ and _400 seconds_ respectively. This makes shortest possible cooldown for Meteor Strike _8 minutes and 20 seconds (500 seconds)_ if one doesn't count Time Shift and Bachram Boost.
