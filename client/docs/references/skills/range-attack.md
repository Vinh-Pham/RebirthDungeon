## Details

- Requires a [Bow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Bows 'Category:Bows') or [Crossbow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Crossbows 'Category:Crossbows') and the respective [Missile](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Missiles_List 'Missiles List') to use.
- Clicking once on a monster will load the skill and start the aiming meter. A second click will fire the missile.
    - You must have Automatic Combat off and [Reserved Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Options#Control 'Options') on 'Once' or 'Not Used' to "hold" the missile after it is fully aimed.
- The Aiming meter goes up to 100%.
    - If the opponent is standing still, max aim meter is 100%.
        - Aim Rate can be modified to go up to 120% while using a [Sharp Aiming](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magical_Music#Sharp_Aiming 'Magical Music') magically Composed music score.
    - If the opponent is walking, max aim meter is 95%.
    - If the opponent is running, max aim meter is 90%.
    - Occasionally an enemy may be just outside the player's range despite the game showing them as in range, causing your attacks to miss.
    - Firing at any aiming value less than 100% will reduce the player's damage according to the aiming meter's value.
        - 50% Aim Rate results in 50% damage. 120% Aim Rate results in 120% damage.
    - Aiming speed multiplier increases with higher rank.
    - Aiming at 70% causes a visual flare and a bonuses to aim rate will take effect, regardless of aiming speed.
        - This only applies to players.
- One shot will stun the opponent, but will not knock them back.
    - Consecutive shots can push-back.
        - The number of hits required to push an enemy back depends on how quickly the player fires as well as the bow used.
    - Striking an enemy who is being pushed back will bounce them in place.
        - Striking an enemy during this will cause them to stand up, removing any stun or knockback.
- Each shot uses up one missile.
- Stunning with this skill triggers most monsters to attempt to use a basic attack on the player.
    - Critical hits will reset the monster's AI instead.
    - This is dependent on the Monster's AI.
- Elves have their own version of [Ranged Attack](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf_Ranged_Attack 'Elf Ranged Attack').
- Ranged Attack and all Archery fire can be dodged by [Evasion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Evasion 'Evasion').
- The skill's Aim Speed Bonus does not affect [Magnum Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magnum_Shot 'Magnum Shot'), nor [Crash Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crash_Shot 'Crash Shot').
- See [Ranged Based Tactics](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Solo_Battle_Tactics#Ranged_Based 'Solo Battle Tactics'), [Ranged/Melee Based Tactics](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Solo_Battle_Tactics#Ranged.2FMelee_Based 'Solo Battle Tactics'), [Team Tactics for Ranged Party](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Team_Battle_Tactics#Ranged_Based 'Team Battle Tactics'), [Team Tactics for Magic/Ranged Party](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Team_Battle_Tactics#Magic.2FRanged_Based 'Team Battle Tactics'), [Team Tactics for Melee/Ranged Party](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Team_Battle_Tactics#Melee.2FRanged_Based 'Team Battle Tactics'), for strategies to use in battle.
- Prerequisite for learning [Crash Shot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crash_Shot 'Crash Shot') (at rank 9).
- Some enemies in the field will automatically aggro and attack you if you aim at them with any archery skill, even before shooting.

## Obtaining the Skill

- Complete the quest [The Basics of Archery](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner_Talent_Quests#The_Basics_of_Archery 'Beginner Talent Quests') as a [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human 'Human').

Or,

- Equip a [Bow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Bows 'Category:Bows') or [Crossbow](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Crossbows 'Category:Crossbows') as a Human.
