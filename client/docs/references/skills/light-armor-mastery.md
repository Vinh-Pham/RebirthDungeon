## Details

- Passively increases the user's [Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Defense.2FProtection 'Stats'), [Magic Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Defense 'Magic Defense'), [Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Protection 'Protection'), and [Magic Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Protection 'Magic Protection') while wearing [Light Armor](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Light_Armor_List 'Light Armor List').

    - These changes do not show on the armor.

## Obtaining the Skill

- Equip any [Light Armor](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Light_Armor 'Light Armor').

#### Other Methods

- Complete the quest [Close Combat Basics](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book#Third_Memoir 'Memory Book') in the Third [Memoir](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memory_Book 'Memory Book'). The skill will have training requirements up to **Rank 3** completed.

## Training Method

- Training counts will also be given if the player's [Pierrot Marionette](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pierrot_Marionette 'Pierrot Marionette') or [Colossus Marionette](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Colossus_Marionette 'Colossus Marionette') is hit while the player's character is wearing light armor.

### Tips

- At higher levels, Light Armor Mastery requires you to be hit by specific types of attacks.
    - [Melee](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Melee 'Melee') attacks as well as [Critical Hits](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Critical_Hit 'Critical Hit') are exceedingly easy to build up if you have a [Mount](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Mounts 'Category:Mounts'). Go to any low level dungeon like [Alby](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Alby_Dungeon 'Alby Dungeon') or [Ciar](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ciar_Dungeon 'Ciar Dungeon') and look around for a large [Mimic](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mimic 'Mimic') pit. Get on your mount and touch all of the Mimics. They will not awaken immediately. Once you un-mount your pet, the Mimics will simultaneously awaken and aggro you together. Activating [Final Hit](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Final_Hit 'Final Hit') ( [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human 'Human')-only), [Wind Guard](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wind_Guard 'Wind Guard') ( [Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant 'Giant')-only), or [Berserk](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Berserk 'Berserk') (Human/Giant) after aggroing all the mobs will help rack up points faster thanks to their knockdown resistance.

        - Alternatively, go into the [Shadow Mission](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shadow_Mission 'Shadow Mission'), [Sulfur Spiders of the Shadow Realm](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Sulfur_Spiders_of_the_Shadow_Realm 'Sulfur Spiders of the Shadow Realm'), and make your way to the center room. Eliminate the [Corrupt Alchemists](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Corrupt_Alchemist 'Corrupt Alchemist') and [Stone Golems](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stone_Golem 'Stone Golem') that spawn, then move to a corner and allow the [Sulfur Spiders](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Sulfur_Spider 'Sulfur Spider') that spawn to attack you constantly.

    - For [Ranged Attacks](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ranged_Attack 'Ranged Attack'), it's the same, but look for an orb room of [Goblin Archers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Goblin_Archer 'Goblin Archer') instead. Otherwise, you may look for [Shadow Archers](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shadow_Archer 'Shadow Archer') in [Defeat the Shadow Warrior](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Defeat_the_Shadow_Warrior 'Defeat the Shadow Warrior') (Basic), as they do low damage.
    - For [Magic](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic 'Magic') attacks, you may want to go to [Ceo Island](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ceo_Island 'Ceo Island') or [Coill Dungeon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Coill_Dungeon 'Coill Dungeon') and attract one of the elemental [Sprites](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Sprite 'Sprite'). Otherwise, [Rafting](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rafting 'Rafting') may be another option and allow the [Hobgoblin Exorcists](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hobgoblin_Exorcist 'Hobgoblin Exorcist') to strike you with their bolts. Another alternative would be [Shyllien Nature Reserve](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shyllien_Nature_Reserve 'Shyllien Nature Reserve')'s Outskirts District 1 with many [Mutant Carnivorous Plants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mutant_Carnivorous_Plant 'Mutant Carnivorous Plant') close to one another and deal rather low bolt damage. [Wyverns](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Wyvern 'Wyvern') are also a viable method as a mass of them tend to attack at a highly frequent rate, though the damage they deal might be too much for players with less defense.

        - It is suggested to have high [Magic Defense](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Defense 'Magic Defense'), [Magic Protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Magic_Protection 'Magic Protection'), and/or [elemental protection](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elements 'Elements') (such as equipment with Magic Resistance or Elementals) in order to maintain low damage.
- [Bandits](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Bandit 'Bandit') from [Commerce](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Commerce 'Commerce') is another option, as the enemies use varieties of attacks (Imps use Magic, Goblin Archers use Ranged, and Humans, Goblins, Giants and Ogres use Melee). It would be wise to locate the Rookie types as they deal the lowest damage of all the Bandits.
- Alternatively, for all of these, use [Dorren's Request](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dorren%27s_Request "Dorren's Request") as you can simply hit the orbs you need as required.
- One can gain all the required points for rank-up by heading to Ceo Island and get knocked around by the Sprites, fulfilling melee, magic, and possibly critical hit training methods.
