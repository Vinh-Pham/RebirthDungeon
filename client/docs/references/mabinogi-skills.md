## Description

- A **Skill** is an active or passive ability of a character or NPC in Mabinogi.

    - There are a max of 16~19 [Ranks](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rank 'Rank') for each skill. Some skills have fewer ranks implemented.
    - The ranks are labeled and ordered Novice, F, E, D, C, B, A, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, Dan 1, Dan 2, Dan 3. Novice is the lowest rank and Dan 3 is the highest rank.
        - The [Advancement Test Hall](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Advancement_Test_Hall 'Advancement Test Hall') allows some skills to be advanced to Dan Ranks.

- Active Skills may have loading times (the time it takes to be able to use the skill) or cooldowns (the time it takes to be able to use the skill _again_).
- Skills require [Ability Points](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ability_Points 'Ability Points') (AP) to rank them up and become stronger.

### Ranking Skills

- In order to rank up a Skill, a minimum of 100 Skill Experience Points must be earned for that rank's "Training Requirements".
    - Training Requirements differ between skills. Sometimes they require [Books](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Books 'Books') or [Quests](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Quests 'Quests'). Other times they require the Skill to be used in specific ways.
- When 100 Skill Experience Points or more have been earned, an "Advance" button appears next to the Skill
    - Ranking up a Skill not only improves the Skill itself, but can also provide permanent [Stats](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats 'Stats') or progress towards mastery in a [Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talent 'Talent').
    - Ranking up a Skill provides a small amount of Character EXP on rank up.
    - Skills can be set to automatically rank up as long as the Character has enough Ability Points.
- If the Skill is Rank 1, a [Master Title](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Master_Title 'Master Title') for that Skill can be obtained by reaching 100 Skill Experience Points instead.

    - In order to rank up a Skill into [Dan](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dan 'Dan'), at least 100 Skill Experience Points is required as well.

### Training Skills

- The most straight forward way to earn Skill Experience Points is to complete the Training Requirements in the Count specified by the Skill.
    - For example, [Cooking](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Cooking 'Cooking') Rank 4 requires the player to cook pies 10 times for a total of 5 Skill Experience Points. Cooking Rank 4 has a total of 100 Skill Experience Points and this represents 5/100 Points.
- Perfectly meeting the Training Requirements of a Skill increases the Character EXP rewarded for ranking the skill.
    - It is not recommended to perfectly train a Skill, as the Character EXP is very small.
- While it is possible to faithfully follow the Training Requirements of a Skill, players may lessen the burden of training Skills by using two modifiers available to them.

#### Skill Count EXP

- Skill Count EXP Modifiers multiplies the _progress_ a player makes while completing Training Requirements.

    - Using the above Cooking Rank 4 example and a x2 Modifier, the player could cook pies 5 times for the whole total of 5 Skill Experience Points. The player would have all 10/10 Counts of cooking pies and 5/100 of Cooking Rank 4's total Experience Points.
    - **Note:** The maximum for this value is hard capped at x8 Training.

- Being currently [Born](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rebirth 'Rebirth') into a [Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talent 'Talent') provides a x2 Bonus to Skills in that Talent.
- The Passive of [Flying Food Truck](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Flying_Food_Truck 'Flying Food Truck') Pets provides a x2 Bonus to the [Cooking](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Cooking 'Cooking') Skill.
- Various items provide this stacking effect; they include the Skill EXP Potions (i.e. [Combat Skill 2x EXP Potion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Combat_Skill_2x_EXP_Potion 'Combat Skill 2x EXP Potion')), [Complete Skill EXP Potions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Complete_Skill_EXP_Potion 'Complete Skill EXP Potion'), [Royal Complete Skill EXP Potions](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Royal_Complete_Skill_EXP_Potion_(1_Day)> 'Royal Complete Skill EXP Potion (1 Day)'), Astrology Balloons (i.e. [Virgo Balloon (30 days)](<https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Virgo_Balloon_(30_days)> 'Virgo Balloon (30 days)')), and Astrology Potions (i.e. [Virgo Starbright Potion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Virgo_Starbright_Potion 'Virgo Starbright Potion')).
- Various events and titles provide this stacking effect.

#### Skill Total Count EXP

- Skill Total Count EXP Modifiers multiplies the _total Count_ of a Training Requirement.

    - Using the above Cooking Rank 4 example and a x2 Modifier, the player could cook pies 10 times for a total of 10 Skill Experience Points. The player would have 10/10 Counts of cooking pies _but_ 10/105 of Cooking Rank 4's total Experience Points.

- [Reforges](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Reforge#Training_EXP_Reforges 'Reforge') can provide this stacking effect.
- [Workshop Benches](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Housing_Workshop 'Category:Housing Workshop') located in [Homestead Houses](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Homestead_Houses 'Homestead Houses') can provide this stacking effect.
- [Hero Badges](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Accessories_List#Hero_Badges 'Accessories List') earned by selecting Ace Talents at the first Rebirth can provide this stacking effect.
- The Cooking Training EXP [Status Effect](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Status_Effect 'Status Effect') from granted through [Equipment Combination Effects](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Equipment_Combination_Effects 'Equipment Combination Effects') provides a x5 Bonus to the [Cooking](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Cooking 'Cooking') Skill.

    - The Combination of the [Antique Cooking Knife](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Antique_Cooking_Knife 'Antique Cooking Knife') and [Table](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Antique_Cooking_Table 'Antique Cooking Table') or the [Antique Cooking Pot](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Antique_Cooking_Pot 'Antique Cooking Pot') and [Ladle](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Antique_Ladle 'Antique Ladle') can provide this stacking effect.

- The [Whisper of Tuan](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Whisper_of_Tuan 'Whisper of Tuan') Passive Skill of [Spirit of Tuan](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spirit_of_Tuan 'Spirit of Tuan') Pets provides a x2 Bonus to the Music Talent.
- The [Blooming Nostalgia](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Blooming_Nostalgia 'Blooming Nostalgia') Passive Skill of [Catsidhe](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Catsidhe 'Catsidhe') Pets provides a x2 Bonus to a selected Combat or Life Talent.

### Avoiding Training

- There are a few ways to avoid completing Training Requirements.
    - For most skills that have over 30 points of Skill Experience Points, you may train the skill with [AP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/AP_Training 'AP Training') by clicking the AP Train button (see below).

        - This functions similar to a Skill Count EXP Modifier.

    - For most skills that have over 30 points of Skill Experience Points _in a specific Training Requirement_, you may use the empowered [Brionac](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Brionac 'Brionac')'s AP Training to train the skill at the cost of some of the Brionac's durability.

        - This functions similar to a Skill Count EXP Modifier.

    - Using [Skill Training Seals](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skill_Training_Seal 'Skill Training Seal').

        - This functions similar to a Skill Total Count EXP Modifier.

### AP Training

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/5/57/AP_Training_Buttons.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:AP_Training_Buttons.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:AP_Training_Buttons.png 'Enlarge')

Handicraft can be AP trained. Refining cannot because it is Rank 1. Blacksmithing can, but does not have 30 training points yet.

[![](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/c/c4/AP_Training_Selection.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:AP_Training_Selection.png)

[Enlarge](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:AP_Training_Selection.png 'Enlarge')

The AP Training interface, allowing selection of a method to train.

**AP Training** is the ability to complete training requirements for [Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skills 'Skills') by spending [AP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/AP 'AP') instead of training them normally.

- To AP train a skill, the following requirements must be met:
    - The selected skill has at least 30 points of training.
        - These points could be granted from [Skill Training Seals](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Skill_Training_Seal 'Skill Training Seal').
    - The skill must not be Rank 1.
    - The method to be trained must have at least 11 maximum counts.
    - The method cannot be in an "Unknown Training Method" state.
- 20% of the total counts will be added to the chosen method.
    - If this would add more than 30 Skill Experience Points, it will instead stop at adding enough counts to add 30 Points.
- **AP used for training skills will not be refunded from a skill reset.**
- The AP cost can start as low as 1 AP, but gradually increases the more points are given and/or the higher the skill rank.
    - [Production Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Production_Skills 'Category:Production Skills') use about x10 more AP to train.
- Can be used on all skills, except for the following:
    - [All Transformation Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Transformations 'Transformations')
    - [Celestial Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Celestial_Body_Skills 'Category:Celestial Body Skills')
    - [Spirit Weapon Awakening](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Spirit_Weapon_Awakening 'Spirit Weapon Awakening')
    - [Classic Spirit Weapon Awakening](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Classic_Spirit_Weapon_Awakening 'Classic Spirit Weapon Awakening')
    - [Crusader Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Crusader_Skills 'Crusader Skills')

## Other Skills

- **Unlearned:**this tab is for skills that the player has yet to learn. It also tells what category the skill belongs to and how to learn it.

    - When hovering over the skill, important key words, usually pertaining to quests or other skills, are labeled in orange. The coloration does not show when opening the skill's "Learn Method" window.
    - Certain skills, like [Arcana](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Arcana 'Arcana')-related Skills and [Memorial Link](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Memorial_Link 'Memorial Link') are omitted from the list and will never show up under this tab.

- **[Actions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Actions 'Actions'):** Technically skills, however, they require no AP and do not rank up. These have a green background and are accessible from the "Action" window.
- Player skills used by monsters, NPCs, pets, or partners are equal to that of a [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human 'Human'), unless the race is an [Elf](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elf 'Elf') or [Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant 'Giant').
- Player skills used by monsters, NPCs, pets, or partners bear different loading/cooldown times than players even if their race is that of a human, elf, or giant.
    - This is due to the [Genesis](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Patches#Generation_16.2C_Season_2:_Genesis 'Patches') update only applying to player cooldown times.
- **Technique**: Usually passive effects that a player can equip on and off, some of them include effects that are activated in the same manner of skills. See [here](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Technique 'Category:Technique') for more information.
