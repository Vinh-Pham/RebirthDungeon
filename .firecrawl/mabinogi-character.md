## Details

Players must create a Character in order to enter the world of Mabinogi.
Every account starts with six free Character Cards.
Mabinogi offers players the option to purchase [Ace Character Card](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ace_Character_Card "Ace Character Card") or [Hero Character Cards](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hero_Character_Card "Hero Character Card") in order to create more characters.

If no characters over a cumulative level of 1000 exist on the same account, all characters will receive [Beginner's Benefits](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner%27s_Benefits "Beginner's Benefits").

You can have a total of 300 characters and pets on an account. However, once 200 characters are present on the account, only pets and partners can be created up to the 300 limit.

## Character Cards

Character Cards can be used to create new Characters or used to [Rebirth](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rebirth "Rebirth") an existing one. For more information, see [Character Cards](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character_Cards "Character Cards").

There are 3 available [Races](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Race "Race") to choose from. Human character may also obtain a free Elf and Giant Assistant Cards by forming an alliance with [Atrata](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Atrata "Atrata") and [Krug](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Krug "Krug"), respectively. This is limited to one of each card per account, and you are not given free Assistant Cards upon attempting to create an alliance with [Elves](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Elves "Elves") or [Giants](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giants "Giants"). Accounts whom have not had their original character be a [Human](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Human "Human") can still claim these free cards by creating a Human and forming the alliances on the new Human.

## Appearance

- Players may change 4 features of their character's appearance: facial shape and skin color, hair style and color, eye type and color, or mouth type.
- There are **Basic**, [![ColorBoxP 32x32.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/1/19/ColorBoxP_32x32.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:ColorBoxP_32x32.png)**Premium** and [![ColorBoxB 32x32.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/c/cc/ColorBoxB_32x32.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:ColorBoxB_32x32.png)**Coupon** options.

  - Options marked with a [![ColorBoxC 32x32.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/7/71/ColorBoxC_32x32.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:ColorBoxC_32x32.png) are considered Premium; they used to be available only through the old [Beauty Shop](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beauty_Shop "Beauty Shop") system.
- Players may preview appearances before changing or purchasing.
- Special appearances obtainable from limited time crossovers are marked with a [![ColorBoxNum 32x32.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/a/a4/ColorBoxNum_32x32.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:ColorBoxNum_32x32.png) # icon.

### Basic

- Basic options are available to any character, and can be changed at will without a fee.

### Premium

- Premium options require [Pon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pon "Pon") to use. The exact cost depends on which feature is changed:

  - Changing the **Eye** or **Hair** colors will cost 3 Pon each.
  - Changing the **Skin Color** will cost 4 Pon.
  - Changing the **Mouth** will cost 5 Pon.

  - [![Face Beauty Coupon.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/5/56/Face_Beauty_Coupon.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Face_Beauty_Coupon.png) Face
  - [![Hair Beauty Coupon.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/7/71/Hair_Beauty_Coupon.png)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/File:Hair_Beauty_Coupon.png) Hair

- Coupons are **consumed** upon use.
- You will not get the coupon back after changing styles.
- You will need the coupon again if you wish to use the style once more.

- For Hair and Eyes, a [Storage Coupon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Storage_Coupons "Category:Storage Coupons") of the corresponding feature may be used to save the currently used option before changing to a new one.

  - They save both the current style and the color.

## Colors

_Mabinogi: Fantasy Life_ uses a pre-determined palette color system to let the player choose the colors of their character's features.

A number of colors can be selected respectively for **skin color, eye color, and hair color.**

- Depending on the specific feature chosen, there may be variants in the same color between two hair or eye types.
- Some options, like but not limited to eyelashes, henna, blush, tattoos and beards cannot have a different color applied to them.
  - The player's skin color may affect the resulting color of these features, however.

### Skin Colors

- Some items may alter the color of the character's skin. See [here](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Items_Which_Override_Skin_Color "Category:Items Which Override Skin Color") for a list.
- Some Mouth features may end up tinted differently depending on the player's skin color.
- ![ColorBoxP 32x32.png](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/images/1/19/ColorBoxP_32x32.png) Premium colors requires [Pon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pon "Pon") to use under normal circumstances.
- These colors may be also affected by the character's model and underlaying skin color.
  - For Instance, the color #CCCCCC, Fog, will rarely show as-is on the player's model. This is due to the ambient light effects and the character model's specific shading method.
  - The Film Style Post Shader option may also affect the final look of the color.

## Age

_For the stats gained when aging, see [Level#Character Growth](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Level#Character_Growth "Level")._

- Players can Rebirth into ages 10 to 17.
  - The player cannot rebirth as an older age.
- The stats gained from levelup and some titles are reliant on age.
- The player age, though, can range from 10 to 25.
  - Ages higher than 24 are displayed as "x years past 25".
  - Your _actual_ age (for Title bonuses and other things) is x + the age your current life began.

    - Characters that started at age 17 will be age 30 when the display reads "13 years past 25"
    - Characters that started at age 10 will be 30 at "20 years past 25", etc.

### Birthday

- The real-time day the character was created in becomes the birthday of the character.
- If the account has [Premium Service](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Premium_Service "Premium Service") and they log in on that day, they will be sent to [Nao](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Nao "Nao"), where she will give the player a random accessory from a predetermined list.

  - The player will also be able to talk with Nao with the [Keywords](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Keywords "Keywords") the character has collected, or gift her [clothes](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Nao#Gifts_to_Nao "Nao").

## Character Name

- All Characters and [Pets](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Pets "Pets") must have a _unique_ name that identifies them.

  - Names can contain between 3 to 12 letters or numbers.

### Character Name Capitalization Limitations

- Names can only contain capitalized letters at the start of the Name.
- Names can only otherwise contain capitalized letters following other capitalized letters.
- For example:
  - "Name", "NAME", and "NAMe" would be acceptable.
  - "namE", "NAmE", and "NaME" would **not** be acceptable.

### Character Name Numbers Limitations

- Names cannot start with a number.
- Names can only have numbers follow a number.
- For example:
  - "Name1", "Name12", and "Nam312" would be acceptable.
  - "1Name", "Nam3one", and "C00lname3" would **not** be acceptable.

### Character Name General Limitations

- Names cannot have three or more repeated letters or numbers in a row.
- The Names of some [NPCs](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/NPCs "NPCs") are prohibited.
- Names cannot contain special lettering.
- Names cannot contain profanity and attempting to avoid filters can result in punishment.

### Character Name Color

- A Character's Name has a predetermined RGB color based off the Unicode Decimal values of the capitalization, letters, and numbers used in their name.
  - Name Color can also be temporarily changed using a [Name Color Change Potion](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Name_Color_Change_Potion "Name Color Change Potion") or any other [Name Dye](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Name_Dye "Name Dye").
- The placement of the capitalization, letters, and numbers determines if it influences the Red, Green, or Blue values of the Name Color.
  - `(((Character1+Character4+Character7+Character10) * 101) modulo 97) + 159` corresponds to the Red value.
  - `(((Character2+Character5+Character8+Character11) * 101) modulo 97) + 159` corresponds to the Green value.
  - `(((Character3+Character6+Character9+Character12) * 101) modulo 97) + 159` corresponds to the Blue value.
  - For more information, see [Character/Name Color](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character/Name_Color "Character/Name Color").

### Character Name Change

- A Character's Name can be changed with a [Character Name Change Coupon](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character_Name_Change_Coupon "Character Name Change Coupon").

  - The Limitations on choosing a Name with a Character Name Change Coupon are _**not**_ the same as normal.
  - For more information, see [here](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character_Name_Change_Coupon "Character Name Change Coupon").

## Trivia

- According to Naak Kim (the original producer/director of Mabinogi), the video game _Sorcerian_ heavily-influenced Mabinogi's Age mechanic.[\[2\]](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character#cite_note-2)
- Giants used to have a completely different model before they were overhauled, see [Giant](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Giant#Trivia "Giant") for more information.
- Beards are a unique feature of a male Giants's character creation.
- [Coco](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Coco "Coco") used to offer Premium options through the [Beauty Shop](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beauty_Shop "Beauty Shop"). [The Merlin Update](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Patches#Merlin "Patches") removed the beauty shop and replaced it with the Dressing Room.
- Some mouth types, marked with a 'C', used to be **C** oupon-only before being added to the Premium options.
- Prior to 2014, The Butler and Maid hairstyles used to be available to player character, but were then removed. Players who had the hairstyles after the update could not Rebirth and keep the hairstyle.[\[3\]](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Character#cite_note-3)
- There used to be multiple Servers for character creation in the NA region of Mabinogi.
  - The Nao Server for the Closed Beta.
  - The Ruairi, Mari, and Tarlach Servers.
    - The Alexina Server was added later.
  - Ruairi, Mari, and Tarlach got merged into a new Nao Server during the player-requested [NA Server Merge (2019)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/NA_Server_Merge_(2019) "NA Server Merge (2019)").
  - Nao and Alexina were merged into the Erinn Server during the player-requested [NA Server Merge (2022)](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/NA_Server_Merge_(2022) "NA Server Merge (2022)").
- Some colors used to be race-limited.
  - Notably, Elves could not have dark skin or black hair. The Eweca Blue and Iria Green hair colors were unique to elves.
  - Giants did not have access to the Ice Blue hair color. The eye color Prepeace-wine was unique to them.
- The colors Pale Emerald, Eweca Blue, Iria Green, Ice Blue, Lemon Yellow, Misty Rose, Aquamarine, Frilly Pink, Chroma Orange, and Canary Yellow all also appear as [Glyph Design](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Glyph_Design "Glyph Design") choices.

### Color Names

- The Ogre Crown skin color and the Emerald Green eye color reference [Paris Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_green), an infamous dye once made from deadly arsenic.
- Cobalt Green references the real-world [Cobalt Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cobalt_green) and the extended family of Cobalt-produced pigments.

  - Cobalt comes the German goblin, the _[Kobold](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Kobold_(Family) "Kobold (Family)")_, and was once called kobold ore.
- Vergiss Blue uses the German word _vergiss_, which is a form of the verb _to forget_. The color is likely a reference to the forget-me-not ( _Vergissmeinnicht_) flower.
- Carmine Red references the real-world [Carmine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmine) produced by killing the Cochineal bug.
- Seraphim White uses the plural of a type of biblical angel, a seraph; the pure white color is likely referencing angelic holiness as well as the fact that " _שרף_" can mean _to burn_.
- Cornflower Blue references the real-world [Cornflower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centaurea_cyanus).
- Narkiss Aqua Blue is likely referencing the narcissus flower.
- Ultramarine references the real-world [Ultramarine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultramarine) made from ground-up Lapis Lazuli.
- Chroma Yellow and Chroma Orange reference real-world [Chrome Yellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrome_yellow) and [Chrome Orange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrome_orange) pigments.

  - Chroma is the Greek word _χρῶμα_ which can mean _color_.
- Misty Rose is a [Web Color](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_colors) originating with the X11 Computer System, Netscape Navigator Web Browser, and Mosaic Web Browser.

  - Ironically, despite being named after a standardized Web Color, Misty Rose does not share the same exact color values.
- Xanthe is the Greek word _Ξανθή_ which means _blond-haired_.
- Ochre is the Greek word _ωχρός_, meaning _pale_, and it is a family of earth pigments largely comprised of iron.
- Imperial-yellow references the [Qing Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qing_dynasty) which restricted and associated the color with the Emperor.
- Lemon Yellow references a pigment also known as Barium Yellow.
- Desertum is the Latin word _dēsertum_ meaning _desert_.
- Caelum Blue, also known as [Cerulean Blue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerulean), uses the Latin word _caelum_ meaning _sky_ or _heaven_.
- Smalt Blue references a powdered glass pigment related to [Cobalt Glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cobalt_glass).
- Mythril and Eternium are references to fictional metals.
- Shyam-Black, also found as the [Shyam-black Magic Ink](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shyam-black_Magic_Ink "Shyam-black Magic Ink"), is the Hindi/Sanskrit word श्याम (transliterated as śyām or shyam) for 'dark' or 'black'.

  - The name 시암블랙 was also used for a dye from both the [Merlin Fixed Dye Ampoule Set](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Merlin_Fixed_Dye_Ampoule_Set "Merlin Fixed Dye Ampoule Set")#020715 and the [Treasure Hunter Fixed Dye Ampoule Set](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Treasure_Hunter_Fixed_Dye_Ampoule_Set "Treasure Hunter Fixed Dye Ampoule Set") Set #1E1E20, with the former being popular on the Korean Server.
- [Eweca](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Eweca "Eweca") Blue and [Ladeca](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Ladeca "Ladeca") Red reference the two moons found in-game in Mabinogi. However, Eweca appears as a _red_ moon with a blue glow and Ladeca appears as a _blue_ moon.
