## Description

- Alby Beginner is a Dungeon meant to be particularly easy for [Beginning Players](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Beginner%27s_Benefits "Beginner's Benefits").

  - If anyone in the [Party](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Party "Party") is over the Cumulative [Level](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Level "Level") of 200? when entering, the Party will instead be taken to [Alby Normal](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Alby_Normal "Alby Normal") Dungeon.
