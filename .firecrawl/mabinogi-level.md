## Experience

- There are a few types of experience:
  - Normal/Combat, the general experience of the game.
    - Gained through various means, for example: [quests](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Quest "Quest"), killing monsters, ranking [skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Skills "Category:Skills"), [producing items](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Production_Skills "Category:Production Skills").
    - Experience gain can be amplified, or reduced, by various means, for example:
      - The [Grandmaster](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Grandmaster "Grandmaster") effect of the Cleric talent
      - Certain equipment
      - [Experience Crystals](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Experience_Crystals "Category:Experience Crystals"), when the player clears [Missions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Missions "Missions")
      - [Combat experience potions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Combat_Potions "Category:Combat Potions")
      - In a field area, if a player kills too many monsters in the same spot, a [Camping Penalty](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Camping_Penalty "Camping Penalty") is incurred, reducing the experience gained.
    - Doing [Shadow Missions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Shadow_Mission "Shadow Mission") and [Theatre Missions](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Theatre_Mission "Theatre Mission").

      - `Total Mission EXP = [(Mission EXP * Daily Mission Bonus) * (1 + Crystal Bonus + Event Bonus + Talent Level Bonus)]`
        - If you are wearing the [Cessair Officer Robe](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Cessair_Officer_Robe "Cessair Officer Robe"), then:

          - `Final Mission EXP = Total Mission EXP + [Mission EXP * (0.1)]`
        - If you are wearing the [Mysterious Robe](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Mysterious_Robe_(Type_2) "Mysterious Robe (Type 2)"), then:

          - `Final Mission EXP = Total Mission EXP * (1 + 0.1)`
  - Exploration, experience gained through activities on the continent of [Iria](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Iria "Iria").

    - Gained through certain quests, certain killed monsters, [finding relics and artifacts](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/L-Rod_Exploration "L-Rod Exploration"), [sketching](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Sketch "Sketch") certain monsters, [Dungeons](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Dungeons "Dungeons"), [trading](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Voight "Voight") in [artifacts](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Courcle_Artifacts "Courcle Artifacts"), [Rafting](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rafting "Rafting"), and [Hot-Air Ballooning](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Hot-Air_Ballooning "Hot-Air Ballooning").

## Experience Loss

- As a trade off for reviving upon being [Knocked Unconscious](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Knocked_Unconscious "Knocked Unconscious"), the player will lose character experience. The amount lost is based on the revival method.
- Some revival options will not be available if the revival option would result the player having -100% experience.
  - Reviving at a warp point/town, [Nao](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Nao "Nao") (given current environment is suitable), or by another player will always be available.
- Experience can also be lost if the player logs out within 30 seconds after engaging combat or five minutes after entering a dungeon.

## Character Growth

- _For an explanation of the stats, see [Stats](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats "Stats")_
- _For skills, see [Category:Skills](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Category:Skills "Category:Skills")_
- Stats and AP are gained when a character levels up.
  - You earn one [AP](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Stats#Ability_Point "Stats") upon level up.
  - The stats that increase are determined by the character's active [Talent](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Talent "Talent").

    - Stats that are gained from levelling up are lost when the player [Rebirths](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Rebirth "Rebirth"), unlike the stats gained from ranking up Skills, which are permanent.
- When the character becomes one year older after EDT: 3:00pm

UTC: 7:00pm

AEST: 5:00am

12:00pm PDT100 on [Saturday](https://wiki.mabinogiworld.com/view/Time#Saturday "Time"), they will gain 5 AP.

  - There is no known age limit for a character.
    - Beyond age 25, the game display 'X Age past 25', with X being how many Saturdays have passed since the last rebirth.
