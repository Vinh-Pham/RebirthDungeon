NOVA IGRA

Build epic combos in Dicero

Roll out on a new adventure with an exclusive starter pack.

**From the team** at Habby that brought you _Capybara Go_ and the Archero series comes Dicero. In this brand-new adventure, your attacks are determined by a roll of the dice.

Start each round by rolling the dice. Poker hands such as a pair, three of a kind or a straight add multiplier bonuses to your attacks. Level up to access skills like Swordwave or Poison. Dice can hold just one skill each, but they can be upgraded. If the skills of all five dice are Swordwave-related, you have the potential to release dozens of Swordwaves with every attack, making for exhilarating combat.

Hit the ground running in _Dicero_ by exploring different fighting styles and getting the exclusive starter pack.

* * *

**Swordwave**

How to unlock: Available when the game begins.

Effect: This satisfying attack quickly drains health from multiple enemies.

**Greatsword**

How to unlock: Equip a sword.

Effect: When dispatching an individual enemy is a priority, swing your greatsword for a one-shot kill.

* * *

**Poison**

How to unlock: Clear Chapter 6.

Effect: Stack poison effects to deliver devastating damage against bosses or high-HP enemies.

It takes patience to poison your enemies with stacked attacks.

* * *

**Hammer**

How to unlock: Equip a hammer.

Effect: Swinging the hammer has an area effect, making it an essential tool when fighting groups of foes.

* * *

**Staff**

How to unlock: Equip a staff.

Effect: Unleash elemental attack combos or target specific foes. Gathering all the necessary skills isn’t easy, the resulting damage is a sight to behold.
